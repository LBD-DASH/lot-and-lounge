## Official DTW parking rates

From the Wayne County Airport Authority's [Long Term Parking](https://www.metroairport.com/parking/long-term-parking), [Valet Parking](https://www.metroairport.com/parking/valet-parking), [Parking](https://www.metroairport.com/parking) pages, last checked 24 Nov 2026.

| Option | 24-hour max | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Green Lot | $20 flat | Credit card only. Free shuttle 24/7 to both McNamara and Evans Terminal Ground Transportation Centers. Vehicles taller than 7 ft can park here. |
| Big Blue Deck | $23 | Closest to Evans Terminal. Connected by temperature-controlled skywalk. |
| McNamara Parking Garage | $32 | Closest to McNamara Terminal. Skywalk. |
| McNamara Valet | $51 | McNamara Terminal only. Cars stored Level 6 of garage. Card only since 22 June 2026. |

Additional days after the first 24 hours are charged at the same flat 24-hour rate.

An additional 2.65% processing fee per transaction applies to credit or debit card payments. The airport does not say whether rates include tax.

Most garages cannot take vehicles over 7 ft 0 in. Van-accessible spaces up to 8 ft 2 in are on McNamara Deck Level 2 near the B elevators and Big Blue Deck Level 1 zones A and B.

Parking is first come, first served, per the [DTW FAQ](https://www.metroairport.com/about-dtw/frequently-asked-questions). EV charging at McNamara and Big Blue Deck is at no extra cost.

The free Terminal-Westin-Terminal shuttle runs about every 10 minutes between the two terminals.

| Option | Hourly detail |
|---|---|
| Big Blue Deck | 1/2 hr or less $4; to 1 hr $6; to 1.5 hr $7; to 2 hr $8; 2-3 hr $12; 3-4 hr $16; 4-5 hr $20; 5-24 hr $23. |
| McNamara Parking Garage | 1/2 hr $4; to 1 hr $6; to 1.5 hr $7; to 2 hr $8; 2-3 hr $12; 3-4 hr $16; 4-5 hr $20; 5-6 hr $24; 6-7 hr $28; 7-24 hr $32. |

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

These 7-day totals are our arithmetic.

| Option | Formula | 7-day total before card fee |
|---|---|---|
| Green Lot | 7 × $20 | $140 |
| Big Blue Deck | 7 × $23 | $161 |
| McNamara Garage | 7 × $32 | $224 |
| McNamara Valet | 7 × $51 | $357 |

An additional 2.65% processing fee per transaction applies to credit or debit card payments. The airport does not say whether rates include tax.

**The takeaway:** Green Lot saves $84 over the McNamara Garage for a week.

## Public transport

Source: [DTW Public Transportation](https://www.metroairport.com/dtw/other-ground-transportation/public-transportation).

Stops are at Evans Terminal Ground Transportation Center and McNamara Terminal Departures Level near Door 1.

SMART FAST Michigan Route 261 serves both terminals to Downtown Detroit. Schedules are at smartbus.org. SMART Route 125 serves both terminals. SMART Route 280 serves Evans.

Detroit Air Xpress (DAX) goes to Downtown Detroit from 3:30 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily. It costs $8 at the door or $6 in advance.

Michigan Flyer goes to East Lansing, Whitmore Lake and Ann Arbor. AirRide goes to downtown Ann Arbor.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (Delta Sky Club, Lufthansa):** for ticketed passengers under each airline's own rules. DTW says the DTW Destination Pass does not give club access.
- **Priority Pass:** on 24 Nov 2026 it listed the Lufthansa Business Lounge (Evans Terminal), plus non-lounge perks: Anitas Kitchen restaurant (Evans Terminal), Gameway (McNamara Terminal, two locations) and Be Relax Spa (McNamara Terminal).
- **Bank-branded lounges:** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
