## Official JFK parking rates by lot

From the Port Authority's [JFK parking page](https://www.jfkairport.com/to-from-airport/parking), last checked 19 Nov 2026. "Pre-book" means booking at least 3 hours ahead on the official reservations site.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Pre-book rate | Notes (per JFK) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-Term Lot | $45 per 24 hours | from $35/day | AirTrain to terminals (near Lefferts Blvd station). Height limit 11'6". |
| Federal Circle Station Lot | Reservation only | from $45/day | Pre-book only. Uncovered path to AirTrain. No ADA discount. |
| Terminal 8 Red Lot | $6 first ½ hour; $70 per 24 hours max | from $56/day | JFK's recommended lot for Terminal 1. |
| Terminal 5 Yellow Lot | $6 first ½ hour; $75 per 24 hours max | from $59/day | Free Level 2 EV charging (you still pay to park). |
| Terminal 4 Blue Lot | $6 first ½ hour; $82 per 24 hours max | from $66/day | Reduced availability during construction. Height limit 6'4". |

JFK also lists **Premium Parking** (reserved spaces closest to the terminal, online booking only) and a 24-hour maximum of **$35** for travellers with valid ADA plates or placards (not with pre-booked or E-ZPass payment). If you'll park more than **30 days**, you must register with ABM Parking or your car may be towed.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the rates above: 7 × the 24-hour maximum. Pre-book rates are "as low as" prices and vary by date.

| Option | 7 days drive-up | 7 days pre-booked (lowest) |
|---|---|---|
| Long-Term Lot | $315 | **$245** |
| Federal Circle | n/a | $315 |
| Terminal 8 Red | $490 | $392 |
| Terminal 5 Yellow | $525 | $413 |
| Terminal 4 Blue | $574 | $462 |
| **Transit instead:** AirTrain both ways | $17.50 per person + subway or LIRR fare | n/a |

The takeaway: pre-booking the Long-Term Lot saves about $329 over a drive-up week in the Terminal 4 garage (pre-booked Long-Term $245 vs drive-up Terminal 4 Blue $574). Solo travellers living near the subway or LIRR can save even more by leaving the car at home.

## Off-airport parking

JFK says to park only on-airport or with an **authorized off-airport provider**. It lists two:

- **SmartPark JFK**: +1 (877) 535-7275 ([smartparkparking.com](https://smartparkparking.com/))
- **AirPark JFK / JFK Long-Term Parking Inc.**: +1 (718) 898-8400 ([jfklongtermparking.com](https://jfklongtermparking.com/home-3/))

Compare them with the official Long-Term Lot pre-book rate, and check shuttle frequency and whether taxes are included.

## Public transport: AirTrain

- The **AirTrain** links every terminal, the parking lots, Federal Circle (rental cars and hotel shuttles), and **Jamaica** and **Howard Beach** stations for the NYC Subway. Jamaica also connects to the Long Island Rail Road (LIRR).
- Fare: **$8.75** one way to or from Jamaica or Howard Beach. Children under five ride free. Travel between terminals is free.
- With OMNY contactless payment there's a **group fare** ($8.75 for the first rider plus $4.50 for each extra rider, up to 5 riders and $26.75 total) and a **rolling 30-day cap of $43.50**.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges** (Delta, American, British Airways/Terminal 8 lounges, Emirates, Virgin Atlantic, Lufthansa, Turkish, Air France, KAL, Air India, Aer Lingus): premium-cabin ticket, elite status or membership, under the airline's rules.
- **BlueHouse:** JetBlue sets eligibility (certain TrueBlue Mosaic members, Mint transatlantic customers and some other groups). JFK says it isn't available on Blue Basic fares.
- **Priority Pass:** listed lounges in Terminal 1 and Terminal 4 (see FAQ). Priority Pass shows limited hours for some, such as the Terminal 4 Virgin Atlantic Clubhouse.
- **Bank-branded lounges** (Centurion, Capital One, Chase Sapphire): access is set by each issuer for its own cardholders. We don't recommend cards.
