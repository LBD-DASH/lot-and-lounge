## Where to park at Taoyuan Airport

The airport runs car parks at each terminal. At **Terminal 1**, **P1** is at the east end (near Provincial Highway 4) and **P2** at the west end (near National Highway 2). **Terminal 2** has its own car park, with live space counts on the same parking page. You can pay with cash, EasyCard, iPass or a credit card. Options vary by car park.

## Official Taoyuan Airport parking fees

From the airport's **parking** page (Terminal 1 car parks), checked 20 Nov 2026. The Terminal 2 car park has its own tab on that page, so check it before you rely on these rates there.

| | Small vehicles | Large vehicles |
|---|---|---|
| First hour | NT$30 | NT$60 |
| Each extra half hour | NT$20 | NT$40 |
| Daily maximum | **NT$490** | NT$980 |

- **Free 30 minutes:** since 5 May 2023 this applies only **3 times a day**. Stay longer, or use it a 4th time, and you're charged from the time you entered.
- Large vehicles in the No. 2 Parking Lot (Arrival) large-vehicle area are free if they exit within 2 hours.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic. Airport MRT fares are per person.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Airport MRT, Taipei Main Station ↔ airport, return (2 × NT$160) | **NT$320** per person |
| Terminal 1 car park at the daily maximum (7 × NT$490) | **NT$3,430** |

The takeaway: from central Taipei the Airport MRT is much cheaper than parking, even for a family of four (NT$1,280 return).

## Public transport: Taoyuan Airport MRT

The **Taoyuan Airport MRT** stops at **A12 Airport Terminal 1** and **A13 Airport Terminal 2**. Express and commuter trains alternate, about every 7.5 minutes. Fares listed on the airport's page:

- **Taipei Main Station ↔ airport: NT$160**
- New Taipei Industrial Park ↔ airport: NT$115
- Chang Gung Memorial Hospital ↔ airport: NT$60
- Taoyuan HSR Station ↔ airport: NT$35

Express trains take about **35 minutes** from Taipei Main Station to Terminal 1 (39 minutes to Terminal 2), and commuter trains about 49 minutes. At Taipei Main Station you can change to the MRT, high-speed rail (THSR) and Taiwan Railway.

## Lounges at Taoyuan Airport by terminal

From the airport's official lounge directory, Plaza Premium's TPE page and EVA Air's airport page, checked 20 Nov 2026.

### Terminal 1
- **Plaza Premium Lounge The Jade**, Zone C, international departures, 05:00–19:00
- **Plaza Premium Lounge The Blossom**, Zone D, international departures, 24 hours
- **Cathay Pacific Lounge**, 4F departure area, 05:20–22:00

### Terminal 2
- **EVA Air lounges**, 4F, after security and immigration:
  - **The Infinity**, 04:30–23:30
  - **The Star**, 04:30–23:30
  - **The Garden**, 05:15–23:30
  - **The Club**, 05:30–10:00 and 16:00–21:30
- **Plaza Premium Lounge The Terroir**, Zone A, international departures, 05:00–00:00
- **Plaza Premium Lounge The Crest**, Zone A1, international departures, 07:00–19:00
- **Oriental Club Lounge**, 4F departure area (the airport lists its hours as 24 hours, run in separate areas)

### Other airline lounges
- The airport's directory also lists **China Airlines** lounges (3 locations, 05:00–23:30) and **STARLUX Airlines** lounges (2 locations, 05:00 to last departure). It doesn't give their terminals on the summary page, so check with the airline for which one serves your flight.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (EVA Air, China Airlines, STARLUX, Cathay Pacific):** eligible tickets or status, under each airline's rules.
- **Plaza Premium lounges:** paid entry where Plaza Premium sells it. Its TPE page shows The Terroir from TWD 1,500. Check its site for current prices and the memberships it accepts.
