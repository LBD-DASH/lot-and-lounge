## Official DXB parking rates
From [Dubai Airports Car Parking](https://dubaiairports.ae/transport/car-parking), last checked 25 Nov 2026. Rates in AED.

### Terminal 1
| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Car Park A - Premium | 15 min: 15, 30 min: 30, up to 2 hours: 40, 3 hours: 55, 4 hours: 65 | 125 for 1 day, then 100 per additional day | 2-3 minute walk |
| Car Park B - Economy | 1 hour: 25, 2 hours: 30, 3 hours: 35, 4 hours: 45 | 85 for 1 day, then 75 per additional day | 7-9 minute walk |

### Terminal 2
| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Car Park A - Premium | 1 hour: 30, 2 hours: 40, 3 hours: 55, 4 hours: 65 | 125 for 1 day, then 100 per additional day | 3-5 minute walk |
| Car Park B - Economy | 1 hour: 15, 2 hours: 20, 3 hours: 25, 4 hours: 30 | 70 for 1 day, then 50 per additional day | 10-12 minute walk |

### Terminal 3
| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| All levels | 15 min: 15, 30 min: 30, up to 2 hours: 40, 3 hours: 55, 4 hours: 65 | 125 for 1 day, then 100 per additional day | 2-3 minute walk |

To pay for parking, guests can scan the QR code on their parking ticket or use an automatic pay station before returning to their vehicle. Salik is also accepted at DXB if a sufficient balance is available and the parking fee remains unpaid; the amount will be automatically deducted from the Salik account upon exit.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from official rates (1 day plus 6 additional days).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Terminal 2 Car Park B - Economy | 70 + 6 × 50 = 370 AED |
| Terminal 1 Car Park B - Economy | 85 + 6 × 75 = 535 AED |
| Terminal 1 or 2 Premium, or Terminal 3 | 125 + 6 × 100 = 725 AED |

The takeaway: if you fly from Terminal 2, its economy car park costs 355 AED less for a week than premium parking.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives
- Dubai Airports offers car park booking on its official website. Compare that total with the drive-up rates above.

## Public transport
- **Dubai Metro Red Line:** Stations at DXB Terminals 1 and 3. Hours: Monday to Thursday 5 a.m. to midnight, Friday 5 a.m. to 1 a.m. (next day), Saturday 5 a.m. to midnight, Sunday 8 a.m. to midnight; hours may extend on public holidays. Tickets at ticket offices and vending machines in Terminals 1 and 3, or use a Nol Card. Up to two pieces of luggage, including hand luggage. Source: [Dubai Airports Metro](https://dubaiairports.ae/transport/metro).
