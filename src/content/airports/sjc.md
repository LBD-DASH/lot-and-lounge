## Official SJC parking rates
From [FlySanJose Parking](https://www.flysanjose.com/parking), last checked 25 Nov 2026. SJC doesn't say whether rates include tax.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per day | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economy Lot 1 | $19 per day | $19 | Free shuttle to Terminal A, then Terminal B, every 7-10 minutes, 24 hours |
| Terminal A Garage (Hourly Lot 2) | $4 per 30 minutes (first day only) | $25 | About 15 minutes' walk to Terminal B |
| Daily Lot 4 | $4 per 30 minutes (first day only) | $31 | About 10 minutes' walk to Terminal B, 25 to Terminal A |
| Hourly & Daily Lot 5 | $4 per 30 minutes (first day only) | $31 | About 10 minutes' walk to Terminal B, 20 to Terminal A |
| Terminal B Garage (Hourly Lot 3) | $5 per 30 minutes (first day only) | $41 | About 15 minutes' walk to Terminal A |

Economy Lot 1's full daily rate accrues at the beginning of each 24-hour period. SJC's parking page offers online reservations for every lot above except the Terminal B Garage. Its Park Perks loyalty program gives 1 point per dollar on parking reserved online, and 100 points take $10 off a reservation. Accessible parking is in all SJC lots, first-come, first-served. The cell phone waiting area at 2470 Airport Blvd. is free.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from official rates (7 x daily rate).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy Lot 1 | $133 (7 x $19) |
| Terminal A Garage | $175 (7 x $25) |
| Daily Lot 4 or Lot 5 | $217 (7 x $31) |
| Terminal B Garage | $287 (7 x $41) |

The takeaway: Choosing Economy Lot 1 instead of the Terminal B Garage saves you $154 over a 7-day trip.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives
- SJC's pages we checked don't name off-airport lots, so if you use a private lot, compare final totals including fees against Economy Lot 1's $19 per day.

## Public transport
- **Free airport shuttles** link Terminal A, Terminal B, Economy Lot 1 and the Rental Car Center every 7 to 10 minutes, 24 hours a day. All are wheelchair accessible.
- **VTA buses and light rail:** SJC suggests the Transit App to track them alongside the airport shuttles. Scheduled and charter buses use Terminal A Ground Transportation Center Stop #4, and Terminal B Stops #12 (scheduled) and #11 (charter).

## Lounges at SJC by terminal
From [The Club SJC](https://www.flysanjose.com/Services-Amenities/airport-lounge-club-sjc) and [Priority Pass San José Lounges](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/san-jose-international), checked 25 Nov 2026.

### Terminal A
- **The Club SJC**, near Gate 15, between Terminals A and B (Priority Pass lists it as The Club SJC A15, Terminal A). Daily hours 5 a.m. to 10 p.m.; on Thanksgiving Day (26 Nov), Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Day, 5 a.m. to 8 p.m. (until the last departing flight). Day pass $60, with a special rate for AAA members.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** SJC says airline-operated lounge memberships are not accepted at The Club SJC, and the pages we checked list no airline lounges.
- **Priority Pass:** on 25 Nov 2026 it listed The Club SJC in Terminal A. LoungeKey members also get in.
- **Bank-branded lounges:** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
