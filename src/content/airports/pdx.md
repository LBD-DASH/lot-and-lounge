## Official PDX parking rates
From [FlyPDX Parking](https://www.flypdx.com/Parking), last checked 25 Nov 2026. PDX doesn't say whether rates include tax.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per day | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short-Term garage | $4/hr | $30 | Skybridges on level 4 and tunnels to the terminal |
| Long-Term garage | $4/hr | $24 | Walk through the North and South tunnels |
| Economy | $4/hr | $15, 7th day free | Free shuttle every 7-9 minutes from 4:00 a.m. to midnight, every 15 minutes from midnight to 4:00 a.m. |
| Valet | $10/hr | $45 | Drop-off next to the ticket counter |

Reservations for parking are not available; all lots are first-come, first-served. PDX accepts cash, debit cards, American Express, Discover, MasterCard, Visa and Apple Pay. Garage clearance is 8 feet in the Short-Term and Long-Term garages. EV chargers: 24 in the Short-Term garage (levels 3 and 4), 10 in the Long-Term garage (level 2), and 36 in Economy near the Red E and Red L shelters. The cell phone waiting area off N.E. Airport Way and N.E. 82nd Avenue is free for up to 30 minutes.

Disabled parking is $15 per day in the Short-Term garage, Long-Term garage or Economy with a valid state-issued disabled permit, placard or plate, paid at a staffed exit booth. US Armed Forces veterans with a service-related disability can park free in Economy for up to 14 consecutive days with the documents PDX lists.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from official rates (7 x daily maximum; Economy is 6 x $15 because the 7th day is free).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy | $90 (6 x $15) |
| Long-Term garage | $168 (7 x $24) |
| Short-Term garage | $210 (7 x $30) |
| Valet | $315 (7 x $45) |

The takeaway: Choosing Economy instead of the Short-Term garage saves you $120 over a 7-day trip.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives
- TriMet says parking at its Park & Ride lots is limited to 24 hours, so for longer trips have someone drop you off at a MAX station. PDX's pages we checked don't name off-airport lots, so if you use a private lot, compare final totals including fees.

## Public transport
- **TriMet MAX Red Line:** 15-minute service most of the day, every day. The trip to or from Downtown Portland takes about 39 minutes and costs $2.80 for adults 18-64, and $1.40 for Honored Citizens and Youth. The MAX station and Hop card machines are near baggage claim on the lower level.
