## Official IAD parking rates

From the airport's [Parking Information](https://www.flydulles.com/parking-transportation/parking-information), [Economy Parking Lot](https://www.flydulles.com/parking-transportation/parking-information/economy-parking-lot), [Parking Garages 1 & 2](https://www.flydulles.com/parking-transportation/parking-information/parking-garage-1-2), [Terminal Parking](https://www.flydulles.com/parking-transportation/parking-information/terminal-parking) and [Valet Parking](https://www.flydulles.com/parking-transportation/parking-information/valet-parking-open-now) pages, last checked 25 Nov 2026. The airport is run by the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority. The pages don't say whether rates include tax.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economy Parking Lot | Not listed | $20 | Free ECONOMY shuttle, 24/7, from Curb 2F |
| Parking Garages 1 & 2 | $7 per hour | $25 | Walkways, or free GARAGES shuttle 24/7 from Curb 2F |
| Terminal Parking | $7 per hour | $40 | Directly across from the terminal |
| Valet, Valet Service Center drop-off | Day 1 $39, then $10 per hour | $39 | Service Center in the Terminal Lot |
| Valet, curbside drop-off | Day 1 $45, then $10 per hour | $45 | Departures Level, Zone 3 |

- **Garage 1** connects to the terminal by an underground walkway with moving sidewalks. **Garage 2** connects by a covered walkway. The airport says Garage 2 is the most convenient for most international destinations.
- **Terminal Parking** is the closest self-parking option, with two access points to the underground pedestrian walkway.
- **Valet:** curbside drop-off is open 4 a.m. to 10 p.m. The Valet Service Center in the Terminal Lot is open 24 hours, and all pick-ups are there.
- **Reservations:** the airport says reserving in advance gets the lowest rates ([IAD Parking Reservations](https://reserve.flydulles.com/)). No reserved price is quoted on the pages.
- There is no minimum or maximum stay, but vehicles left more than 45 days must be registered with the parking office.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

This is our arithmetic from the drive-up daily maximums.

| Parking | Formula | 7-day total |
|---|---|---|
| Economy Lot | 7 × $20 | $140 |
| Garages 1 & 2 | 7 × $25 | $175 |
| Valet (Service Center drop-off) | 7 × $39 | $273 |
| Terminal Parking | 7 × $40 | $280 |
| Valet (curbside drop-off) | 7 × $45 | $315 |

**The takeaway:** Economy saves $140 over Terminal Parking and $35 over the garages for a week.

## Public transport

Sources: [IAD: Silver Line Metrorail](https://www.flydulles.com/metro), [IAD: Silver Line FAQs](https://www.flydulles.com/parking-transportation/silver-line-metrorail/silver-line-faqs), [WMATA fares](https://www.wmata.com/pay.html), [IAD: Other Public Transportation](https://www.flydulles.com/parking-transportation/other-transportation-options).

- **Metrorail Silver Line:** the station connects to the main terminal by an indoor pedestrian tunnel with moving sidewalks. The tunnel also links to Parking Garage 1.
- **Fares:** $2.25 to $6.75 by distance on weekdays. The airport says a trip to Metro Center downtown is $6.75. Fares drop to $2.25 to $2.50 after 9:30 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends.
- **Paying:** tap a contactless credit or debit card at the faregates. 1, 3 and 7-day passes need a SmarTrip card.
- **To Reagan National:** the airport says it takes about 50 to 55 minutes to Rosslyn, then change to the Blue Line.
- **Buses** leave from Garage 1, Stop 2: Loudoun County Transit commuter bus, Fairfax Connector Routes 952 and 983, Megabus and OurBus.

## Lounges at IAD by terminal

Sources: [IAD: Airport Lounges](https://www.flydulles.com/travel-information/services-amenities/airport-lounges), [American Express lounge finder](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/IAD), [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/washington-dulles-international). Checked 25 Nov 2026. The airport says lounges are run by the airlines. Check each one for membership rules, hours and partner access.

### Main Terminal, Ticketing Level
- **Capital One Lounge**, just beyond TSA PreCheck between the East and West security checkpoints.

### Concourse A
- **Air France lounge**, next to Gate A19. Shared with Emirates, KLM, Korean Air, Icelandair and certain SkyTeam partners.
- **Etihad Airways lounge**, next to Gate A14.
- **Virgin Atlantic Clubhouse**, next to Gate A32.

### Concourse B
- **British Airways lounge**, across from the B-Gates AeroTrain station. Shared with Aer Lingus.
- **Turkish Airlines Lounge**, next to Gate B43. Shared with many Star Alliance airlines, including United and Lufthansa. Walk-ins accepted.
- **Lufthansa lounge**, next to Gates B49/B51, temporarily closed for renovations.

### Concourses C, D and E
- **United Club**, next to Gates C7, C17 and D8 and at the E AeroTrain station. Shared with Star Alliance Gold members.
- **United Polaris lounge**, near Gate C18.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** membership, eligible tickets or status, under each airline's rules.
- **Priority Pass:** on 25 Nov 2026 it listed the Air France - KLM Lounge and Virgin Atlantic Clubhouse (Concourse A) and the Turkish Airlines Lounge Washington (Concourse B). It also listed Chef Geoff's restaurant (Concourse C) and Gameway (Concourse E).
- **Bank-branded lounges:** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
