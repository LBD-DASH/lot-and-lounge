## Official CPT parking rates
From the [ACSA parking tariffs 2025-2026](https://www.airports.co.za/Documents/ParkingTariffs.pdf) (CTIA section) and [ACSA parking information](https://www.airports.co.za/airports/cape-town-international-airport/At-the-airport/parking-information), last checked 25 Nov 2026. Pay at the payment units on the ground floor of the parkades or at the landings of the shaded parking areas.

| Lot | Drive-in rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parkade 1 & 2 | R28 for 0-1 hour, R56 for 1-2 hours, R170 for 4-12 hours | R230, then R150 per 12 hours | Parkades on either side of the Transport Plaza |
| Parkade 3 & 4 (shade parking) | R28 for 0-1 hour, R38 for 1-2 hours, R120 for 4-12 hours | R160, then R110 per 12 hours | Either side of the car rental facility, via pedestrian subways |
| P-5 Long Stay (5 days or more) | 7-day band: 7 × R72 = R504 | Then R72 per 24 hours | 189 bays, first come, first served |
| Pick-up (Parkade 2) | Free for 20 minutes, R30 for 21-40 minutes, R60 for 41-60 minutes | Then R60 per hour | Pick-up and e-hailing are in Parkade 2 |

The P-5 bands are as printed on the ACSA sheet, which also shows a 0-5 day band of R1 040, so confirm the long stay total at the pay station. Special needs passengers can use long stay parking on the ground floor of Parkade P1 by contacting Katanga Parking Management on 021 936 3612/3 or ACSA's Landside Hotline on 021 935 3737. Valet companies operate from the ground floor of Parkade 2.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from the official tariff sheet (first 24 hours, then 12 further 12-hour periods).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| P-5 Long Stay | R504 (7-day band) |
| Parkade 3 & 4 | R160 + 12 × R110 = R1 480 |
| Parkade 1 & 2 | R230 + 12 × R150 = R2 030 |

The takeaway: if a P-5 long stay bay is free, a week costs R976 less than Parkade 3 & 4. Otherwise Parkade 3 & 4 is R550 cheaper than Parkade 1 & 2.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives
- Valet operators listed by ACSA work from the ground floor of Parkade 2, including Katanga Executive Parking, Cape Executive Parking, APS Valet Service and Automotion. Compare their totals with the official parkades before you book.

## Public transport
- The official pages we checked do not give public transport details. See ACSA's Cape Town International public transport page before you travel.

## Lounges at CPT by terminal
From [ACSA lounges](https://www.airports.co.za/airports/cape-town-international-airport/at-the-airport/lounges) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/south-africa/cape-town-international), checked 25 Nov 2026.

### Domestic departures terminal
- **South African Airways Lounge**
- **British Airways Terraces Lounge**
- **Bidvest Premier Lounge:** Hours: 04:30 - 19:30 (Priority Pass listing).
- **SLOW Lounge**

### International departures terminal
- **British Airways Terraces Lounge**
- **Bidvest Premier Lounge:** Hours: 04:00 - 00:30 (Priority Pass listing).
- **CPT Premier Lounge:** Hours: 05:30 - 23:30 (Priority Pass listing).
- **Smokers' lounge:** Managed by British American Tobacco, free entrance.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** Access depends on your ticket class, frequent flyer status and airline rules.
- **Pay per visit:** ACSA says The Premier Lounge is open to any passenger, whatever the airline or ticket class, by paying per visit or buying a pre-paid card for six or more visits.
- **Priority Pass:** Priority Pass lists the two Premier lounges in the International Terminal and the Bidvest Premier Lounge in the Domestic Terminal.
- **Bank-branded lounges:** Access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
