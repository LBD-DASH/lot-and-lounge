## Official SLC parking rates

From SLC's [Parking](https://slcairport.com/parking-transportation/parking/), [Economy Parking](https://slcairport.com/parking-and-transportation/parking/economy-parking/) and [Lot E](https://slcairport.com/parking-and-transportation/parking/lot-e) pages, last checked 24 Nov 2026.

| Parking option | Hourly rate | Per 24 hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-Term Economy | $2 first hour, then $1 each additional hour | $14 (daily maximum) |
| Lot E | $5 per hour | $25 (daily maximum) |
| Hourly Garage, Levels 2 to 5 | $5 per hour | $45 |
| Daily Garage, Levels 2 and 3 | not listed | $45 |
| Premium Reserved, Garage Level 2 | not listed | $65 |

[Long-Term Economy](https://slcairport.com/parking-and-transportation/parking/economy-parking/) includes a free shuttle to the terminal every eight to ten minutes, 24 hours a day. Cash and major cards are accepted. Economy lots are west of the parking area: the Blue lot (shuttle stops 1 to 5) and the Red lot (stops 6 to 8).

[Lot E](https://slcairport.com/parking-and-transportation/parking/lot-e) is outdoor and uncovered. It is just east of the Parking Garage, across the street from the terminal, and you walk.

The Garage is between the terminal and economy lots, directly south of the terminal. Valet services are in the hourly parking garage. SLC offers online parking and valet reservations, but no prices for those are on the parking page.

SLC does not say whether rates include tax. Vehicles parked over 30 days may be relocated at the owner's expense. Oversized vehicles need special arrangements and extra fees.

EV charging is free, first come first served. There are 78 dual-port Level 2 chargers in the economy lot, Lot E and every garage level.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

These 7-day totals are our arithmetic, using the daily maximums above.

| Parking option | 7-day formula | 7-day total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Economy | 7 × $14 | $98 |
| Lot E | 7 × $25 | $175 |
| Garage | 7 × $45 | $315 |
| Premium Reserved | 7 × $65 | $455 |

**The takeaway:** Economy saves $217 over the garage for a week.

## Public transport

Source: [SLC Public Transportation](https://slcairport.com/parking-and-transportation/public-transportation/).

Take the UTA TRAX Green Line. The Airport station is on the ground level outside the terminal on the east side. Exit door 1A and go east. One-way fare is $2.50. Schedules are at rideuta.com.

## Lounges at SLC by terminal

Sources: [SLC Airport Services](https://slcairport.com/airport-services/), [American Express lounge finder](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/SLC), [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/salt-lake-city-international). Checked 24 Nov 2026. Hours are not listed. Check with the operator. All lounges are after security.

### Concourse A

Delta Sky Club. The entrance is around the corner from Starbucks.

Minute Suites offers private suites. Amex and Priority Pass list it.

### Concourse B

Delta Sky Club. The entrance is by Ally the Allosaurus, near Gate B26 per Amex.

The Centurion Lounge is in Concourse B-east. The entrance is next to Gate B31.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (Delta Sky Club):** membership, eligible premium tickets or status, under Delta's rules.
- **Priority Pass:** on 24 Nov 2026 it listed only Minute Suites (Concourse A, sleep pod) and XpresSpa (Concourse A); no traditional lounges.
- **Bank-branded lounges (The Centurion Lounge):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
