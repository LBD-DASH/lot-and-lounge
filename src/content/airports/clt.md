## Official CLT parking rates

From [CLT's official parking pages](https://www.cltairport.com/parking/parking-options/), last checked 19 Nov 2026. The pages give a daily maximum for each product. We found no statement on whether tax is included, so check at booking.

| Lot | Daily max | How you can get in | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long Term Lot 1 | **$14** | Online booking only | Free shuttle |
| Long Term Lot 2 | **$14** (credit card only) | Drive-up | Free shuttle |
| Daily North Lot | $18 (credit card only) | Drive-up | Free shuttle |
| Express Deck Self-Park | $24 | Online booking only | Non-stop Express Deck shuttle to the terminal's lower level, Zone 2 |
| Express Deck Preferred | $24 | Online booking only | As above |
| Daily Decks | $28 | Online booking only | Free shuttle between the two decks and the terminal |
| Hourly Deck | $35 (credit card only; $8/hour on the first day) | Drive-up | Deck at the terminal; see CLT for the walk |
| Valet | $50 | Book online; CLT says drive-up is available if online is sold out | Valet area on Level 1 of the Hourly Deck |

**Booking:** CLT says it guarantees availability for travelers who book online ahead, even on the busiest days. Express Deck Preferred changed on 1 July 2026 from valet to a traditional self-park deck, and is still online-booking only. Valet is a separate product, and needs a physical key.

**Shuttles (per CLT):** run 24 hours a day, every 10–15 minutes depending on traffic. Call 704-359-4038 if you return after 11 p.m. and want to be sure a shuttle is available. On your return, shuttles pick up on the inner lanes outside the Arrivals/Baggage Claim level.

**Good to know (per CLT):** you can leave a vehicle for up to 30 days without telling parking staff. Height limits: 7'6" at the Express Decks, 8'4" at the Daily Decks' entrance and first level (7' elsewhere) and 7' at valet.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic: 7 × the published daily maximum. Online-only products need a booking, and prices shown at booking may differ.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Long Term Lot 1 or 2 | **$98** (7 × $14) |
| Daily North Lot | $126 (7 × $18) |
| Express Deck Self-Park or Preferred | $168 (7 × $24) |
| Daily Decks | $196 (7 × $28) |
| Hourly Deck | $245 (7 × $35) |
| Valet | $350 (7 × $50) |

The takeaway: the Long Term lots save $98 against the Daily Decks for a week, and $252 against valet.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Private lots near CLT sell daily parking with shuttles. Compare their final total, after taxes and fees, against Long Term Lot 2 at $14 a day. CLT's own pages name no authorized off-airport operators.
- CLT lists a cell phone lot for waiting to pick someone up.

## Public transport

From the CATS airport pages, checked 19 Nov 2026:

- **CATS Sprinter (Route 5)** runs between the airport and the Charlotte Transportation Center in Uptown. CATS lists **$2.50 per ride** and weekday service from 5:30 a.m. to 1:22 a.m., Saturday from 6:00 a.m. to 1:22 a.m. and Sunday from 6:00 a.m. to 1:14 a.m.
- **Route 60** connects the airport to the Blue Line's Tyvola Station, every 30 minutes, seven days a week.
- **Frequency:** CATS's airport page says every 30 minutes for the Sprinter. A CATS service change effective 5 Oct 2026 is reported to make Route 5 every 15 minutes on weekdays and 20 on weekends, but we couldn't confirm that on CATS's own pages. Check the current timetable.

## Lounges at CLT by concourse

From CLT's official [lounge news](https://www.cltairport.com/news/stories/item/more-lounges-elevated-comfort/), [American Airlines](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travelInformation/airportAmenities/clt-club.html), the [Amex Centurion page](https://thecenturionlounge.americanexpress.com/locations/clt/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/charlotte-douglas-international), checked 19 Nov 2026. We list no hours: check with each operator.

### Concourse A
- **Delta Sky Club**, Concourse A (Delta says the mezzanine level)
- **Provisions by Admirals Club**, next to Gate A1 (a grab-and-go snack space)
- **The Club at CLT**. CLT places it in the A Connector; Priority Pass lists it in Concourse A.

### Concourse B
- **American Airlines Admirals Club**, through security, between Gates 3 and 5

### Concourse C and D
- **American Airlines Admirals Club**, at the intersection of Concourses C and D, after security

### Concourses D and E
- **American Express Centurion Lounge**, mezzanine level where Concourses D and E meet

### Elsewhere in the terminal
- **USO Center**, Atrium, upper level (for military)

CLT says a **Capital One Lounge** is planned for the mezzanine of Concourse A, with no opening date announced, so it isn't open as far as we can tell.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (Admirals Club, Delta Sky Club):** membership, eligible tickets or elite status, under each airline's rules.
- **Priority Pass:** on 19 Nov 2026 it listed The Club CLT (Concourse A), Minute Suites (Concourse D and the Atrium), Gameway (Concourse E) and Be Relax Spa (Concourse A).
- **Bank-branded lounges (Centurion):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
