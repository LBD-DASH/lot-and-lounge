## Official LHR parking rates

From Heathrow's [Terminal Parking](https://www.heathrow.com/transport-and-directions/heathrow-parking/heathrow-terminal-parking) and [Park & Ride](https://www.heathrow.com/transport-and-directions/heathrow-parking/heathrow-park-and-ride) pages, last checked 20 Nov 2026. These are the "on the day" (drive-up) prices; the Terminal Parking table says they're effective 3 November 2026. Heathrow's price tables don't mention VAT.

| Car park | Drive-up rate | Each extra 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Terminal Parking** (formerly Short Stay; Terminals 2, 3 and 5) | £8.50 to 29 min, £12.50 to 44 min, £16.00 to 59 min, then £8.50 per hour; **£100.00** day maximum | £100.00 | Next to the terminal, via covered walkways |
| **Park & Ride** (formerly Long Stay) | Free under 30 min; £10.00 from 30 min to 1 h 59 min; **£50.00** first day | £40.00 | Free shuttle every 15 minutes, 04:00–23:30 |

**Name change:** Heathrow renamed Short Stay to Terminal Parking on 29 September 2025 and Long Stay to Park & Ride. Its road signs still say Short Stay, so follow those.

**Good to know (per Heathrow):**
- Terminal Parking can be pre-booked for stays over nine hours, up to two hours before you arrive. Shorter stays are paid at the machines or exit gates. The longest pre-bookable stay is 99 days; drive-up has no limit.
- Pre-booked Park & Ride and Terminal Parking can be amended or cancelled free up to two hours before arrival.
- The T5 Terminal Parking car park has a 2.20 m height limit. Taller vehicles use the Fast Track/Overheight entrance on the ground floor: £20.00 for the first hour, £15.00 per extra hour, £115.00 per day.
- Park & Ride shuttle times to the terminal doors: 10–20 minutes for Terminals 2 and 3, 10–15 minutes for Terminal 4. The T2 & 3 car park is on Eastern Perimeter Road (TW6 2SB) and the T4 car park is on Scylla Road (TW6 3XL).
- Picking up from the terminal forecourts is prohibited, and Heathrow says unattended cars will be removed by the police.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from Heathrow's drive-up prices. Pre-booked prices change with dates and demand, so get a quote on Heathrow's site and compare.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Park & Ride, drive-up (£50 + 6 × £40) | **£290** |
| Terminal Parking, drive-up (7 × £100) | £700 |
| T5 Fast Track/Overheight (7 × £115) | £805 |

The takeaway: turning up at Terminal Parking for a week costs £410 more than Park & Ride. For anything over a day, get a pre-booked quote first.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Heathrow's own **Park & Ride** car parks are the official long-stay option (see above).
- Private off-airport car parks and meet-and-greet services operate around Heathrow. Compare final totals, including any fees, before booking.
- You can skip the car: the Elizabeth line, Heathrow Express and the London Underground all serve the airport (below).

## Public transport

From Heathrow's rail pages and Heathrow Express, checked 20 Nov 2026:

- **Heathrow Express** runs non-stop to **London Paddington**. A Standard single is **£26.00** and a First Class single is £32.00. An **Advance Discounted Single** booked 30+ days ahead is from **£10.00** (non-refundable). A same-day return is £30.
- **Elizabeth line:** Heathrow says journeys to or from the airport that start, end or pass through Zone 1 start from **£13.90**, paid with contactless or Oyster. Six trains an hour serve Terminals 2 and 3. Check TfL's fare finder for your exact fare.
- **London Underground:** Heathrow calls the **Piccadilly line** the most cost-effective rail route into London. Trains stop at all Heathrow terminals.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges** (British Airways, American, Virgin Atlantic, Emirates, Qatar Airways and others): eligible premium-cabin tickets or frequent-flyer status, under each airline's rules.
- **Independent lounges** (Club Aspire, No.1, My Lounge, Clubrooms, Plaza Premium): a paid visit booked with the operator, or a membership where the operator accepts it.
- **Priority Pass:** on 20 Nov 2026 it listed No.1 Lounge and Plaza Premium Lounge (T2); No1 Lounge Heathrow, My Lounge, Clubrooms (additional fee) and Plaza Premium Lounge (T3); Blush Lounge and Plaza Premium Lounge (T4); and Plaza Premium Lounge (T5).
- **Bank-branded lounges (Centurion):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
