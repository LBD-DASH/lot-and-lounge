## Where to park at Suvarnabhumi

Airports of Thailand (AOT) runs these car parks:

- **Zones 2 and 3:** 6-storey parking buildings with lifts, linked to the passenger terminal on Level 3 (Doors 3 and 8). Together they hold more than 4,450 cars.
- **Zones 1 and 4:** open-air lots beside the parking buildings, the closest to the terminal.
- **Zone 5:** open-air lot beside the Novotel Suvarnabhumi.
- **Zones 6 and 7:** open-air lots opposite the Novotel, used as overflow when other lots are full.
- **Long-term parking (Zone A and Zone D, Public Transportation Center):** near the public transport pick-up point, with more than 1,341 spaces. AOT calls it the cheaper choice for overnight stays.

## Official AOT parking rates

From the rate tables on Suvarnabhumi Airport's **parking zones** page and AOT's car parking page, checked 20 Nov 2026. Rates are in Thai baht, for cars.

| Time | Zones 2–7 | Long-term (Zones A and D) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 hour | ฿25 | ฿20 |
| 2 hours | ฿50 | ฿40 |
| 3 hours | ฿80 | ฿60 |
| 4 hours | ฿110 | ฿80 |
| 5 hours | ฿145 | ฿100 |
| 6 hours | ฿180 | ฿120 |
| 7–24 hours | **฿250** | **฿140** |

- Part-hours are charged as full hours.
- After 24 hours the count starts again from the first hour.
- Pick-up and drop-off vehicles are free for 15 minutes.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the tables above. Each 24 hours is charged at the 7–24-hour rate.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Long-term parking, Zone A or D (7 × ฿140) | **฿980** |
| Zones 2–7 (7 × ฿250) | ฿1,750 |

The takeaway: for any overnight trip, use the long-term car park. It's ฿110 a day cheaper than the parking buildings. Because the count restarts every 24 hours, a few extra hours on your last day are charged at the hourly rates.

## Public transport: Airport Rail Link

The **Airport Rail Link** station is on **Level B1** of the main terminal, and trains run **05:30–24:00**. For help, AOT lists the Era One call centre (+66 2 091 1595). Suvarnabhumi's official transport page doesn't publish rail fares, so we don't list them. Check the fare at the station before you plan around it.

The same AOT page also lists taxis (Level 1, between Gates 4 and 7), AOT Limousine, public buses and vans.

- **Airline lounges** (Thai Airways, Bangkok Airways, Singapore Airlines, JAL, EVA Air, China Airlines, Emirates, Oman Air, Turkish Airlines, Qatar Airways, Cathay Pacific, Air France-KLM): eligible tickets or status, under each airline's rules.
