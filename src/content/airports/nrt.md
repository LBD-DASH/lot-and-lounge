## Where to park at Narita

Narita's official car parks: **P-1 and P-5** are on the **Terminal 1** side, and **P-2 and P-3** are on the **Terminal 2** side. Narita asks **Terminal 3** passengers to use the Terminal 2 car parks. P-1 and P-5 can fill up. If they do, park at Terminal 2 and take the free inter-terminal shuttle bus. Reserved spaces are available in P-1, P-2 and P-5.

## Official Narita parking charges

From Narita Airport's **parking charges** page (standard car), checked 20 Nov 2026.

| Time | P-1, P-2 (and cargo area) | P-3, P-5 |
|---|---|---|
| First 30 min | Free | Free |
| Up to 3.5 h | ¥260 per 30 min | ¥210 per 30 min |
| 3.5 h to 24 h | ¥2,100 fixed | **¥1,570 fixed** |
| Days 2–5 (24–120 h) | ¥260 per 30 min, capped at ¥2,100 per 24 h | ¥210 per 30 min, capped at ¥1,570 per 24 h |
| From day 6 (over 120 h) | ¥10,500 + **¥1,000 per 24 h** | ¥7,850 + **¥1,000 per 24 h** |

You can pay by cash, credit card or electronic money. Narita also advertises early-morning and late-night parking discounts. See its parking page for the conditions.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the tariff above. Skyliner fares are per person.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Skyliner, Ueno or Nippori ↔ Narita, IC card, return (2 × ¥2,465) | ¥4,930 per person |
| P-3 or P-5 (¥7,850 for 120 h + 2 × ¥1,000) | **¥9,850** |
| P-1 or P-2 (¥10,500 for 120 h + 2 × ¥1,000) | ¥12,500 |

The takeaway: for one person, the Skyliner costs about half as much as a week in P-3 or P-5. For two people the costs are about even, so driving can make sense, especially for longer trips at ¥1,000 a day from day 6.

## Public transport: Keisei Skyliner

The **Keisei Skyliner** runs from Narita Airport Terminal 1 and Terminal 2·3 stations to Tokyo. From Keisei's fare table:

- **Nippori or Keisei-Ueno: ¥2,470** one way for adults (¥1,240 child), or **¥2,465** with an IC card such as Suica or PASMO at the gate.
- **Akihabara**, changing to the JR Yamanote Line at Nippori: ¥2,630 (the Skyliner fare plus ¥160 for the JR Yamanote Line).

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** eligible tickets or status, under each airline's rules. Narita says to check entry conditions with your airline.
- **Card company lounges (NOA, KoCoo, Executive Lounges 1 and 2):** Narita says they're for same-day passengers holding eligible cards, at gold level or higher, from the affiliated card companies. Eligible cards differ before and after passport control. We don't recommend cards.
- **ASPIRE LOUNGE and NARITA PREMIER LOUNGE:** Narita lists both with the airline lounges, for eligible passengers of the airlines that use them. Check with your airline or the operator about paid entry or lounge memberships.
