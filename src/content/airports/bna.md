## Official BNA parking rates
From [FlyNashville Parking](https://flynashville.com/park-at-bna), last checked 25 Nov 2026. Since 1 July 2026 BNA parking is cashless; travelers who need to use cash can convert it to a prepaid card at Cash-to-Card machines inside the terminal. Cards accepted: Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valet Parking | $48 flat daily rate | $48 | Ground floor of Terminal Garage 1, across from the terminal; 24/7 |
| Terminal Garage 1 & 2 | Free up to 30 min, $18 for 30 to 60 min, $36 after 60 min | $36 | Short walk to the terminal |
| Economy Lot B | Free up to 10 min, then $24 | $24 | Free shuttle every 10 minutes; shuttle station on the Arrivals Level (Level 2) |
| Economy Lot C | Free up to 10 min, then $24 | $24 | Shuttle from the Arrivals Level (Level 2) shuttle station |

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from official rates (7 × daily maximum).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Valet Parking | 7 × $48 = $336 |
| Terminal Garage 1 & 2 | 7 × $36 = $252 |
| Economy Lot B & C | 7 × $24 = $168 |

The takeaway: a week in Economy Lot B or C costs $84 less than the terminal garages and $168 less than valet.

## Lounges at BNA by terminal
From [American Airlines](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travelInformation/airportAmenities/bna-club.html), [American Express](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/BNA/Delta-Sky-Club-North-Terminal-Concourse-B-rC1h2vJctk/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/nashville-international), checked 25 Nov 2026.

### Concourse B
- **Delta Sky Club:** North Terminal, adjacent to Gate B1.

### Concourse C
- **American Airlines Admirals Club:** After security, mezzanine level between Gates C14 and C16. Hours: daily 4 a.m. to 7:30 p.m.

### Concourse D
- **Minute Suites Nashville:** Sleep pods, Concourse D. Hours: 04:00 - 23:00 (listed by Priority Pass).

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** Access is set by each airline's membership, ticket and status rules. Check with the airline before you go.
- **Priority Pass:** Priority Pass lists Minute Suites in Concourse D.
- **Bank-branded lounges:** Access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
