## Official BOS parking rates

From Massport's [Terminal Parking](https://www.massport.com/logan-airport/getting-to-logan/parking/terminal-parking) and [Economy Parking](https://www.massport.com/logan-airport/getting-to-logan/parking/economy-parking) pages, last checked 23 Nov 2026. Massport doesn't say whether these rates include tax.

| Length of stay | Central and Terminal B garages | Economy Parking Garage |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 1 hour | $10 | $10 |
| 1 to 2 hours | $26 | $26 |
| 2 to 3 hours | $31 | $28 |
| 3 to 4 hours | $36 | $31 |
| 4 to 7 hours | $40 | $37 (4 to 24 hours) |
| 7 to 24 hours | **$46** | **$37** |
| Each additional day, first 6 hours | $23 | $19 |
| Each additional day, 6 to 24 hours | $46 | $37 |

Lost ticket: the applicable parking fee plus $5. Massport now shows a "Parking Garages are Now Cashless" alert, so don't plan on paying cash. Kiosks take major cards and prepaid reservations.

**Getting to the terminal:** Central Parking Garage connects to Terminals A, B, C and E by pedestrian bridges on Level 4, with enclosed walkways to Terminals C and E. The Terminal B garage serves Terminal B. The Economy Parking Garage is about 2 miles from the terminals with a free shuttle from Level 1 to all terminals. Massport's FAQ gives two frequencies for that shuttle on the same page: "approximately every 15 minutes" and "approximately every 3–5 minutes". Walking is not recommended.

**Booking:** Massport says a limited number of spaces are available for $29 per day with an Advance Parking Reservation at both terminal garages and Economy. Massport says weekly and monthly Economy rates are not offered.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from Massport's rate tables: the first 24 hours plus six additional full days.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy Parking Garage | $259 ($37 + 6 × $37) |
| Central or Terminal B garage | $322 ($46 + 6 × $46) |
| Advance Parking Reservation at $29 per day (if available) | $203 (7 × $29) |

**The takeaway:** Economy saves $63 over the terminal garages for a week. A $29 advance reservation, when spaces are available, is cheaper again. Logan Express (below) is far cheaper for a week.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- **Logan Express** (Massport's own buses to all terminals): park at Braintree, Danvers, Framingham or Woburn. Massport's Logan Express page says $7 a day parking and $9 each way when you buy online, marked as a limited-time offer. Kids ride free with a ticketed family member. Its Logan Express parking page lists Danvers, Framingham and Woburn at $7 per day, and Braintree at $9 per day on weekdays and $3 per day on weekends (advance reservation only, via SpotHero).
- Our arithmetic for a week from Framingham, Danvers or Woburn with one adult: $49 parking (7 × $7) plus $18 for two $9 rides, for a total of $67.
- Back Bay Logan Express: $3 to the airport with a free ride back. No parking listed.
- Private off-airport lots exist. Massport says it offers the only official parking at the airport, so compare final totals including fees.

Links: [Logan Express](https://www.massport.com/logan-airport/getting-to-logan/logan-express) and [Logan Express parking](https://www.massport.com/logan-airport/getting-to-logan/logan-express/parking).

## Public transport

From [Massport: Public Transportation](https://www.massport.com/logan-airport/getting-to-logan/public-transportation):

- **Silver Line SL1:** picks up at every terminal curb and runs to the South Boston Waterfront/Seaport and South Station. Massport says it is free from the airport to downtown, with a free transfer to the Red Line. Going to the airport requires a standard fare.
- **Blue Line:** take Massport's free On-Airport Shuttle (Routes 22, 33, 55 or 66) to Airport Station. Massport says a one-way subway or bus fare is usually $2.40 with a CharlieCard or contactless payment.
- **Water ferry:** free On-Airport Shuttle Route 66 to the Water Transportation Dock for MBTA ferries to Long Wharf, Hull and Hingham.
- Check the MBTA for schedules and current fares.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** eligible tickets, membership or status, under each airline's rules.
- **Priority Pass:** on 23 Nov 2026 it listed The Lounge (Terminal C) and the Air France Lounge (Terminal E), plus restaurant offers at Davio's Northern Italian Steakhouse (Terminal C) and Stephanies (Terminals B and E).
- **Bank-branded lounges (Chase Sapphire):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards. American Express lists a Centurion Lounge for Terminal C as coming soon, expected in 2029.
