## Where to park at O.R. Tambo

ACSA's [parking map](https://www.airports.co.za/airports/or-tambo-international-airport/At-the-airport/parking-maps) groups the parkades by terminal:

- **Near International Terminal A:** P1 Parkade 1 (short stay), P4 Carports (long stay), P7 open parking (pick-up and drop-off, Piazza).
- **Near Domestic Terminal B:** P2 Parkade 2 South (short stay on levels 2–4, premium/XXL on level 2, valet on level 4, car rental on level 1) and P3 Parkade 2 North (short stay on levels 0, 2, 3 and 4, pre-booked parking in the lower basement).

## Official ACSA parking tariffs

From ACSA's **ORTIA Parking Tariffs 2025–2026** sheet, last checked 19 Nov 2026. The tariff sheet names facilities (Basements, MSP1, MSP2) that don't map neatly to the P-numbers on the map, so check the signs at the entrance.

| Time | Basements | MSP1 (Levels 3 & 4) | MSP2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–30 min | Free | Free | not listed |
| 0–1 h | R40 | R30 | R20 |
| 1–2 h | R50 | R50 | R30 |
| 2–3 h | R58 | R58 | R35 |
| 3–4 h | R80 | R80 | R45 |
| 4–12 h | R200 | R200 | R105 |
| 12–24 h | R220 | R220 | R180 |
| After that | R110 per 12 h | R110 per 12 h | R90 per 12 h |

**MSP2 long stay (Level -1), as printed by ACSA:** 0–3 days R540; 4 days R288 (4 × R72); 5 days R360 (5 × R72); then R72 per 24 h. Yes, ACSA's sheet prices 4 days below 3 days. Check the price at the boom.

**Pre-booked parking:** **R72 per 24 h** if you book 5 days ahead at ACSA's [pre-booking site](https://prebooking.airportsapp.co.za/). The drive-in gate rate is R180 for the first 24 h and **R180 per 12 h** after that, so don't use the pre-booked area without a booking.

**Parkade 2 South notice:** ACSA's parking-calculator page lists a different tariff for **Parkade 2 South Levels 3 & 4** (the preferred drop-off area): 30 min free, R30 to 1 h, R48 for 1–2 h, R55 for 2–3 h, R75 for 3–4 h, R190 for 4–12 h, R210 for 12–24 h, then R110 per 12 h. Its **Level 2** is for pick-up only: 30 min free, R30 to 60 min, R60 to 120 min, then R60 per hour. ACSA doesn't recommend it for overnight parking.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the ACSA and Gautrain tariffs above. Gautrain fares are per person.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Pre-booked (5+ days ahead), 7 × R72 | **R504** |
| MSP2 long stay (R360 for 5 days + 2 × R72) | **R504** |
| Gautrain from Sandton: station parking R99 (7 days, rail user) + R228 × 2 | R555 per person |
| MSP2 short stay (R180 + 12 × R90) | R1,260 |
| Basements or MSP1 (R220 + 12 × R110) | R1,540 |
| Pre-booked area **without** a booking (R180 + 12 × R180) | R2,340 |

The takeaway: book 5 days ahead or use MSP2 long stay. Turning up at the pre-booked area without a booking can cost more than four times as much.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride: Gautrain

The **Gautrain** connects the airport's station (ACSA's map labels it the "Gautrain Terminal") with Rhodesfield, Marlboro and Sandton, and on to Rosebank, Park and the Pretoria line. On the fare table **effective 1 September 2026**:

- **O.R. Tambo ↔ Sandton or Marlboro: R228** one way. O.R. Tambo ↔ Rosebank or Park: R240. O.R. Tambo ↔ Midrand: R240. O.R. Tambo ↔ Centurion, Pretoria or Hatfield: R258.
- **Station parking for rail users** (with a return train trip): R10 for 1–24 h, R27 for 2 days, R70 for 5 days, R99 for 7 days, R142 for 10 days, then R135 for each extra day. Non-rail users pay much more (R135 per day).

On cost alone, pre-booked airport parking (R72 a day) beats Gautrain plus station parking even for one person, because the train costs R456 return. Gautrain makes sense if you'd rather not drive to the airport, or if someone drops you at a station.

## Lounges at O.R. Tambo by terminal

From ACSA's official lounge list, SAA's lounge page and Priority Pass, checked 19 Nov 2026. Where the sources disagree, we say so.

### Domestic departures (Terminal B)
- **South African Airways: The Lounge** (SAA lists separate Platinum and Premium lounges at O.R. Tambo Domestic)
- **Bidvest Premier Lounge** (ACSA calls it the Bidvest Domestic Lounge). Priority Pass lists it.
- **Bidvest Sky Lounge** (Priority Pass calls it "ORT Sky Lounge")
- **British Airways Slow Lounge** is still on ACSA's list. Confirm with the operator before relying on it.
- **Menzies Mashonzha Lounge**: ACSA lists it under domestic, but Priority Pass lists it under International Terminal A. Check before you go.

### International departures (Terminal A)
- **SAA lounges:** SAA lists an International Platinum Lounge, an International Premium Lounge and an International Arrivals Lounge. ACSA's list uses the names SAA Baobab Lounge, SAA Cycad Lounge and SAA Arrival Lounge.
- **Bidvest Premier Lounge**. Priority Pass lists it as open 24 hours.
- **Menzies Shongololo Lounge**. Priority Pass lists it.
- **Aspire Lounge** (Swissport). Priority Pass lists it.
- **British Airways Lounge**
- **Emirates Lounge**
- **Air France Lounge**
- **Virgin Atlantic Clubhouse**

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges** (SAA, British Airways, Emirates, Air France, Virgin Atlantic): eligible premium-cabin tickets or frequent-flyer status, under each airline's rules.
- **Independent lounges** (Bidvest, Menzies Shongololo/Mashonzha, Aspire, Sky Lounge): Priority Pass where listed, or a paid visit where the operator sells one. Check the operator's site for current prices.
