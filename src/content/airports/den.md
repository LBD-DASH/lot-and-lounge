## Official DEN parking rates

From DEN's official [parking lots page](https://www.flydenver.com/parking-and-transportation/parking-lots/), last checked 19 Nov 2026. DEN's fee estimator returned these prices for a sample stay in December 2026; the 6-hour and 24-hour results were identical, so each lot works as a flat daily rate. DEN's FAQ says its parking facilities never add taxes or additional fees to the published rates.

| Lot | Per day | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|
| Pikes Peak shuttle lot | **$10** | Free shuttle |
| West Economy / East Economy | **$20** | Free shuttle |
| West Garage / East Garage | **$35** | Walk to the terminal |
| Premium Reserve (West or East, Arrivals Level 4) | **$45** | Walk to the terminal; reservation required |

**Reservations:** DEN only takes reservations for Premium Reserve, booked at least 24 hours ahead at [go.lazparking.com/flydenver/park](https://go.lazparking.com/flydenver/park). Every other lot is first come, first served, and DEN says you don't need a coupon or reservation. DEN also lists hourly lots and a 61st and Peña lot; DEN says short-term parking isn't intended for overnight stays, so we leave them out of the tables.

**Shuttles (per DEN):** free, 24 hours a day, every 7–10 minutes, picking up at the shelters in the Economy lots. On your return, West and East Economy shuttles pick up at the DEN Transit Center outside the Westin. Shuttle stops can change during construction.

**Good to know (per DEN):** maximum garage clearance is 8 feet and DEN can't take oversized vehicles. Parking beyond 30 days needs advance approval. There's a 7-minute grace period in the Garage and Economy lots. EV charging is free to use on Level 1 of the garages and at 61st and Peña, but standard parking rates apply. DEN warns of vehicle break-ins and catalytic converter theft, and says you park at your own risk.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic: 7 × the official daily rate (the DEN estimator shows the same totals for a 7-day stay).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Pikes Peak shuttle lot | **$70** (7 × $10) |
| Economy lots | $140 (7 × $20) |
| Terminal garages | $245 (7 × $35) |
| Premium Reserve | $315 (7 × $45) |

The takeaway: Pikes Peak saves $175 against the garages for a week, and $245 against Premium Reserve.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- DEN's parking page lists several privately run off-airport lots as options. Compare their final total, after taxes and fees, against Pikes Peak at $10 a day and Economy at $20.
- RTD lists its own Park-n-Ride lots on its [parking page](https://www.rtd-denver.com/how-to-ride/parking), which DEN links to. Check the fee and the Airport fare requirement before relying on one.
- DEN's free cell phone waiting lot, Final Approach, is about three miles from the terminal on 77th Avenue.

## Public transport

From DEN's official public transit page and RTD's fares page, checked 19 Nov 2026:

- **A Line train** from the DEN Transit Center to **Denver Union Station**.
- **RTD buses** AB, AL, AT, 104L, 145X and 169L also serve the Transit Center. DEN lists buses from about 3:30 a.m. to midnight, with the last bus leaving the airport at 1:28 a.m., and about every 15 minutes at peak.
- **The Transit Center** is at the south end of the Jeppesen Terminal: exit through the glass doors and take the escalator or elevator down to ground level.

**Fares (RTD):** any trip to or from DEN needs an Airport fare product. The standard **Airport Day Pass is $10**. A standard 3-Hour Pass holder pays a $7.25 airport upgrade, and a Day Pass holder pays $4.50. Discount fares, for seniors 65+, people with disabilities, Medicare recipients and LiVE members, include the airport at no extra charge. Youth 19 and under and active-duty US military ride free.

## Lounges at DEN by concourse

From DEN's official [lounge areas page](https://www.flydenver.com/at-the-airport/lounge-areas/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/denver-international), checked 19 Nov 2026. We list no hours: check with each operator.

### Concourse A
- **Capital One Lounge (Plaza Premium)**, near Gate A-34, Mezzanine Level
- **Delta Sky Club**, past security at the north end of Concourse A, on the bridge from the Jeppesen Terminal
- **United Club (A Gates West)**, near Gate A-25, Mezzanine Level
- **USO Denver**, Level 4, above the Mezzanine Level in the centre of Concourse A

### Concourse B
- **United Club (B Gates East)**, near Gate B-44, Mezzanine Level
- **United Club (B Gates West)**, near Gate B-32, Mezzanine Level
- **United Club Fly**, near Gate B-60

### Concourse C
- **American Airlines Admirals Club**, near Gate C-32, Mezzanine Level
- **American Express Centurion Lounge**, C Gates Mezzanine, near Gate C-46

DEN also lists a free **Rest and Recharge** seating area on the Concourse A mezzanine.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (Admirals Club, Delta Sky Club, United Club):** membership, eligible tickets or elite status, under each airline's rules.
- **Priority Pass:** on 19 Nov 2026 it listed **no lounges** at DEN. It listed a restaurant voucher at Mercantile Dining and Provision and Golf DEN, both on Concourse A.
- **Bank-branded lounges (Centurion, Capital One):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
