## Where to park at Incheon

Each terminal has **short-term** and **long-term** car parks, and there is a separate **reserved** car park. Long-term parking at Terminal 1 includes the **T1 Parking Tower**. The airport advises using long-term parking for any stay over one day. The short-term car park takes only passenger vehicles up to 2.1 m high, and the reserved car park doesn't accept vehicles over 2.8 m.

## Official Incheon parking fees

From Incheon Airport's **parking fees** page (small vehicles), checked 20 Nov 2026. The same rates appear for Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 in the airport's parking terms of use.

| Car park | Rates |
|---|---|
| Short-term | First 10 min free; ₩1,200 for the first 30 min, then ₩600 per 15 min (₩2,400 an hour); **₩24,000 per day** |
| Long-term (including T1 Parking Tower) | First 10 min free; ₩1,000 per hour; **₩9,000 per day** |
| Reserved | First 10 min free; ₩1,000 per hour; **₩9,000 per day** |

**Reserved parking:** book from 45 days to 4 hours before you arrive, on the airport's reservation site or the Incheon Airport+ app (not by phone), for 1 to 30 days. A **₩10,000 deposit** is charged when you book and cancelled after you exit. Fees follow your actual entry and exit times, and payment is by card only at the unmanned exit machines. If you arrive more than 4 hours after your booked time, the booking is cancelled and the deposit is kept.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the daily rates. AREX fares are per person.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| AREX express, Seoul Station ↔ Incheon, return (2 × ₩13,000) | ₩26,000 per person |
| Long-term or reserved parking (7 × ₩9,000) | **₩63,000** |
| Short-term parking (7 × ₩24,000) | ₩168,000 |

The takeaway: never leave the car in short-term parking for a trip. It costs more than double the long-term car parks. For one or two people from central Seoul, the express train is cheaper than a week of long-term parking.

## Public transport: AREX express train

The **AREX express train** runs non-stop between **Seoul Station** and Incheon Airport Terminals 1 and 2. You buy a ticket before boarding. From AREX's express page:

- **Adult ₩13,000, child ₩9,500.** AREX shows the standard fare as ₩18,500 (Seoul Station ↔ Terminal 1) and ₩19,100 (↔ Terminal 2).
- **Journey time:** 43 minutes to Terminal 1, 51 minutes to Terminal 2.
- **Extras:** reserved seats, free Wi-Fi, and free use of the City Airport Terminal at Seoul Station.
- AREX all-stop trains also serve stations such as Gimpo Airport, Hongik University and Digital Media City. Check AREX for those fares.

- **Airline lounges (Korean Air, Asiana, Oneworld, SilverKris, China Eastern):** eligible tickets or status, under each airline's rules. We list Asiana lounges under the names the airport uses today.
