## Official IAH parking rates

From Houston Airports' [Terminal Parking](https://www.fly2houston.com/iah/parking/terminal-parking/), [Valet Parking](https://www.fly2houston.com/iah/parking/iah-valet-parking/) and [ecopark](https://www.fly2houston.com/iah/parking/ecopark/) pages, last checked 23 Nov 2026. The terminal and valet rates below include tax, as Houston Airports lists them.

| Lot | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terminal garages (next to all terminals, A/B and C/D/E) | **$25** (tax included) | walk; next to all terminals |
| Valet (Terminal A street level, Terminal C Level 4 Blue Garage, Terminal E Purple Garage) | **$35** (tax included, 0 to 24 hours) | at the terminal garage |
| ecopark (16152 John F. Kennedy Blvd) and ecopark2 (5021 Will Clayton Parkway) | see note below | free 24/7 shuttle to all terminals |

Hourly rates for terminal garages (tax included):

| Time | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 10 min | $1 |
| 11 min to 1 hr | $5 |
| 1 to 2 hrs | $6 |
| 2 to 3 hrs | $8 |
| 3 to 5 hrs | $10 |
| 5 to 24 hrs | $25 |

**ecopark prices: Houston Airports' own pages disagree.** The [ecopark page](https://www.fly2houston.com/iah/parking/ecopark/) lists no rate. Houston Airports' [How to park](https://www.fly2houston.com/iah/related-content/how-to-park/) article lists ecopark uncovered $9/day, ecopark covered $11/day and ecopark2 covered $10/day. Its [Parking at IAH: top insider tips](https://www.fly2houston.com/iah/related-content/parking-at-iah-top-insider-tips/) article lists ecopark covered $12/day and uncovered $10/day, and ecopark2 covered $12/day. Its [holiday parking](https://www.fly2houston.com/iah/related-content/holiday-parking/) article says ecopark uncovered $9/day and ecopark2 from $10/day. Check the price in the [official booking engine](https://iah.booking.fly2houston.com/book/IAH/Parking?parkingCmd=collectParkingDetails) before you go.

**Good to know (per Houston Airports):**

- Since 1 June 2026, ecopark2 is reservation-only: no drive-up parking.
- Once terminal garage capacity reaches 85%, only pre-booked reservations can park.
- ecopark shuttles run 24 hours a day, about every 10 minutes. One shuttle serves Terminal A. A second serves Terminals C, D and E. The ride takes about 10 minutes depending on traffic. Shuttles leave the lot 10 minutes after the first passenger boards.
- ecopark and ecopark2 take campers and large trucks up to 13'6".
- EV charging is available in the terminal garages and at both ecoparks.
- Discounted online rates were not available for reservations from 15 Dec 2025 to 1 Jan 2026 (Houston Airports' holiday note); check for a similar holiday blackout.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the published daily rates (7 × daily rate). Where Houston Airports' figures disagree, we show both ends.

| Option | 7 days |
| --- | --- |
| ecopark uncovered | $63 (7 × $9) to $70 (7 × $10) |
| ecopark covered | $77 (7 × $11) to $84 (7 × $12) |
| ecopark2 covered (reservation only) | $70 (7 × $10) to $84 (7 × $12) |
| Terminal garage | $175 (7 × $25) |
| Valet | $245 (7 × $35) |

**The takeaway:** ecopark uncovered saves about $105 to $112 over a week in the terminal garage, depending on which published ecopark rate applies.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Private off-airport lots exist. Houston Airports' [off-airport parking shuttles page](https://www.fly2houston.com/iah/ground-transportation/shuttles-and-buses/off-airport-parking-shuttles/) gives their pick-up doors (for example Terminal A Door A-114 and Terminal C Door C-104) but doesn't name or price the lots, so compare final totals including fees against ecopark.

## Public transport

From [METRO: George Bush Airport (IAH)](https://www.ridemetro.org/riding-metro/houston-attractions/george-bush-intercontinental-airport):

- **500 Downtown Direct:** nonstop to downtown Houston for $4.50, every 15 to 30 minutes, from Terminal E, Level 2, outside Door E201 between signs 6A and 6B.
- **102 Bush IAH:** local route to the Downtown Transit Center for $1.25, every 15 to 30 minutes, from Terminal C outside Door C105, with stops including Greenspoint Transit Center.
- Pay with a RideMETRO card, the RideMETRO app, a contactless card or phone, or cash on the bus (cash gets no change and no free transfers).
## Lounges at IAH by terminal

From Houston Airports' [Shop, Dine & Relax directory](https://www.fly2houston.com/iah/shop-dine-relax/?scope=all), its [Terminal B North press release](https://www.fly2houston.com/airport-business/newsroom/press-releases/item/united-airlines-terminal-b-north-opens-at-iah/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/george-bush-intercontinental), checked 23 Nov 2026. The airport directory doesn't list lounge hours, so check with each operator.

### Terminal A
- **American Admirals Club**, Gate A25
- **United Club**, Gate A9

### Terminal B
- **United Club**, Terminal B North, mezzanine level (opened 22 Oct 2026; Houston Airports says it is 54,000 sq ft with seating for 800 and an outdoor terrace)
- **United Club Fly**, south side of Terminal B (grab-and-go format, opened February 2025)

### Terminal C
- **United Club**, Gate C1
- **United Club**, Gate C33

### Terminal D
- **Air France Lounge**, Gate D18
- **Executive Club**, Gate D12
- **KLM Crown Lounge**, Gate D15
- **The Centurion Lounge** by American Express
- **USO** (military members and families), Gate D8

### Terminal E
- **United Club**, Gate E12
- **United Polaris Lounge**, Gate E12

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** eligible tickets, membership or status, under each airline's rules. Houston Airports says United Club locations are open to United Club members, eligible United cardholders and select premium cabin tickets.
- **Priority Pass:** on 23 Nov 2026 it listed the KLM Crown Lounge and Air France Lounge in Terminal D, plus Minute Suites (Terminal C), Be Relax Spa (Terminal D), Landry's Seafood (Terminal C) and Cadillac Mexican Kitchen & Tequila Bar (Terminal A).
- **Bank-branded lounges (Centurion):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
