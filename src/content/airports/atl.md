## Official ATL parking rates

Rates below are the daily rates published on [atl.com/parking](https://www.atl.com/parking/), last checked 19 Nov 2026. ATL doesn't say on that page whether taxes are included, so check the rate posted at the entrance.

| Lot | Official rate | Getting to the terminal (per ATL) |
|---|---|---|
| Hourly (domestic) | $50 max for the first day, then $75 per additional day | At the Domestic terminal |
| Daily (domestic) | $30/day | Domestic terminal lots |
| ATL West Deck | $30/day | 3–5 minute SkyTrain ride; the SkyTrain is on level 4 of the deck |
| Economy | $20/day | 3–5 minute shuttle ride to Domestic check-in and arrivals |
| ATL Select | $15 uncovered · $20 covered · $40 oversized (per day) | Remote lot; check ATL for transfer details |
| Park-Ride | $15/day | Remote lot with a complimentary shuttle |
| International Hourly | $70 max for the first day, then $100 per additional day | Directly in front of the International terminal |
| International Park-Ride | $30/day | Covered, a few minutes from the International terminal |

ATL also runs a free **Park and Wait** lot for people picking up (1920 Autoport Drive, College Park, GA 30337) and lists EV chargers in several lots, including Economy, the ATL West Deck and ATL Park-Ride Select. You can pre-book through **ParkATL** from the official parking page.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the official daily rates above (7 × daily rate; for Hourly, first-day maximum plus 6 extra days). Your real bill depends on when you enter and exit.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| ATL Select uncovered / Park-Ride | **$105** |
| ATL Select covered / Economy | $140 |
| Daily / ATL West Deck / International Park-Ride | $210 |
| ATL Select oversized | $280 |
| Domestic Hourly | $500 |
| International Hourly | $670 |

The takeaway: for a week away, the shuttle lots save roughly $100–$400 compared with the decks next to the terminal.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- **Private off-airport lots.** Several privately run lots near ATL sell daily parking with shuttles. Compare them through the booking sites below, and check whether the quoted price includes taxes and fees and how often the shuttle runs.
- **MARTA station parking.** MARTA says many of its stations offer secure long-term parking for a lower fee than airport lots. See MARTA's airport page for parking options and current fees.

## Public transport

MARTA's **Airport Station is inside the Domestic Terminal**, between the North and South baggage claims. The **Red and Gold lines** run directly to it. MARTA notes that after 8:30 pm, riders heading to Buckhead, Medical Center, Dunwoody, Sandy Springs or North Springs may need to take the Gold line and change to the Red line at Lindbergh Center. Check [MARTA](https://itsmarta.com/airport/) for fares. If you fly from the International Terminal (Concourse F), check ATL's ground transportation page for how to connect to the Domestic Terminal.

## Lounges at ATL by concourse

Locations come from ATL's official amenities page, Delta's Atlanta Sky Club page and Priority Pass's ATL page, checked 19 Nov 2026. We've left out opening hours because the sources disagree. Check the operator's page on the day you fly.

### Concourse A
- **Delta Sky Club**, Centerpoint (second level)
- **Delta Sky Club**, near Gate A17

### Concourse B
- **Delta Sky Club**, near Gate B18
- **Minute Suites** (private rest suites; listed by Priority Pass)
- **Be Relax Spa** (spa; listed by Priority Pass)

### Concourse C
- **Delta Sky Club**, near Gate C37

### Concourse D
- **Delta Sky Club**, D Centerpoint near Gate D18. Delta calls this its newest Atlanta club.

### Concourse E
- **Delta Sky Club**, across from Gate E15
- **The Centurion Lounge** (American Express)
- **Minute Suites** (listed by Priority Pass)

### Concourse F (International)
- **Delta Sky Club**, mezzanine level
- **The Club ATL**, mezzanine level. ATL says it serves British Airways, Lufthansa, Priority Pass, Lounge Club and Diners Club International customers and sells day passes.
- **Poppin**, a grab-and-go lounge listed by Priority Pass
- **Minute Suites** (listed by Priority Pass)

### Concourse T
- **Delta Sky Club**, near Gate T6
- **American Airlines Admirals Club**, across from Gate T10 (per ATL)
- **United Club**, at T11 (per ATL)

### How you get in
- **Delta Sky Club, Admirals Club, United Club:** airline membership, eligible premium-cabin tickets or elite status, under each airline's current rules.
- **The Club ATL and Poppin:** Priority Pass (per Priority Pass), partner airlines or a day pass where sold.
- **Centurion Lounge:** eligible American Express card members only, under Amex's rules. We don't recommend cards.
- **Minute Suites:** pay by the hour, or through Priority Pass where listed.
