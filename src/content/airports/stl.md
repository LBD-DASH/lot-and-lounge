## Official STL parking rates
From [FlySTL Parking](https://www.flystl.com/parking-transportation/), last checked 25 Nov 2026. STL says tax is included in all rates.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terminal 1 Garage | 0-2 hrs $5, 2-4 hrs $14, 4-6 hrs $20, 6-12 hrs $26 | $31 | Walk to Terminal 1 (Air Canada, Alaska, American, Delta, Frontier, Southern Airways Express, Sun Country, United) |
| Terminal 2 Garage | 0-2 hrs $5, 2-4 hrs $14, 4-6 hrs $20, 6-12 hrs $27 | $33 | Walk to Terminal 2 (British Airways, charter flights, Lufthansa, Southwest) |
| Lot C covered | 0-6 hrs $8, 6-12 hrs $10 | $15 | Free shuttle, 24 hours a day, every 8-15 minutes |
| Lot C uncovered | 0-6 hrs $7, 6-12 hrs $9 | $12 | Free shuttle, 24 hours a day, every 8-15 minutes |
| Lot D | 0-24 hrs $11 | $11 | Free shuttle, 24 hours a day, every 8-15 minutes |

Lot C uncovered spaces can also be reserved at the same rates plus a $5.00 reservation fee. STL says Lot E is reservation-only through Super Park from July 1. The garages and Lot C take cash, Visa, MasterCard and American Express; Lot D takes Visa, MasterCard, American Express and Discover. STL suggests allowing 30 extra minutes for parking.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from official rates (7 x daily maximum).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Lot D | $77 (7 x $11) |
| Lot C uncovered | $84 (7 x $12) |
| Lot C covered | $105 (7 x $15) |
| Terminal 1 Garage | $217 (7 x $31) |
| Terminal 2 Garage | $231 (7 x $33) |

The takeaway: Choosing Lot D instead of the Terminal 2 Garage saves you $154 over a 7-day trip.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives
- STL's pages we checked don't name off-airport lots, so if you use a private lot, compare final totals including fees. STL's own lot shuttles can be tracked live in the Passio GO app.

## Public transport
- **MetroLink:** light rail stations at both terminals. Terminal 1: exit Door 1 on the Ticketing Level, east of all ticket counters. Terminal 2: exit Door 12, through the parking garage. Advance fare is required: buy at the ticket machines at both stations or in the Transit app.

## Lounges at STL by terminal
From [FlySTL Shop, Dine & Services](https://www.flystl.com/shop-and-dine/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/lambert-st-louis-international), checked 25 Nov 2026.

### Terminal 1
- **USO**, T1 Baggage Claim near Carousel 6

### C Concourse
- **American Airlines Admirals Club**, C Gates

### Terminal 2 (E Gates)
- **Wingtips St Louis**, at E31 (Priority Pass lists hours 07:00 - 13:00 and 15:30 - 20:00, pre-booking available)

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** access to the Admirals Club is set by American Airlines.
- **Priority Pass:** on 25 Nov 2026 it listed Wingtips Lounge St. Louis in Terminal 2.
- **Bank-branded lounges:** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
