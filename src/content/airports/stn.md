## Lounges at STN by terminal
From [London Stansted Airport Escape Lounge](https://www.stanstedairport.com/at-the-airport/escape-lounge/) and Priority Pass, checked 26 Nov 2026.

### Gates 1-19
- **Essence by Escape Lounges**, located near Gates 1-19 (accessible via the Stansted Transit System). Open daily from 04:45 to 19:30 (Christmas Eve 04:45 to 13:30, Christmas Day closed).

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** Available for passengers flying with eligible airlines, including Air Algerie, Arkia, British Airways Cityflyer, Corendon Airlines, Emirates, Flyone, HiSky, Jet2, Lufthansa, Pegasus, Royal Air Maroc, Royal Jordanian, SunExpress, Tui, and Turkish Airlines. Due to terminal location, Ryanair and easyJet passengers cannot access the lounge.
- **Priority Pass:** DragonPass, Priority Pass, and Lounge Key cardholders can pre-book their lounge visit through the Seat Reservations portal. Cardholders travelling with eligible airlines can use their cards for entry for themselves and up to three guests, subject to availability.
- **Bank-branded lounges:** Access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
