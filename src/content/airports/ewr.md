## Official EWR parking rates
From the Port Authority's [official EWR parking page](https://www.newarkairport.com/transportation/airport-parking), last checked 22 Nov 2026. The airport does not specify whether rates include tax.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Prebooked (as low as) | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P6 Economy Parking | $35 for the first 24 hours | **$35** | $30/day | Free shuttle to Terminals A, B and C, 24/7, about every 10 minutes |
| P4 Daily Parking | $4.25 first 1/2 hour; $8.50 each additional half hour (over three hours) | **$60** | $40/day | AirTrain (P4 Station) |
| Terminal A Short-Term | $5.25 first 1/2 hour; $10.50 each additional half hour (over three hours) | **$65** | $50/day | Closest option for Terminal A |
| Terminals B & C Short-Term | $5.25 first 1/2 hour; $10.50 each additional half hour (over three hours) | **$70** | $50/day | Closest option for Terminals B and C |

**Booking:** prebook through the airport's parking page at least 3 hours in advance. You may enter up to 2 hours before your booked time. Prebooked rates are published only as "as low as", so your quote may be higher. Premium reserved spaces, closer to the terminals, are in each garage and can only be reserved online.

**Good to know (per the Port Authority):**
- Parking for more than 30 days in any lot requires registration with ABM on arrival or the vehicle will be towed.
- Level II charging is available in P4 (13 ports) and the Terminal A Short-Term garage on level 5 (162 ports). Level III charging is also available at EWR.
- Drivers with restricted mobility can receive a courtesy discount with a valid state ADA plate or placard. The 24-hour maximum is $35. This discount applies after a 4.5-hour minimum stay and cannot be used with prebooked rates or E-ZPass.
- Pay with a card, E-ZPass Plus (purple lanes), Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Samsung Pay.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from the official 24-hour maximums (7 × daily maximum) and the lowest published prebook rates. The airport publishes no weekly rate, and P6's table lists only the first 24 hours; we use the $35 daily maximum from the airport's parking FAQ.

| Option | 7 days drive-up | 7 days at the lowest prebook rate |
|---|---|---|
| P6 Economy Parking | **$245** (7 × $35) | $210 (7 × $30) |
| P4 Daily Parking | $420 (7 × $60) | $280 (7 × $40) |
| Terminal A Short-Term | $455 (7 × $65) | $350 (7 × $50) |
| Terminals B & C Short-Term | $490 (7 × $70) | $350 (7 × $50) |

The takeaway: For a week, P6 drive-up costs $245, which is $175 less than P4 and $245 less than Terminal B & C Short-Term parking.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives
- The airport names these authorized off-airport parking providers: Crowne Plaza Newark Airport (IHG), Hilton Newark Airport, Newark Airport Long Term Parking, The Parking Spot Haynes, Newark Liberty International Airport Marriott, Renaissance Newark Airport Hotel, (SNAP) Secure Newark Airport Parking, Victoria Parking, and Vista Parking.
- The airport advises parking only on-airport or with an authorized provider. Compare these final totals against the $245 drive-up cost for seven days at P6.

## Public transport

From the Port Authority's [AirTrain page](https://www.newarkairport.com/transportation/airtrain), checked 22 Nov 2026:

- **AirTrain** is free between terminals, parking, P3 hotel shuttles and rental cars. To or from **Newark Liberty International Airport Train Station** (NJ Transit and Amtrak) it costs **$8.75** one way, unless that's already included in your rail ticket. PATH connections pay a separate fare.
- Trains run every 3 to 5 minutes; from 11:00 p.m. to 5:00 a.m. ET, every 15 minutes.
- **Terminal A isn't directly on the AirTrain:** take the 5-minute shuttle bus or walk about 15 minutes along a covered pathway from the Terminal A station. From the train station, the airport estimates 7 minutes to Terminal C, 11 to Terminal B and 20 to Terminal A.
- For train fares into New York or around New Jersey, check [NJ Transit](https://www.njtransit.com/) or [Amtrak](https://www.amtrak.com/).

## Lounges at EWR by terminal

From the airport's [official lounge directory](https://www.newarkairport.com/dine-shop-relax/lounge-and-rest?filterGroups=Lounges) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/newark-liberty-international), checked 22 Nov 2026. Hours change often, so check with each lounge operator before you go.

### Terminal A
- **American Airlines Admirals Club**, near Gate A8
- **Delta Sky Club**, near Gate A8 (the airport says hours vary; check Delta)
- **United Club**, near Gate A27 (the directory also mentions a new United Club near Gate A26)

### Terminal B
- **Lounge & Co** (La Compagnie's business class lounge, also serving other airlines from Gates B50 to B57), near Gate B51
- **Lufthansa Senator / Business Lounge**, near Gate B68
- **SAS Lounge**, near Gate B60

### Terminal C
- **United Club**, near Gate C74
- **United Club**, near Gate C123 (30,000 sq ft, with shower suites)
- **United Polaris Lounge**, near Gate C120

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** eligible tickets, membership or status, under each airline's rules. The airport says the United Polaris Lounge is for long-haul international business class passengers, Star Alliance Gold members are invited into the Lufthansa Senator Lounge, the Lufthansa lounge sells access subject to availability, and Lounge & Co is generally open three hours before scheduled departure.
- **Priority Pass:** on 22 Nov 2026 it listed **no lounges** at EWR, only two experiences: Minute Suites (sleep pods) in Terminal C and Be Relax Spa (Gate 7) in Terminal A.
- **Bank-branded lounges:** the airport's directory lists none at EWR.
