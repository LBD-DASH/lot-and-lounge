## Official LAX parking rates

From [flylax.com/parking-at-lax](https://www.flylax.com/parking-at-lax), last checked 19 Nov 2026. LAX says these fees **include the 10% Los Angeles City Parking Occupancy Tax**, and it advises checking the rates posted at each entrance. Reserved parking is sold separately, and LAX says it can save up to 60% off drive-up rates.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Central Terminal Area garages, General (P1–P7) | 10 min free; $9 first hour ($11 in P3/P4), then $7 per 30 min ($9 in P3/P4) | **$60** | Across from the terminals |
| CTA Premium (P1, P3, P7 Arrivals level) | 10 min free; $11 first hour, then $9 per 30 min | $70 | Across from the terminals |
| CTA Valet (P1, P3, P4, P7) | 24-hour minimum | $75 | Across from the terminals |
| LAX Economy (6100 W. 94th St.) | $8 first hour, then $7 per 30 min | **$35** | Free shuttle, 24 hours |
| LAX Budget (5455 W. 111th St.) | $5 per hour | **$20** | Free shuttle, 24 hours, every 20–25 min; uncovered |

Height limits (per LAX): P1, P3 and P4 are 7'6"; P2A and P6 are 8'2"; P2B is 6'10"; P5 and P7 are 7'0". Economy Level 1 is 14'0" and Levels 2–4 are 8'1". EV charging in the CTA garages and Economy costs **$2 per session plus $0.45/kWh (Level 2) or $0.80/kWh (DC fast)**, on top of parking.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from official rates (7 × the daily maximum; transit fares are per person).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Metro park-and-ride (e.g. Aviation/LAX, $3/day) + Metro fare both ways ($1.75 × 2) | **$24.50** |
| Van Nuys FlyAway parking ($6/day) + FlyAway both ways ($12.75 × 2) | $67.50 |
| LAX Budget | $140 |
| LAX Economy | $245 |
| CTA garage, General | $420 |
| CTA Premium | $490 |

The takeaway: a solo traveller saves about $395 by using a Metro park-and-ride instead of a terminal garage for a week. Families should add the transit fare for each extra adult.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- **Metro park-and-ride ($3/day):** LA Metro lists daily parking at **Aviation/LAX** and **Hawthorne/Lennox** (C Line) and **Crenshaw/Expo** and **Fairview Heights** (K Line) at $3 a day. Norwalk is $3 a day or $60 a month. Then ride to the LAX/Metro Transit Center.
- **Van Nuys FlyAway:** parking is $1 an hour up to **$6 a day**, for FlyAway riders only, first come first served (reserved parking also available). The vehicle height limit is 8 feet.
- **Private off-airport lots:** many private lots near LAX sell daily parking with shuttles. Compare with the official Budget lot ($20) before you book, and check whether the price includes the 10% city parking tax and how often the shuttle runs.

## Public transport

- **LA Metro:** take the **C Line or K Line** (or Metro bus lines 102, 111, 117, 120 or 232) to the **LAX/Metro Transit Center**. A free, frequent shuttle runs from Bus Bays 1 and 2 to every terminal. The regular fare is **$1.75**, with free transfers within two hours.
- **FlyAway bus:** nonstop between each LAX terminal and **Union Station** or **Van Nuys**, **$12.75** one way. You can pay contactless on board or use the FlyAway app. At LAX, board on the Lower/Arrivals level at the blue FlyAway columns.

### How you get in
- **Airline and alliance lounges:** premium-cabin ticket, elite status or membership, under each airline's rules.
- **Priority Pass:** on 19 Nov 2026 it listed only the Virgin Atlantic Clubhouse (Terminal B, extra fee), plus Gameway (Terminals 3 and 6) and Be Relax spas. Don't count on a free Priority Pass lounge at LAX.
- **Centurion Lounge:** eligible American Express card members only. We don't recommend cards.
