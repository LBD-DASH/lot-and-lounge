## Official AUS parking rates
From [ABIA Parking](https://www.abiaparking.com/), last checked 25 Nov 2026. Rates are subject to change.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red Garage | $7 per hour | $35 | Closest to the Barbara Jordan Terminal, short walk; height limit 7 ft 8 in |
| Blue Garage | $5 per hour | $26 | Garage with real-time space guidance; height limit 8 ft 0 in |
| Economy Parking | $5 per hour | $14 | Complimentary shuttle service available 24/7, every 10 to 15 minutes |
| H Lot / Overflow Parking | Not listed | $14 | Used as overflow only; 24/7 shuttle, pick-up on the lower level across from baggage claim |

The airport's [rate advisory](https://www.flyaustin.com/news/advisory-economy-parking-rate-changes-austin-bergstrom-international-airport-go-effect-may-12) set Economy Parking at $14 per day and left Red and Blue Garage rates unchanged. Reservations can be made on the ABIA parking site.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from official rates (7 x daily maximum).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy Parking | 7 × $14 = $98 |
| Blue Garage | 7 × $26 = $182 |
| Red Garage | 7 × $35 = $245 |

The takeaway: a week in Economy Parking costs $84 less than the Blue Garage and $147 less than the Red Garage.

## Lounges at AUS by terminal
From [American Airlines](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travelInformation/airportAmenities/aus-club.html), [American Express](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/AUS/Delta-Sky-Club-Barbara-Jordan-Terminal-wUhN1d8UDd/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/austin-bergstrom-international), checked 25 Nov 2026.

### Barbara Jordan Terminal
- **American Airlines Admirals Club:** Inside security, across from Gate 22. Hours: daily 4:15 a.m. to 8:15 p.m.
- **Delta Sky Club:** Near Gate 4.
