## Official O'Hare parking rates

From the Chicago Department of Aviation's (CDA) [flychicago.com](https://www.flychicago.com/ohare/tofrom/parking/pages/economy.aspx) parking pages, last checked 19 Nov 2026. The CDA says **rates include all taxes**. You pay the cashier when you exit.

| Lot | 0–1 h | 1–2 h | 2–3 h | 3–4 h | 4–8 h | 8–24 h |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Main Garage, Level 1 (hourly) | $3 | $6 | $10 | $14 | $36 | **$79** |
| Main Garage Levels 2–6 (daily) and Lots B & C | $3 | $6 | $10 | $14 | $36 | **$43** |
| Terminal 5 Garage and Lot D | $3 | $6 | $10 | $14 | $36 | **$43** |
| Economy Lot F (covered, Multi-Modal Facility floors 4–5) | $3 | $6 | $10 | $14 | $30 | **$30** |
| Economy Lots G and H | $3 | $16 | $16 | $16 | $16 | **$16** |

**Clearances (per CDA):** main garage 6'6"; Terminal 5 garage 8'6"; Lot B 9'0"; Lot C 8'5"; Economy Lot F 8'4". If you need more height near the terminals, the CDA suggests Lots B and C.

**Reserved parking:** you can reserve a guaranteed covered space on Level 2 of the Terminal 5 garage, Level 4 of the main garage, or Level 4 of Economy Lot F. The **reservation fee is $10 per space per day**, charged on top of parking plus taxes and fees.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic: 7 × the official 24-hour maximum, taxes included.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy Lot G or H | **$112** |
| Economy Lot F (covered) | $210 |
| Economy Lot F reserved (+$10/day fee, before taxes and fees) | $280+ |
| Main garage daily / Lots B & C / Terminal 5 garage | $301 |
| Main garage Level 1 (hourly) | $553 |
| **Transit instead:** CTA Blue Line to O'Hare ($5) | $5 one way from O'Hare, plus the regular fare into the airport |

The takeaway: Economy G and H save $189 against the terminal garages for a week. Don't leave the car on the hourly level: it costs $441 more than Lot G for the same week.

## Getting from the economy lots to your terminal

- **Lots G and H** (Bessie Coleman Drive, at Mannheim and Zemke Road): take the shuttle bus to the **Multi-Modal Facility (MMF)**, then the free **Airport Transit System (ATS)** to every terminal. The CDA says the ATS and lot shuttles come every 5–10 minutes, 24 hours a day.
- **Lot F** is inside the MMF (10255 W. Zemke Blvd.), so you board the ATS directly. It has 24 Level 2 EV chargers on Level 4.

## Off-airport alternatives

Private off-airport lots around O'Hare and Rosemont sell daily parking with shuttles. Compare them with Lot G or H at $16 a day, tax included. Many off-airport quotes add taxes and fees at checkout, so compare the final total.

## Public transport

- **CTA Blue Line:** 24-hour service to downtown and the rest of the "L" network. The O'Hare station is on the **lower level of the parking garage**. From Terminals 1, 2 and 3, follow "Trains to City" through the basement-level tunnels. From **Terminal 5**, ride the ATS to Terminal 2 or 3 first. CTA charges **$5** for trains boarded at O'Hare. A 1-day Ventra pass ($5) or 3-day pass ($15) covers it at no extra charge.
- **Metra North Central Service:** weekday commuter trains from the O'Hare Transfer station behind the Multi-Modal Facility (reach it by ATS). You buy tickets on board, and the fare depends on the zone.
- **Pace buses 250 and 330** stop at the MMF (bus bays 8 and 9).

## Lounges at O'Hare by terminal

From the CDA's official lounge list and Priority Pass, checked 19 Nov 2026. The CDA says travellers without membership can buy day passes for most clubs, but airline policies change, so check with the operator.

### Terminal 1 (United)
- **United Club**, Gate B6
- **United Club**, Gate B18
- **United Club**, Gate C10
- **United Polaris Lounge**, Gate C18 (international first and business only)

### Terminal 2
- **United Club**, Gate E7
- **United Club**, Gate F9

### Terminal 3 (American)
- **Admirals Club**, Gate G8
- **Admirals Club**, Gate L2A
- **Admirals Club**, Gate H6/K6
- **American Flagship Lounge**, Gate H6/K6

### Terminal 5 (international)
- **Air France Lounge**, Gate M17. Priority Pass lists it.
- **Delta Sky Club**, Gate M13
- **LOT Business Lounge**, Gate M18 (Turkish Airlines, LOT and Star Alliance business-class travellers)
- **Wingtips Lounge**, near Gate M19. Priority Pass lists it, and the CDA lists more than 20 partner airlines that use it.

### How you get in
- **United, American and Delta clubs:** membership, eligible premium-cabin tickets or elite status, under each airline's rules.
- **Air France, LOT and Wingtips:** eligible tickets and status on the airlines they serve, or Priority Pass (Air France Lounge and Wingtips, per Priority Pass).
