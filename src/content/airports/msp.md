## Official MSP parking rates

From the Metropolitan Airports Commission's [Parking](https://www.mspairport.com/parking), [Quick Ride Ramp](https://www.mspairport.com/parking/quick-ride-ramp), [Value Parking](https://www.mspairport.com/parking/value-parking), [Daily Parking](https://www.mspairport.com/parking/daily-parking), and [Valet Parking](https://www.mspairport.com/parking/valet-parking) pages, last checked 23 Nov 2026. MSP labels every rate "Tax Excluded", so tax is added on top. Clearances are from MSP's [ground transportation](https://www.mspairport.com/directions/ground-transportation) and parking pages.

| Option | First hour | Each additional hour | Daily maximum | Clearance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quick Ride Ramp | $3 | Not listed | $19 | 9 feet 10 inches |
| Value Parking (Orange and Purple Ramps, T2) | $5 | $3 | $23 | 7 feet |
| Daily Parking (T1 Ramps) | $5 | $3 | $30 | 7 feet |
| Terminal 1 Hourly (Gold Ramp Level 3, under 4 hours) | $5 | $3 | $30 | 7 feet (9 feet 2 inches for oversized vehicles) |
| Terminal 2 Hourly (Orange and Purple Ramps) | $5 | $3 | $23 | 8 feet 2 inches |
| Valet at Terminal 1 | $10 | $3 | $55 | 7 feet |

**Getting to the terminal:** 
- **Quick Ride Ramp:** located on the MSP campus at 5315 Northwest Drive, St. Paul. Free 24/7 shuttle picks you up at your vehicle and drives you to Terminal 1 (Brown Ramp lobby near the T1 Ground Transport Center) or Terminal 2 (T2 Ground Transport Center, ground level of the Purple Ramp).
- **Value Parking:** Orange and Purple Ramps at Terminal 2, connects to Terminal 1 by free light rail. EV charging Level 7 Orange Ramp.
- **Daily Parking:** Terminal 1 Green, Gold, Blue, Red, Silver, Pink and Brown Ramps. Green and Gold connect by enclosed skyway on Level 3; the others via Level T (tram). EV charging Level 8 of Blue, Red and Silver Ramps.
- **Valet at Terminal 1:** no limit on length of stay; credit card only.

**Booking:** MSP says online rates are lower than drive-up, prebooking guarantees a spot, and there is free cancellation up to 12 hours before arrival. Holiday sale: save 15% when you prebook 5+ days at least seven days before entry, for entries before 2 Jan 2027. Online prices vary by lot, length of stay, dates and demand; taxes added at checkout. ePark Elite offers a reserved section next to the terminal for frequent flyers with no price quoted.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from MSP's daily maximum rate tables: seven days at the daily maximum, before tax.

| Option | 7 days (before tax) |
|---|---|
| Quick Ride Ramp | $133 (7 × $19) |
| Value Parking | $161 (7 × $23) |
| Daily Parking at Terminal 1 | $210 (7 × $30) |
| Valet at Terminal 1 | $385 (7 × $55) |

**The takeaway:** the Quick Ride Ramp saves $28 over Value Parking and $77 over the Terminal 1 ramps for a week, before tax.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- MSP doesn't name any authorized off-airport lots. Private lots exist; compare final totals including fees and tax.
- MSP's own Quick Ride Ramp ($19 daily maximum) is the airport's remote-lot option, with a shuttle every 20 minutes from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. and on-demand service outside those hours, per MSP.

## Public transport

From [MSP: Light Rail Transit](https://www.mspairport.com/directions-and-transportation/ground-transportation/light-rail-transit):

- **METRO Blue Line:** stops at Terminal 1 (station below the Red, Blue and Silver ramps, reached by tram from the Tram Level) and Terminal 2 (north side of the Orange Ramp). Full route every 15 to 20 minutes, about 5 a.m. to midnight daily; travel between the airport terminals is free and runs 24 hours. Mall of America about 12 minutes, downtown Minneapolis Warehouse District about 25 minutes. Tickets at station machines and the Metro Transit app; fares not listed on the airport page, check Metro Transit.
- **City bus:** Route 54 from the Transit Center, Level 1 of the Silver Ramp; no bus pickup at Terminal 2.
- **Intercity bus:** Jefferson Lines intercity buses also serve MSP.

## Lounges at MSP by terminal

From [MSP Airport Lounges](https://www.mspairport.com/airport/msp-airport-lounges) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/minneapolis-saint-paul-international), checked 23 Nov 2026. MSP says all seven lounges are in Terminal 1; none are listed at Terminal 2. Priority Pass on 23 Nov 2026 also listed The Chiroport at Gates G9, F7 and C12. Hours are as MSP lists them.

### Terminal 1
- **Escape Lounge**, above the entrance to Concourse E, 4 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily; access via Priority Pass, eligible cards, day pass. The [American Express lounge finder](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/MSP) showed a 9 p.m. close on 23 Nov 2026: sources differ, check with the lounge.
- **PGA Lounge**, between Concourses D and E, 5 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily; PGA membership, Priority Pass, select Mastercard benefits, day pass. Priority Pass lists it 08:00 to 20:00: sources differ.
- **Portal Lounge**, near the entrances to Concourses C and D, 4 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily; Priority Pass, eligible cards, day pass.
- **Delta Sky Club**, Concourse G near Gate G17, 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily (largest MSP Sky Club, near international gates).
- **Delta Sky Club**, Concourse C near Gate C12, 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily.
- **Delta Sky Club**, entrance to Concourse F, 4:45 a.m. to 10:15 p.m. daily.
- **United Club**, near Gate E6, 5 a.m. to 6 p.m., closes 5:15 p.m. Saturdays.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (Delta Sky Club, United Club):** club membership, eligible premium tickets or status, under each airline's rules.
- **Priority Pass:** on 23 Nov 2026 it listed Portal Lounge, Escape Lounges and PGA MSP Lounge (all Terminal 1), plus The Chiroport at Gates G9, F7 and C12.
- **Day passes:** MSP lists day passes for Escape, PGA and Portal lounges.
- **Bank-branded access:** set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
