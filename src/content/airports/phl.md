## Official PHL parking rates

From PHL's [Lots and Garages](https://www.phl.org/parking/garages), [Economy Parking](https://www.phl.org/parking/garages/economy-parking) and [Parking](https://www.phl.org/parking) pages, last checked 24 Nov 2026. On-airport parking is run by the Philadelphia Parking Authority.

**Garage C is currently closed for upgrades.** PHL says plan alternate parking and allow extra time.

Economy Lot (entrance 4400 Island Ave) is $18 daily flat rate up to 24 hours, taxes and fees included. It has 4,200 spaces, first come first served. Free shuttles run to all terminals 24 hours a day including holidays. It is patrolled by Philadelphia Police. No buses, trailers, semis or vehicles over 9,000 lb GVW. EV charging has three stations (6 vehicles) next to the toll plaza.

| Duration | Garage (upper levels) | Short-Term (ground level) |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 1/2 hr | $5 | $5 |
| 1 hr | $7 | $7 |
| 1.5 hr | $9 | $9 |
| 2 hr | $12 | $12 |
| 2.5 hr | $14 | $14 |
| 3 hr | $17 | $17 |
| 3.5 hr | $20 | $20 |
| 4 hr | $28 | $28 |
| Up to 24 hr | $32 | $54 |

Overnight parking is not permitted in Short-Term. PHL recommends Short-Term for visits up to 1 hour. The garage height limit is 6 ft 2 in on the rates page (the accessible-parking note says 6 ft, so sources differ on the same page). Vehicles must park with the license plate facing the drive aisle. PHL does not say whether garage rates include tax. Economy explicitly includes taxes and fees.

PHL Reserve online booking is at [parking.phl.org](https://parking.phl.org/book/PHL/Parking); no price is quoted. PHL Valet is curbside at all terminals, reserve online or drive up; no rate is published on the valet page.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

This is our arithmetic.

| Parking | Formula | 7-day total |
|---|---|---|
| Economy Lot | 7 × $18 | $126 |
| Garage | 7 × $32 | $224 |

The Economy total includes taxes and fees.

**The takeaway:** Economy saves $98 over the garage for a week.

## Public transport

Source: [PHL Public Transportation](https://www.phl.org/getting-around/public-transportation).

SEPTA Airport Regional Rail Line trains run every 30 minutes between Center City and the airport. The airport stations are Terminals E & F, Terminal C & D, Terminal B, and Terminal A. After that come Eastwick, Penn Medicine, William H. Gray III 30th Street, Suburban, Jefferson, and Temple University. Connections to Amtrak and NJ Transit Atlantic City Line are at 30th Street. Contactless tap-to-pay is accepted (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Amex). Fares are not listed on PHL's page, so check SEPTA. SEPTA buses Route 37, 108 and 115 stop at Zone 1 of the Arrivals Road outside baggage claim.

## Lounges at PHL by terminal

Sources: [PHL: Our Airlines](https://www.phl.org/about/airlines), [American Airlines: PHL clubs](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travelInformation/airportAmenities/phl-club.html), [American Express lounge finder](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/PHL), [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/philadelphia-international). Checked 24 Nov 2026. Hours are as American Airlines lists them. Others are not listed, so check with the operator.

### Terminal A-West
- Flagship Lounge and Admirals Club, mezzanine between Gates A15 and A16. Admirals Club daily 5 a.m. to 9:30 p.m., showers.
- British Airways Club near Gate A14.
- The Centurion Lounge (American Express lists it as temporarily closed).

### A/B connector
- Minute Suites (Priority Pass lists it under Terminal B).

### B/C connector
- Admirals Club, third floor, daily 4:30 a.m. to 9:30 p.m.

### C/D walkway
- United Club.

### D/E connector
- Delta Sky Club.

### Terminal F
- Admirals Club, level 2 above the food court, daily 6:30 a.m. to 9:30 p.m.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (Admirals Club, Flagship Lounge, British Airways, United Club, Delta Sky Club):** membership, eligible premium tickets or status, under each airline's rules.
- **Priority Pass:** on 24 Nov 2026 it listed only Minute Suites (Terminal B, sleep pod) and XpresSpa (Terminal B); no traditional lounges.
- **Bank-branded lounges:** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
