## Where to park at Haneda

Haneda has five official car parks. **P1 and P2** serve **Terminal 1**, **P3 and P4** serve **Terminal 2**, and **P5** serves **Terminal 3** (international). Free shuttle buses run between terminals. You can park for up to 14 days in P1–P4 and up to 20 days in P5. Beyond that, contact the car park's management company first.

The airport warns that on Fridays, weekends, holidays and in crowded periods the lots are busy from 05:30 to 20:00, and the wait for a space can be several hours. That can make you miss the security cut-off for your flight.

## Official Haneda parking fees

From Haneda Airport's **P1–P4** and **P5** fee charts (standard car), checked 20 Nov 2026. P1/P4 fees were revised on 1 Aug 2025, P2/P3 on 8 Aug 2025 and P5 on 24 Oct 2025.

| Time | P1–P4 | P5 |
|---|---|---|
| First 30 min | Free | Free |
| Up to 1 h | ¥200 | ¥100 |
| Then, up to 8 h | ¥200 per 30 min | ¥200 per 30 min |
| Cap, first 24 h (regular / crowded) | **¥2,800** / ¥3,400 | **¥2,800** / ¥3,400 |
| Days 2–3 | ¥150 per hour, capped at ¥2,800 (crowded ¥3,400) per 24 h | Same |
| Day 4 onward | ¥100 per hour, capped at **¥2,000** (crowded ¥2,400) per 24 h | Same |

- **Crowded periods:** Golden Week (24 Apr–6 May 2026), summer (10 Jul–30 Aug 2026) and New Year (**18 Dec 2026–3 Jan 2027**).
- **Reservations** cost extra on top of the metered fee: ¥1,000 for P2–P4 and ¥1,400 for P5. Private garage spaces are charged at double the standard fee plus a ¥2,000 (P4) or ¥2,800 (P5) reservation fee.
- Disabled drivers pay half the standard rate after the airport's procedure.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the official caps (any of P1–P5). Train fares are per person.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Keikyu, Terminal 3 ↔ Shinagawa, IC card, return (2 × ¥327) | **¥654** per person |
| Any lot, regular period (¥12,400 for 120 h + 2 × ¥2,000) | **¥16,400** |
| Any lot, crowded period (¥15,000 for 120 h + 2 × ¥2,400) | ¥19,800 |
| Reserved space, regular period (add ¥1,000 P2–P4 or ¥1,400 P5) | ¥17,400–¥17,800 |

The takeaway: the train is far cheaper, and in peak periods the lots may be full anyway. If you must drive, the per-day cap drops to ¥2,000 from day 4.

## Public transport: Keikyu and the Tokyo Monorail

Haneda is directly connected to the **Keikyu Line** and the **Tokyo Monorail**. Haneda's access pages also cover express buses and taxis.

- **Keikyu from Haneda Airport Terminal 3 to Shinagawa:** **¥330** with a paper ticket or **¥327** with an IC card (Suica, PASMO), from Keikyu's fare table for that station.
- For other stations and the Monorail, Haneda points you to each rail company's site for fares. We only list fares we could check on an official fare table.

## Lounges at Haneda by terminal

From Haneda Airport's official **airline lounge** and **card lounge** pages, checked 20 Nov 2026.

### Terminal 1 (domestic)
- **Japan Airlines (JAL)** lounges
- **POWER LOUNGE SOUTH**, 2F domestic gate area (south), 6:00–21:00
- **POWER LOUNGE NORTH**, 2F domestic gate area (north), 6:00–20:30
- **FOREST LOUNGE**, 2F, North Satellite, in front of Gate 26, 6:00–19:00
- **POWER LOUNGE CENTRAL**, 1F domestic arrival lobby, 6:00–20:00

### Terminal 2 (domestic and international)
- **All Nippon Airways (ANA)** lounges for domestic and international flights
- **Lounge Us**, 4F domestic gate area (north), 6:00–21:30, opened 1 Oct 2026, for domestic departures only
- **POWER LOUNGE NORTH**, 3F domestic gate area (north), 6:00–21:30, for domestic departures only
- **POWER LOUNGE CENTRAL**, 3F domestic departure lobby, 6:00–20:00, with paid shower rooms
- **POWER LOUNGE PREMIUM**, 3F **international** gate area, 6:30–25:30, 3-hour maximum
- The former Airport Lounge (South) closed on 30 Sep 2026.

### Terminal 3 (international)
- Airline lounges: **Japan Airlines (JAL)**, **All Nippon Airways (ANA)**, **Cathay Pacific** and **Delta Air Lines**
- **TIAT LOUNGE**, 4F international gate area, 24 hours, 3-hour maximum
- **SKY LOUNGE**, 4F international gate area, 24 hours, 3-hour maximum (soft drinks free; snacks and alcohol paid)
- **SKY LOUNGE SOUTH**, 3F international gate area, 24 hours, 3-hour maximum
- **The Centurion Lounge (AMEX)**, 4F international gate area, 8:00–22:00

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (JAL, ANA, Cathay Pacific, Delta):** eligible tickets or status, under each airline's rules.
- **Pay at the door, prices from Haneda's page (tax included):** the domestic POWER LOUNGE, FOREST LOUNGE and Lounge Us charge **¥1,320** per adult (¥660 for ages 4–12). TIAT LOUNGE and POWER LOUNGE PREMIUM charge **¥4,400** per adult (¥2,200 for ages 4–12). SKY LOUNGE charges **¥1,500** per adult (¥750 for ages 4–12). Children 0–3 get in free.
- **Lounge memberships:** Haneda lists Priority Pass at SKY LOUNGE SOUTH, and at TIAT LOUNGE only from 1:00 to 5:00 AM. Priority Pass users with children pay the adult rate for them. Haneda notes that, with some exceptions, cards issued overseas can't be used for free entry at the card lounges, so expect to pay at the door. We don't recommend cards.
