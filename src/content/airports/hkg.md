## Where to park at Hong Kong airport

The Airport Authority runs three public car parks for private cars: **Car Park 1** for short stays, and **Car Parks 3 and 4** for daily and long-term parking. All of them are automated. No ticket is issued, so you enter and pay with an **Octopus card, Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay or American Express** card. Taxis and light goods vehicles can't use Car Parks 1 and 4.

## Official HKG parking charges

From the airport's **parking charges** page, private cars, checked 20 Nov 2026. All rates are in Hong Kong dollars.

| | Car Park 1 | Car Park 3 | Car Park 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hourly | First hour HK$35, then HK$50 per hour | HK$32 per hour | HK$32 per hour |
| Daily | Not available | HK$256 | HK$256 |
| Long-term | Not available | First 3 days HK$470, then HK$256 a day | First 3 days HK$470, then HK$256 a day |

- Part of an hour is charged as a full hour.
- In Car Parks 3 and 4 you pay the hourly or the daily rate, whichever is less, in any 24-hour period.
- The long-term discount covers only the first 3 days. After that, each day costs the lower of the hourly or daily rate. The airport says the **3-day offer may be suspended** for operational needs, so watch for airport announcements.
- Holders of a Disabled Person's Parking Permit get 50% off.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the rates above. Airport Express fares are per person.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Airport Express Round Trip, Hong Kong Station (1 person) | **HK$215** |
| Airport Express Round Trip, Kowloon Station (1 person) | HK$195 |
| Airport Express Round Trip, Hong Kong Station (2 people) | HK$430 |
| Car Park 3 or 4 with the long-term offer (HK$470 + 4 × HK$256) | **HK$1,494** |
| Car Park 3 or 4 at the daily rate (7 × HK$256) | HK$1,792 |

The takeaway: for one or two people, the train costs a fraction of a week's parking. If you do drive, use Car Park 3 or 4 rather than Car Park 1.

## Public transport: Airport Express

The MTR **Airport Express** runs between Airport Station and Tsing Yi, Kowloon and Hong Kong stations. Adult fares from the MTR fare table:

- **Hong Kong Station:** HK$120 one way with Octopus, a contactless bank card or a QR code (HK$130 with an Airport Express QR code ticket). **Round Trip: HK$215**, valid 30 days.
- **Kowloon Station:** HK$105 one way (HK$115 QR ticket). Round Trip: HK$195.
- **Tsing Yi Station:** HK$73 one way (HK$80 QR ticket). Round Trip: HK$130.
- Children aged 3–11 pay half fare with Octopus or a QR code. Children under 3 travel free.
- **Same Day Return** is free on the way back if you use the same Octopus, bank card or QR code both ways on the same service day. MTR's conditions apply.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** eligible premium-cabin tickets or frequent-flyer status, under each airline's rules.
- **Plaza Premium and Kyra lounges:** a paid visit where the operator sells one, or a lounge membership the operator accepts. Check the operator's site for current prices.
- **Bank-branded lounges (Centurion):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
