## Official DFW parking rates

From DFW Airport's official [parking pages](https://www.dfwairport.com/park/remote/) (rates effective 1 October 2026), last checked 19 Nov 2026. DFW quotes two prices: one if you pay with an **NTTA TollTag**, and a higher one if you pay **by credit card at the exit**. No cash is accepted. DFW's FAQ says prebooked online rates include all fees and taxes; the drive-up tables don't say whether tax is included.

| Lot | 0–2 h | 2–4 h | 4–6 h | 6–24 h (TollTag / card) | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Remote North and South | $3 | $4 | $6 | **$14 / $16** | Non-stop shuttle bus to every terminal |
| Express North, uncovered | $4 | $5 | $8 | **$20 / $22** | Shuttle picks up at your car; 4–5 min ride to the terminals |
| Express North, covered | $4 | $5 | $8 | **$23 / $25** | As above |
| Terminal garages (A–E) | see note | $11 / $13 | $16 / $18 | **$37 / $40** | Walk to the terminal |
| Valet | – | – | – | **$55/day** | Drop-off at the terminal |

**Terminal garage short stays:** DFW's table lists $5 (TollTag) or $7 (card) for 1–2 hours, plus separate rows for stays under an hour. Check the [terminal parking page](https://www.dfwairport.com/park/terminal/) for those.

**Prebooking:** DFW says you can save up to 50% off TollTag rates when you prebook online, and prebooking doesn't need a TollTag. Prebooked prices vary by date, so check the booking engine for your trip. There are no booking, change or cancellation fees, and the maximum prebooked stay is 45 days. Prebooking isn't valid for valet.

**Good to know (per DFW):** parking is limited to 90 days, and vehicles left longer are towed off-airport. Terminal C's south garage section has been closed for construction since 14 Sep 2024. Garage clearances vary by terminal section, from 6'0" to 7'2", so check the FAQ if you drive a tall vehicle; Express and Remote attendants direct oversized vehicles. EV charging is available for valet customers in every terminal and in select spots in Terminals A and E.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic: 7 × the official 6–24 hour rate. Drive-up rates; prebooking may be cheaper.

| Option | 7 days with TollTag | 7 days paying by card |
|---|---|---|
| Remote North or South | **$98** (7 × $14) | **$112** (7 × $16) |
| Express uncovered | $140 (7 × $20) | $154 (7 × $22) |
| Express covered | $161 (7 × $23) | $175 (7 × $25) |
| Terminal garage | $259 (7 × $37) | $280 (7 × $40) |
| Valet | $385 (7 × $55) | $385 (7 × $55) |

The takeaway: Remote saves $168 against the terminal garage for a week if you pay by card ($161 with a TollTag).

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Private off-airport lots around DFW sell daily parking with shuttles. Compare their final total, after taxes and fees, against Remote at $14–$16 a day and DFW's own prebooked prices.
- DFW also runs two free **cell phone lots** for waiting to pick someone up.

## Public transport

From DFW's official public transit page, checked 19 Nov 2026:

- **DART Orange Line** from **Terminal A** to downtown Dallas.
- **DART Silver Line** from **Terminal B** to North Dallas.
- **TEXRail** (Trinity Metro) from **Terminal B** to downtown Fort Worth.
- **TRE LINK** bus to CentrePort/DFW Airport station for the Trinity Railway Express between Dallas and Fort Worth.

**Fares (DART):** a 3-Hour Local Pass is $3 and a Local Day Pass is $6. The $9 Regional Day Pass adds Trinity Metro (including TEXRail) and DCTA services. Check Trinity Metro for TEXRail-only fares.

## Lounges at DFW by terminal

From DFW's official [Travel Lounges directory](https://www.dfwairport.com/explore/lounges/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/dallas-fort-worth-international), checked 19 Nov 2026. All five terminals are connected inside security by **Skylink**, so you can use a lounge in a different terminal from your flight. We list no hours: check with each operator.

### Terminal A
- **Admirals Club**, Gate A24

### Terminal B
- **Admirals Club**, Gate B3

### Terminal C
- **Admirals Club**, Gate C20

### Terminal D
- **Admirals Club**, Gate D24
- **American Flagship Lounge**, Gate D23, with **Flagship First Dining** next door
- **American Express Centurion Lounge**, near Gate D12
- **Capital One Lounge**, near Gate D22
- **Chase Sapphire Lounge by The Club**, near Gate D25
- **The Club DFW**, near Gate D27. Priority Pass lists it.
- **Plaza Premium First**, near Gate D16 (DFW says Plaza Premium has lounges in Terminals D and E)
- **Air France Lounge**, Gate D16
- **Emirates Lounge**, Gate D16
- **Korean Air Lounge**, Gate D16
- **Lufthansa Lounge**, Gate D16
- **British Airways Lounge**, Gate D27
- **Qantas Business Lounge**, Gate D27

### Terminal E
- **Admirals Club**, Gate E22
- **Delta Sky Club**, Gate E10
- **United Club**, Gate E6
- **Plaza Premium Lounge**. Priority Pass lists it in Terminal E.

### Outside the terminals
- **PS DFW**, a private terminal near the Corporate Aviation facilities, with private or semi-private TSA and Customs processing and transport across the airfield to the aircraft.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (American, Delta, United, Air France, BA, Emirates, Korean Air, Lufthansa, Qantas):** membership, eligible premium-cabin tickets or elite status, under each airline's rules. DFW says to check with your airline.
- **Priority Pass:** on 19 Nov 2026 it listed The Club DFW (Terminal D) and the Plaza Premium Lounge (Terminal E), plus a dining offer at Drew Pearson's Sports 88 (Terminal E), Minute Suites (Terminal A) and Gameway (Terminals B and E).
- **Bank-branded lounges (Centurion, Capital One, Chase Sapphire):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
