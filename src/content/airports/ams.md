## Official AMS parking rates

From Schiphol's [parking rates without a reservation](https://www.schiphol.nl/en/parking/parking-rates-without-a-reservation/), last checked 20 Nov 2026. These are drive-up rates. Schiphol says costs without a reservation vary with parking duration, booking time and availability, and that booking in advance is often cheaper. The page doesn't mention VAT.

| Car park | 0–24 hours | 24–48 hours | After 48 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 Pick-up and Drop-off** (same prices as P1 Parking) | €2.70 per 20 min; **€59.50** per 24 h | €119.00 | 48-hour maximum, or €100 per additional day | Covered walk to departures, 2–6 minutes |
| **P3 Long Term Sheltered** | €59.50 | €110 | **€16** per additional day | Free 24/7 shuttle every 10 minutes (about 5 minutes) |
| **P3 Long Term Unsheltered** | €59.50 | €100 | **€16** per additional day | Free 24/7 shuttle every 10 minutes (about 5 minutes) |

Privium members have their own car parks with separate rates.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from Schiphol's drive-up rates.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| P3 Long Term Unsheltered (€100 + 5 × €16) | **€180** |
| P3 Long Term Sheltered (€110 + 5 × €16) | €190 |
| P1 without a reservation (€119 + 5 × €100) | €619 |

The takeaway: for a week, drive-up P3 costs €439 less than P1. For P1 beyond 48 hours, book.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Schiphol's own **P3 Long Term** is its long-stay option, with a free 24/7 shuttle.
- Private car parks and valet services operate around Schiphol. Compare final totals, including any fees, before booking.

## Public transport

From Schiphol and NS, checked 20 Nov 2026:

- The **NS train station** is directly below the terminal. Schiphol says trains run **eight times an hour** between Amsterdam Centraal and Schiphol, in about 17 minutes. NS gives about **16 minutes**.
- **Fare:** NS sells e-tickets between Amsterdam and Schiphol Airport **from €5.50** and charges a **€1.60 surcharge** on tickets that aren't e-tickets. You can also tap in and out with a contactless card or phone at the yellow card readers in Schiphol Plaza.
- The **Amsterdam Travel Ticket** includes unlimited travel on the Amsterdam Express bus (line 397 or Niteliner N97) from Schiphol.

## Lounges at AMS by area

From Schiphol's [lounges page](https://www.schiphol.nl/en/services/schiphol-lounges/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/netherlands/amsterdam-schiphol), checked 20 Nov 2026. At Schiphol, the "Lounges" (Lounge 1, 2, 3, 4) are the airside areas after security; the lounges below are inside them. We don't list opening hours.

**Which area is yours:** Departures 1 leads to Lounge 1 (European flights; Gates B, C and D59–87). Departures 2 and 3 lead to Lounges 2 and 3 (Gates D1–D57, E, F, G and H). M gates use Lounge 3 or 4.

### Lounge 1 (European flights)
- **Aspire Lounge 26**, second floor, after security
- **Privium ExpressLounge**, beginning of Pier D, second floor (Privium members)

### Lounge 2
- **KLM Crown Lounge 25**, second floor, opposite the D pier
- **KLM Crown Lounge 52**, opposite the F pier
- **Aspire Lounge 41**, top floor at the E pier

### Holland Boulevard
- **Privium ClubLounge West**, after security near Pier E, behind Dutch Bar & Kitchen (Privium members)

### Before security
- **Privium ClubLounge Departures**, between Departure Hall 1 and 2, behind check-in area 9 (Privium members)

### How you get in
- **KLM Crown Lounges:** eligible tickets or Flying Blue/SkyTeam status, under KLM's rules.
- **Privium lounges:** Privium membership.
- **Aspire Lounges:** a paid visit booked with the operator, or a membership the operator accepts.
- **Priority Pass:** on 20 Nov 2026 it listed Aspire Lounge (No.41) in Departure Hall 2 and Aspire Lounge (No.26) in Departure Hall 1.
