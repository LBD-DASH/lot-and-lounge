## Where to park at Changi

Changi's car parks sit at each terminal. Car Parks **2A/2B, 3A/3B and 4A/4B** serve Terminals 2, 3 and 4. The **T1/Jewel Car Park** sits under Jewel, with short-term parking on Levels **B2M and B2** and general parking on **B3 to B5**. The open-air **Hub & Spoke Car Park** sits between Terminal 2 and JetQuay. LTA's listing says its spaces are limited and first-come, first-served.

## Official Changi car park rates

From Changi Airport's **airport parking** page and the Changi Airport Group **list of fees and charges** (updated 1 April 2026), checked 20 Nov 2026. Rates include 9% GST.

| Car park | Rate for cars |
|---|---|
| Hub & Spoke (between T2 and JetQuay) | **S$0.60 per 15 min** |
| T2, T3 and T4 Car Parks A and B | S$0.65 per 15 min |
| T1/Jewel general parking (B3 to B5) | S$0.65 per 15 min |
| T1/Jewel short-term parking (B2M and B2) | S$0.65 per 15 min for the first 90 min, then **S$5 per 30 min** |

Neither Changi's page nor the fees list shows a daily cap, so each 15-minute block is charged. Don't use the T1/Jewel short-term levels for long stays: after the first 90 minutes they cost S$5 for every half hour.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the per-block rates above (7 days = 672 blocks of 15 minutes).

| Option | 24 hours | 7 days |
|---|---|---|
| Hub & Spoke | S$57.60 | **S$403.20** |
| T2, T3 or T4 car parks, or T1/Jewel B3 to B5 | S$62.40 | S$436.80 |

The takeaway: Changi has no long-stay discount, so a week in any official car park costs more than S$400. For most trips, getting dropped off or taking the MRT is far cheaper.

## Public transport: the MRT

Changi's official directions from **Changi Airport MRT Station (CG2)** are:

- **To the city:** take the train to **Tanah Merah (EW4)**, then change to the East West Line towards Tuas Link (EW33).
- **Alternative:** take the train to **Expo (CG1/DT35)**, then change to the Downtown Line towards Bukit Panjang.
- **Paying:** contactless Mastercard, Visa or NETS bank cards, mobile wallets, or a stored-value EZ-Link card. You can buy EZ-Link cards at Changi Recommends stores, the Passenger Service Centre or the TransitLink Ticket Office at the station.

MRT fares depend on distance, and Changi's page doesn't list them. Check the fare in the SimplyGo or MyTransport.SG app before you travel. Most MRT and bus services don't run overnight, but taxis and private-hire cars operate 24 hours from every terminal.

## Lounges at Changi by terminal

From Changi Airport's official lounge pages, checked 20 Nov 2026. All lounges are in the **transit area (after immigration)**.

### Terminal 1
- **Plaza Premium Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 24 hours. Pay-per-use.
- **SATS Premier Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 24 hours
- **Marhaba Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 24 hours
- **Qantas First Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 15:45–23:00 daily
- **Qantas Business Lounge**, Transit Level 3, hours vary by day (Changi lists them day by day)
- **British Airways Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 15:00–23:00 daily
- **Emirates Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 07:35–09:35, 12:40–14:40 and 17:00–00:40
- **Qatar Airways Premium Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 07:15–10:15 and 17:15–03:15 daily

### Terminal 2
- **Singapore Airlines SilverKris Lounge** and **KrisFlyer Gold Lounge**, Transit Level 3, both listed as open 24 hours
- **SATS Premier Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 24 hours
- **Ambassador Transit Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 24 hours. Pay-per-use.

### Terminal 3
- **Singapore Airlines SilverKris Lounge** (Business and First Class, with The Private Room for First) and **KrisFlyer Gold Lounge**, Transit Level 3, both listed as open 24 hours
- **SATS Premier Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 24 hours
- **Marhaba Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 24 hours
- **Ambassador Transit Lounge**, Transit Level 3, 24 hours. Pay-per-use.

### Terminal 4
- **Blossom – SATS & Plaza Premium Lounge**, Transit Level 2M, 24 hours. Pay-per-use.
- **Cathay Pacific Lounge**, Transit Level 2M, from 3 hours before the first Cathay Pacific departure until the last departure

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges** (Singapore Airlines, Qantas, British Airways, Emirates, Qatar Airways, Cathay Pacific): eligible tickets or frequent-flyer status, under each airline's rules.
- **SATS Premier and Marhaba:** Changi says entry depends on criteria set by the airlines that use them.
- **Pay-per-use lounges, adult walk-in rates on Changi's pages:** Plaza Premium (T1) is S$55 for 3 hours, S$90 for 5 hours and S$178 for 10 hours. Ambassador Transit (T2 and T3) and Blossom (T4) are S$55 for 3 hours, S$97 for 5 hours and S$195 for 10 hours. Children under 2 get in free. Plaza Premium and Blossom say local taxes and service charges may be added.
