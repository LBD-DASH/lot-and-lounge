## Official DUB parking rates

From Dublin Airport's [car park FAQs](https://www.dublinairport.com/car-parks/car-park-booking-faqs) and [long-term parking](https://www.dublinairport.com/car-parks/long-term-parking) page, last checked 20 Nov 2026. These are the flat rates without pre-booking. Dublin Airport says online rates vary by dates, times and how far ahead you book, and there's no booking fee. The pages don't mention VAT.

| Car park | Drive-up rate | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|
| **Holiday Blue** (long term) | **€20.00** per day | Shuttle every 15 minutes, about 15 minutes to T1 and T2 |
| **Express Red** (long term) | €25.00 per day | Shuttle every 10 minutes, about 5 minutes |
| **Short-term car parks** (T1 and T2) | €4.00 first hour, €6.00 each extra hour or part, **€50.00** per day maximum | A short walk to both terminals |
| **Terminal 2 Surface** (270 spaces) | Dublin Airport recommends booking; no drive-up rate listed | 5-minute walk to departures |

**Good to know (per Dublin Airport):**
- If online parking shows sold out, a limited number of drive-up spaces may still be available, with prices shown at the entrance, but it isn't guaranteed at very busy times.
- Shuttle pick-up at the terminals: T1 from Zones 8 and 9 of the Bus Park (behind Blocks A and B of the T1 multi-storey); T2 from Zone 21, opposite the ground-floor entrance to departures.
- Pre-booked customers can arrive up to 4 hours before their booked entry time, and the exit time is extended by 24 hours at no cost.
- There are 30 EV charging points across the car parks.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from Dublin Airport's drive-up rates (7 × daily rate).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Holiday Blue (7 × €20) | **€140** |
| Express Red (7 × €25) | €175 |
| Short-term car park (7 × €50) | €350 |

The takeaway: Holiday Blue saves €210 on a week compared with short-term parking, and pre-booking online may cut it further.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Dublin Airport's own **Holiday Blue** and **Express Red** car parks are its long-stay options.
- Private car parks operate near the airport. Compare final totals, including any fees, before booking.

## Public transport

From Dublin Bus, checked 20 Nov 2026:

- Dublin Bus stops in the **Transport Zones** outside Terminals 1 and 2.
- **Route 16** runs between the airport and Ballinteer on the south side, about every 10–15 minutes. **Route 41** runs between Swords and Lower Abbey Street in the city centre, **around the clock**. Routes 19 and 24 also serve the airport (to Merrion Square).
- **Fare:** Dublin Bus lists a single fare of **€2.00** with a Leap Card. Its airport page gives a standard cash fare of **€2.60**, paid in coins (drivers can't take notes or cards).
- Private coaches (Dublin Express, Aircoach) also run to the city centre; check their sites for fares.

## Lounges at DUB by terminal

From Dublin Airport's [lounge pages](https://www.dublinairport.com/enhance-your-journey/lounges) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/ireland/dublin), checked 20 Nov 2026. Dublin Airport runs all four lounges and publishes their hours. A visit is up to 2 hours.

### Terminal 1
- **The Phoenix Lounge**, 04:00–21:00 daily. Two entrances: right after Fast Track on the T1 mezzanine, and between the security lanes after central security. From €35 per person online, €45 at reception.

### Between Terminals 1 and 2 (after security)
- **The Liffey Lounge**, 04:00–21:00 daily, Level 1 on the corridor linking the terminals. From €37 online, €40 at reception. Priority Pass lists it.
- **The Martello Lounge**, 04:00–20:30 daily (may change with the season), in the same corridor. From €39 online, €55 at reception.

### Terminal 2, after U.S. Preclearance
- **The 51st & Green Lounge**, 07:15–17:00 daily, just past the U.S. departure gates. Open to all U.S.-bound passengers. From €45 online, €55 at reception.

### How you get in
- **Pay per visit:** book online up to 2 hours before entry (cheaper) or pay at reception.
- **The Airport Club:** Dublin Airport's membership for unlimited access to all its lounges.
- **Priority Pass:** on 20 Nov 2026 it listed the Liffey Lounge. Dublin Airport says access there for walk-ins, BA flights, and Priority Pass, DragonPass and similar card holders is subject to availability.
