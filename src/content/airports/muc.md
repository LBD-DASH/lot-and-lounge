## Official MUC parking rates

From Munich Airport's [parking fees 2026](https://www.munich-airport.com/_b/0000000000000041700356bb6a4769a5/2026-tarif-bersicht-parken-englisch.pdf) (standard rates, effective April 2026), last checked 20 Nov 2026. The sheet says these rates **don't apply to online reservations**, which have their own terms, and it doesn't mention VAT. Days are counted in 24-hour blocks from entry.

| Length of stay | Economy: P41, P43, P44, P50, P90 | Terminal: P1, P3, P4, P20 | Terminal: P5, P7, P26, P35 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 3 hours | €5.00 per hour | €4.00 per 30 minutes | €8.00 per hour |
| 1 day | **€34.00** | €44.00 | €44.00 |
| 2 days | €55.00 | €88.00 | €88.00 |
| 3 days | €66.00 | €132.00 | €132.00 |
| 5 days | €88.00 | €220.00 | €220.00 |
| 7 days | **€107.00** | **€250.00** | **€250.00** |
| 14 days | €160.00 | €337.00 | €337.00 |
| Each additional day after 15 | +€7.00 | +€12.00 | +€12.00 |

**Good to know (per Munich Airport):**
- **Online booking:** the sheet quotes "7 days of parking from 59.99 euros when booked online". The parking page also advertises a 25% discount with code P8-2026 on online bookings with entry dates from 1 September to 31 December 2026 (P8 and many other car parks). Bookings can be cancelled free until the booked entry.
- **Economy shuttle:** from P41, P43, P44, P50, P51, P90, P91 or P92, your parking ticket covers the **S1 and S8** between Visitors Park station and the terminals and **bus 635** between P41/P43 and Terminalstrasse Mitte (level 04), both every 10 minutes, for everyone travelling with you.
- In P50, vehicles over 2.10 m pay double. P20 has an XXL surcharge of €10 a day.
- **Curbside lanes at T1 and T2:** first and second entry free for 0–10 minutes, €3 for 11–20 minutes, then €3 per 10 minutes, up to €180 a day. The airport recommends at most 1 hour.
- From 2 November 2026 until about October 2027, bridge works on Terminalstrasse West and Mitte will cause traffic and pedestrian restrictions; allow extra time.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic is easy here: Munich Airport publishes 7-day prices.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Online booking (airport's "from" price) | from €59.99 |
| Economy Parking, standard rate | **€107** |
| Terminal Parking, standard rate | €250 |

The takeaway: Economy saves €143 on a week compared with Terminal Parking, and the S-Bahn or bus transfer is included in the parking ticket.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- The airport's own **Economy Parking** is its long-stay option, with free S-Bahn or bus transfers on the parking ticket.
- Private car parks operate around the airport. Compare final totals, including any shuttle and fees, before booking.

## Public transport

From Munich Airport and MVV, checked 20 Nov 2026:

- **S-Bahn S1 and S8** connect the airport with central Munich. S1 runs via the west of the city and S8 via the east; they alternate, so there's a train about every **10 minutes**, taking about **40 minutes** to the main station. The airport says the S8 runs 24/7 (every 40 minutes from about 1 a.m. to 4 a.m.).
- **Tickets:** MVV's **Airport-City-Day-Ticket** (unlimited journeys between the airport and the city centre on one day, for one person or groups of up to 5) is **from €17.50** on prices valid from 1 January 2026. Check MVV for single-trip fares.
- **Lufthansa Express Bus:** every 20 minutes from Munich Central Station via Munich North (Schwabing); 45 minutes from the central station.

## Lounges at MUC by terminal

From Munich Airport's [lounge directory](https://www.munich-airport.com/lounges-830529) and lounge pages, and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/germany/munich), checked 20 Nov 2026. We don't list opening hours; check each operator.

### Terminal 1
- **Airport Lounge World**, Gates A, level 04, behind security (run by the airport; from €72 online)
- **Emirates Lounge**, Gates A, level 04
- **Airport Lounge Europe**, Terminal 1 D, level 05, behind security (for Schengen-area flights only; €52 per person)
- **Air France KLM Lounge**, Terminal 1 D, level 05
- **VipWing** (Munich Airport VIP Services), Terminal 1 E, level 04

### Terminal 2
- **Lufthansa Lounges**, Gates G–L, levels 04 and 05, behind security
- **Bayern Lounge**, public pick-up and waiting area next to the arrivals exit, level 03

### How you get in
- **Lufthansa, Emirates and Air France KLM lounges:** eligible tickets or frequent-flyer status, under each airline's rules. Munich Airport says the Air France KLM Lounge is open to Business Class on Air France, KLM and SkyTeam partners, and SkyTeam Elite Plus members.
- **Airport Lounge World and Airport Lounge Europe:** the airport sells tickets (Lounge World online from €72 per person; Lounge Europe at €52 per person). Access is from at most 3 hours before departure.
- **Priority Pass:** on 20 Nov 2026 it listed Airport Lounge World (Terminal 1 Pier) and Airport Lounge Europe (Terminal 1).
