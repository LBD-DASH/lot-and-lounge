## Official BWI parking rates

From BWI's [Parking](https://bwiairport.com/to-from-bwi/parking/) page and its lot pages, last checked 25 Nov 2026. BWI says Long Term Parking is all-inclusive with no hidden fees. The other lot pages don't say whether tax is included.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Long Term Parking](https://bwiairport.com/to-from-bwi/parking/long-term-parking/) (Lots A and B) | $11 per day | $11 | Free shuttle, about every 10 to 15 minutes, 24/7 |
| [Express Parking](https://bwiairport.com/to-from-bwi/parking/express-parking/) | $14 per day | $14 | Free shuttle from 8 covered stops |
| [Daily Garage](https://bwiairport.com/to-from-bwi/parking/daily-garage/) | $16 per day | $16 | Shuttle, about every 10 to 15 minutes, 24/7 |
| [Hourly Garage](https://bwiairport.com/to-from-bwi/parking/hourly-garage/) | $6 per hour | $30 | Skywalks across from the terminal |

- **Long Term** has more than 10,000 spaces in two surface lots off MD 162 (Aviation Blvd), with covered shelters and real-time shuttle monitors. No oversized vehicles. On 25 Nov 2026 BWI's live status showed Lot A closed (full) and Lot B open.
- **Express** has about 1,400 spaces across from the Daily Garage. It is credit card only: swipe the same card to enter and exit. There is no ticket, and cash is not accepted.
- **Daily Garage** has about 8,400 covered spaces. Clearance is 8 ft 2 in, except Level 2 at 11 ft.
- **Hourly Garage** has nearly 5,000 covered spaces. Clearance is 6 ft 8 in on Levels 1 to 3 and 8 ft on Levels 4 to 6.

Lots and garages can fill and close. BWI then directs drivers to other lots at the posted rates. EV charging is allowed only at EV charging stations. Vehicles carrying flammable gas cylinders must use the Express or Long Term lots. BWI warns that parking shuttles can be delayed and recommends arriving 2 hours before departure.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

This is our arithmetic from BWI's daily rates.

| Parking | Formula | 7-day total |
|---|---|---|
| Long Term | 7 × $11 | $77 |
| Express | 7 × $14 | $98 |
| Daily Garage | 7 × $16 | $112 |
| Hourly Garage | 7 × $30 | $210 |

**The takeaway:** Long Term saves $133 over the Hourly Garage and $35 over the Daily Garage for a week.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

BWI has set curb pick-up zones for off-airport parking shuttles: Zone 2, and Zone 4, which is shared with hotels ([Off-Airport Courtesy Shuttles](https://bwiairport.com/to-from-bwi/transportation/shuttles/off-airport-courtesy-shuttles/)). BWI does not name the operators. Compare final totals including fees.

## Public transport

- **MTA Light Rail** ([BWI: MTA Light Rail](https://bwiairport.com/to-from-bwi/transportation/transit/mta-light-rail/), [MDOT MTA fares](https://www.mta.maryland.gov/regular-fares)): runs to downtown Baltimore, Timonium and Hunt Valley. The station is just outside the lower level of the terminal, next to Concourse E. The fare is $2.00 one way. For Penn Station, change at Mt. Royal Avenue on the same fare. BWI lists first arrivals and final departures as 4:45 a.m. to 12:40 a.m. Monday to Friday, 5:05 a.m. to 12:45 a.m. Saturday, and 10:35 a.m. to 8:40 p.m. Sundays and holidays.
- **MARC Penn Line and Amtrak** ([BWI: MARC](https://bwiairport.com/to-from-bwi/transportation/transit/mta-marc-train/), [BWI: Amtrak](https://bwiairport.com/to-from-bwi/transportation/transit/amtrak/)): trains stop at the BWI Marshall Rail Station. MARC's Penn Line serves Washington Union Station and Baltimore Penn Station. A free shuttle links the rail station and the terminal 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, about every 10 to 15 minutes, and every 25 minutes between 1 a.m. and 5 a.m. MARC fares are in MDOT MTA's Penn Line fare chart, so check [MARC Fares](https://www.mta.maryland.gov/marc-fares).

## Lounges at BWI by terminal

Sources: [BWI: The Club BWI](https://bwiairport.com/at-bwi/places-to-go/the-club-bwi/), [BWI: Places to Go](https://bwiairport.com/at-bwi/places-to-go/), [American Express lounge finder](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/BWI), [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/baltimore-washington-international-thurgood-marshall). Checked 25 Nov 2026. Hours aren't listed here, so check with the operator.

### Concourse D
- **The Club BWI**, near Gate 10. A shared-use lounge open to all travelers, whatever the airline or ticket class. Complimentary drinks (including alcohol), snacks and Wi-Fi. Seats 50.

### Concourse C
- **Minute Suites** (private rest suites, not a traditional lounge).

### Military travelers
- **USO International Gateway Lounge** for military personnel (BWI's Places to Go page).

BWI's pages list no airline-operated lounges.

### How you get in
- **The Club BWI:** buy a day pass online or at the lounge. BWI says it also welcomes Priority Pass, Lounge Key and Lounge Club members.
- **Priority Pass:** on 25 Nov 2026 it listed The Club BWI (Concourse D) and Minute Suites (Concourse C, rest).
- **Bank-branded lounges:** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
