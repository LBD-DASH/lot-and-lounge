## Official FRA parking rates

From Fraport's [parking price list](https://www.frankfurt-airport.com/content/dam/fraport-travel/airport/dokumente/anreise-und-parken/Parken/en/parking_prices-frankfurt-airport.pdf/_jcr_content/renditions/original.media_file.download_attachment.file/parking_prices-frankfurt-airport.pdf) (prices valid from 1 July 2026) and its [parking offers page](https://www.frankfurt-airport.com/en/transport-and-parking/parking-offers.html), last checked 20 Nov 2026. The price list doesn't mention VAT.

### Without pre-booking

| Car park | Hourly | Daily maximum | Up to 7 days | From day 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Terminal Parking**: Terminal 1 entrance P2/P3, Terminal 3 entrance P11 | €4 to 30 min, €7.50 to 60 min, then €7.50 per hour | **€43** | **€258** | €13 per day |
| **Business Parking**: Terminal 1 entrance P1/P4, level 1 (extra-wide 2.70 m spaces) | €4 to 30 min, €8.50 to 60 min, then €8.50 per hour | €49 | €294 | €18 per day |
| **eParking Terminal**: Terminal 1 entrance P2, level 14, row 1406 (electricity extra) | €4 to 30 min, €8.50 to 60 min, then €8.50 per hour | €49 | €294 | €18 per day |

### Online deals (from Fraport's examples; live prices depend on dates and availability)

| Deal | Example |
|---|---|
| **Holiday Parking** (Gateway Gardens car park or Terminal 2 underground car parks) | **from €64** for 7 days; from €95 for 14 days |
| **Terminal Parking** | from €99 for 7 days; from €178 for 14 days |
| Weekend Special | 2 days from €55 (Terminal Parking) or €66 (Business Parking) |

Online bookings are paid in advance, use a QR code at the barrier, and can be cancelled free up to 24 hours before the reserved start.

**Drop-off areas** (Terminal 1 P35/P39, Terminal 3 P61/P63): free up to 10 minutes (at most twice a day), €3.50 to 15 minutes, €6 to 30, €8.50 to 45, €17 to 60, then €17 per hour, with a 3-hour maximum. **Short-term parking** at Terminal 1 P31 runs up to €55 a day.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from Fraport's price list. Online prices are "from" examples, so your quote may be higher.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Holiday Parking, online (from) | **from €64** |
| Terminal Parking, online (from) | from €99 |
| Terminal Parking, drive-up (7-day cap) | €258 |
| Business Parking, drive-up (7-day cap) | €294 |

The takeaway: at the quoted starting price, booking Holiday Parking online saves €194 on a week compared with driving up to Terminal Parking.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Fraport's own **Holiday Parking** (online only) is its long-stay option.
- Private car parks operate around the airport. Compare final totals, including any shuttle and fees, before booking.

## Public transport

From Frankfurt Airport's train page, checked 20 Nov 2026:

- The **Regional Train Station** is on the underground level of **Terminal 1 (level 0)**, reachable from all Terminal 1 areas (A, B, C). Follow the signs for "Train stations S T".
- **S-Bahn S8 and S9** and regional trains **RB58, RE59, RE2 and RE3** connect the airport with Frankfurt, Offenbach, Hanau, Mainz, Wiesbaden and the wider RMV area. They use tracks 1–3.
- **Terminal 3** is linked to the station by the **SkyLine** train in about 10–15 minutes. From the station, follow the signs for "Terminal 3 G, H, J".
- A separate **Long-Distance Train Station** serves ICE and IC trains.
- **Fare:** regional trains use RMV tickets. Fraport's page doesn't list fares, so check RMV for the fare to your stop.

## Lounges at FRA by terminal

From Frankfurt Airport's [lounge directory](https://www.frankfurt-airport.com/en/at-the-airport/facilities-services/lounges.html) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/germany/frankfurt), checked 20 Nov 2026. We don't list opening hours; check each operator.

### Terminal 1
- **Lufthansa Business Lounges**: the directory lists them in Area A (two), Area B (two) and Area Z
- **Lufthansa First Class Lounges** in Area A and Area B, and the **Lufthansa First Class Terminal** (Area A)
- **Lufthansa Panorama Lounge A26** (Area A)
- **Lufthansa Bistro Lounge (Non-Schengen)** (Area C)
- **Air Canada Lounge** (Area B)
- **Lufthansa Welcome Lounge** (Area B, public area)
- **Luxx Lounge** (Area C; the directory lists it under the public area). Priority Pass lists it as LuxxLounge.
- **VIP-Services Frankfurt Airport** (Area A, public area)

### Terminal 3
- **Air France & KLM lounge**, Schengen area, Pier H (new). Priority Pass lists an Air France Lounge and a SkyTeam Lounge in Terminal 3.
- **Priority Lounge**, Area J, security area

### Outside the terminals
- **Airport Club Frankfurt** (FAC, public) and **DB Lounge** (The Squaire, public)

### How you get in
- **Lufthansa and other airline lounges:** eligible tickets or frequent-flyer status, under each airline's rules.
- **Priority Lounge and Luxx Lounge:** Priority Pass lists both and says pre-booking is available.
- **Priority Pass:** on 20 Nov 2026 it listed the Air France Lounge, SkyTeam Lounge and Priority Lounge (Terminal 3) and LuxxLounge (Terminal 1).
