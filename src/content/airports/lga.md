## Official LGA parking rates

From the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey [airport parking](https://www.laguardiaairport.com/transportation/airport-parking) page, last checked 23 Nov 2026. The page does not say whether rates include tax.

| Parking option | Drive-up rate | Prebook rate |
|---|---|---|
| Prepaid Economy Parking | No drive-up | As low as $40 per day |
| Terminal A Parking | $5 per 1/2 hour, $10 each additional half hour or fraction (marked over three hours stay), 24-hour maximum $75 | As low as $50 per day |
| Terminal B Parking (covered) | $5 per 1/2 hour, $10 each additional half hour, 24-hour maximum $80 | As low as $55 per day |
| Terminal C Parking (covered, access through Level 3 security checkpoint) | $6 per 1/2 hour, $12 each additional half hour, 24-hour maximum $89 | As low as $60 per day |

Booking at least 3 hours ahead saves money and guarantees a spot. You can enter up to 2 hours before the booking time. Premium reserved spaces are available in each lot on an advance-only basis with no cash accepted. Parking more than 30 days requires registering with ABM on arrival to prevent towing. There is free motorcycle parking with 10 spots near Terminal A, Parking P7. 

Payment methods include cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay, and E-ZPass Plus (except at Terminal A). EV charging is available with Level II in the Terminal B garage on Level 4 (4 ports) and the Terminal C garage, plus Level III Electrify America (12 ports) along Runway Drive. A disabled-plate courtesy rate offers a 24-hour maximum of $35 after a 4.5-hour minimum, not valid with prebook or E-ZPass.

The All Terminals Shuttle connects terminals, parking, rental cars, and pickup areas (source: [All Terminals Shuttle](https://www.laguardiaairport.com/transportation/all-terminals-shuttle)).

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic: 7 × the 24-hour drive-up maximum, and 7 × the lowest published prebook rate (actual prebook prices vary by date).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy Parking (prebook) | $280 (7 × $40) |
| Terminal A Parking (prebook) | $350 (7 × $50) |
| Terminal A Parking (drive-up) | $525 (7 × $75) |
| Terminal B Parking (prebook) | $385 (7 × $55) |
| Terminal B Parking (drive-up) | $560 (7 × $80) |
| Terminal C Parking (prebook) | $420 (7 × $60) |
| Terminal C Parking (drive-up) | $623 (7 × $89) |

**The takeaway:** Prebooking Economy parking saves $343 compared to a drive-up stay in the Terminal C garage for a week.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

The airport advises parking only on-airport or with an authorized off-airport provider, and names three approved providers: AirPark LaGuardia, Hyatt Place Flushing, and Sheraton LaGuardia East Hotel. No prices are given by the airport.

## Public transport

From [Public Transport](https://www.laguardiaairport.com/transportation/public-transport):

- **Q70 LaGuardia Link:** free, serves Terminals B and C, connecting to Jackson Heights-Roosevelt Ave and 74 St-Broadway subway hubs, and Woodside (LIRR).
- **M60 SBS:** serves Terminals A, B, and C, traveling to Astoria Blvd and 125th St in Harlem (with Metro-North at Harlem-125 St).
- **Q33:** serves Terminal A to Jackson Heights-Roosevelt Ave and 74 St-Broadway.
- **Q72:** serves Terminals B and C to Junction Blvd and 63 Dr-Rego Park.
- **Q90:** serves Flushing Main Street for the LIRR.
- **Fares and Payment:** pay with contactless cards, phones, or OMNY. LIRR and Metro-North CityTicket within NYC is $5.25 off-peak and $7.25 peak. Subway and bus fares are not stated; check the MTA.

## Lounges at LGA by terminal

From the airport [lounges directory](https://www.laguardiaairport.com/dine-shop-relax/lounge-and-rest?filterGroups=Lounges), [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/laguardia), and [American Express](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/LGA), checked 23 Nov 2026. The airport lists no hours (Delta Sky Club: check Delta.com), so check with each lounge.

### Terminal B
- **Air Canada Maple Leaf Lounge** (Gate 59)
- **American Airlines Admirals Club** (Gate 31)
- **The Centurion Lounge** (Food Hall)
- **Chase Sapphire Lounge by The Club** (Level 4, two floors, 16,200 sq ft)
- **United Club** (Gate 59)

### Terminal C
- **Delta Sky Club** (Sky Way); the airport instructs travelers to check Delta.com for hours.

### Terminal A
- No lounges listed.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** access is set by each airline's rules for eligible tickets, membership, or status.
- **Priority Pass:** on 23 Nov 2026, Priority Pass listed no lounge at LGA, only Be Relax Spa in Terminal B. The airport notes the Chase Sapphire Lounge admits certain Chase cardmembers who activated their Priority Pass membership.
- **Bank-branded lounges:** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
