## Official HNL parking rates
From [HNL Official Parking Rates](https://airports.hawaii.gov/hnl/getting-to-from/parking/), last checked 25 Nov 2026. All three garages use the same rates. Cash or a major credit card; no checks.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | 8+ hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terminal 1 Parking Garage | First 15 min free, 16-30 min $1, 31-60 min $3, 1-2 hrs $6, then up to $27 for 7-8 hrs | $29 | Adjacent to Terminal 1 |
| Terminal 2 Parking Garage | Same as Terminal 1 | $29 | Walkways on level 4 and at ground level to Terminal 2 |
| International Parking Garage | Same as Terminal 1 | $29 | Directly across from the International Arrivals Building |

HNL lists the 8+ hour rate rising to $30 from 1 July 2027. All lots are open 24 hours a day, and there is a 30-day parking limit in all lots. Motorcycle parking is $1.00 for two hours and $5.00 maximum per day via payment box, or $50.00 monthly. Drivers picking up arriving passengers can wait in the Cellular Telephone Waiting areas free for up to one hour.

Height limits: Terminal 1 garage 7 ft 0 in at all entrances; International garage 8 ft 0 in inside, 10 ft 0 in uncovered; Terminal 2 garage 9 ft 9 in at the ground-level entrance across from Baggage Claim 19, 6 ft 2 in at the second-level entrance by Lobby 5, and 6 ft 6 in at the Lobby 6 entrance.

EV charging stalls are on the ground level of the International Parking Structure: $0.44 per kWh from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., $0.57 per kWh from 5 p.m. to 9 a.m., plus an idling fee of $0.25 per 15 minutes once fully charged, paid through the OpConnect app.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from official rates (7 x the $29 8+ hour rate, assuming it applies to each 24-hour period).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Terminal 1, Terminal 2 or International garage | $203 (7 x $29) |

The takeaway: the three official garages cost the same, so pick the one closest to your terminal.

## Public transport
- **Skyline rail and TheBus:** both stop at Lelepaua Station, between the International Parking Garage and the Terminal 2 Parking Garage. Skyline runs 4:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. on weekdays and weekends (10:00 p.m. is the start of the last full trip), with trains every 10 minutes from 4:00 a.m. to 8:30 p.m. The W Line bus runs from the airport to Downtown Honolulu, Kakaako, Ala Moana Shopping Center and Waikiki.
- **Fares:** cash or a HOLO card on TheBus; a HOLO card is required for Skyline, sold at the Lelepaua Station ticket machine. Transfers between Skyline and TheBus are free for 2 hours from the first paid tap (not with cash).

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** access set by the operating airline, ticket class and status.
- **Priority Pass:** on 25 Nov 2026 it listed the Premier Club in Terminal 1 and the I.A.S.S Hawaii Lounge in Terminal 2.
- **Bank-branded lounges:** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
