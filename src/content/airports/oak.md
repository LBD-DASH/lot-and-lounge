## Official OAK parking rates
From [Oakland International Airport Parking Information](https://www.oaklandairport.com/park/book-parking/), last checked 25 Nov 2026. OAK says rates already include the City of Oakland Parking Tax.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Premier | $5 per 30 minutes | $40 (after 2 hours) | Directly in front of the terminals |
| Hourly | $4 per 30 minutes | $36 | Second closest lot to the terminals |
| Daily | $4 per 30 minutes | $26 (after 2 hours) | Protected walkways, a 5-8 minute flat walk to either terminal |
| Economy | $3 per 30 minutes | $18 (after 2 hours) | Free shuttle from 7 stops every 15-20 minutes; on demand 2:00-5:00 a.m. (510-563-3890) |

OAK offers reservation discounts when you book ahead; bookings must be made at least 2 hours in advance. OAK accepts cash, Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express, Diners Club and NFC payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay). Free EV charging is in front of Terminal 2 near sections G2, J2 and K2, plus 2 free EV chargers in Economy section M1.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from official drive-up rates (7 x daily maximum).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy Lot | $126 (7 x $18) |
| Daily Lot | $182 (7 x $26) |
| Hourly Lot | $252 (7 x $36) |
| Premier Lot | $280 (7 x $40) |

The takeaway: Choosing the Economy Lot instead of the Premier Lot saves you $154 over a 7-day trip.
