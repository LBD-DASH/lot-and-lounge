## Official MDW parking rates
From [FlyChicago Economy Parking](https://www.flychicago.com/midway/tofrom/parking/Pages/economy.aspx) and [Terminal Garage](https://www.flychicago.com/midway/tofrom/parking/Pages/maindaily.aspx), last checked 25 Nov 2026.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economy Parking (two locations) | $4 for 0-1 hour | $15 (1 to 24 hours) | Free shuttle every 10-15 minutes to the main terminal |
| Terminal Garage - Daily | $4 (0-1 h), $8 (1-2 h), $12 (2-3 h), $16 (3-4 h), $32 (4-8 h) | $43 (8 to 24 hours) | Adjacent to the terminal building |

Hourly parking on Level 1 of the Terminal Garage is meant for short stays. The [Daily Lot](https://www.flychicago.com/midway/tofrom/parking/Pages/daily.aspx) at 55th Street and Kilpatrick Avenue is temporarily closed.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from official rates (7 × daily maximum).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy Parking | 7 × $15 = $105 |
| Terminal Garage - Daily | 7 × $43 = $301 |

The takeaway: a week in Economy Parking costs $196 less than the Terminal Garage.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives
- **CTA Park & Ride, Midway Orange Line station** (4612 W. 59th St.): CTA lists $10.00 for 12 hours and no multi-day rate. Source: [CTA Midway station](https://www.transitchicago.com/station/mdw/).

## Public transport
- **CTA Orange Line:** Midway station (4612 W. 59th St.) connects to Chicago Midway International Airport. CTA buses #54B, #55, #55A, #59, #62H, #63 and #165 also serve the station. Fares are on the [CTA website](https://www.transitchicago.com/station/mdw/).

- **Airline lounges:** None are listed in the sources we checked. Check with your airline.
