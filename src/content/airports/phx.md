## Official PHX parking rates

From [Phoenix Sky Harbor: Parking Rates & Directions](https://www.skyharbor.com/parking/parking-rates-directions/), last checked 23 Nov 2026. Sky Harbor doesn't say whether these rates include tax.

| Lot | First hour | Each extra 30 minutes | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|-----|------------|-----------------------|------------------|--------------------------|
| West Economy Park & Walk (seasonal) | $6 | $3 | **$10** | about a 15-minute walk to Terminal 3; no shuttle |
| East Economy Lot (uncovered) | $6 | $3 | **$16** | PHX Sky Train to Terminals 3 and 4 |
| 24th Street Station | $6 | $3 | **$16** | PHX Sky Train station |
| West Economy Garage | $6 | $3 | **$19** | about a 12-minute walk to Terminal 3; no shuttle |
| East Economy Garages A & B | $6 | $3 | **$19** | PHX Sky Train to Terminals 3 and 4 |
| Terminal 3 Garage | $6 | $3 | **$33** | at Terminal 3 |
| Terminal 4 Garage | $6 | $3 | **$33** | at Terminal 4 |
| Premium Parking (Terminal 3 and 4 garages) | - | - | **$37** | closest available spaces, booked online |
| Valet Parking (Terminal 3 and 4 garages) | $18 first hour | - | **$43** | drop off at the terminal garage |

**Booking:** Sky Harbor says its best available rates are online at [onlineparking.skyharbor.aero](https://onlineparking.skyharbor.aero/PhoenixBooking/). It doesn't publish the booked prices on its rates page. A $2 non-refundable booking fee applies to each reservation. Reservations can be changed or cancelled up to one hour before the scheduled entry time (a changed booking is recharged with a new $2 fee and the original refunded).

**Good to know (per Sky Harbor):**
- PHX Sky Train trains run 24 hours a day and are free; between 5 a.m. and 10 p.m. waits average about 3.5 to 7 minutes; during nightly maintenance (10 p.m. to 5 a.m.) waits can be 15 minutes or more.
- A shuttle circulates the East Economy lot 24/7 to the Sky Train area.
- 24/7 Sky Valet is at Terminal 3 (first level near departures drop-off) and Terminal 4 (Level 4 by the elevator bays).
- Over-height and oversize vehicles: uncovered economy areas and the Oversized Vehicle area east of Terminal 4.
- Three free 24-hour cell phone waiting lots (East, West, and the 44th Street Sky Train station); drivers must stay with their vehicles.
- The West Economy Park & Walk is a seasonal lot, so check it is open before you count on the $10 rate.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the official 24-hour maximums (7 × daily maximum).

| Option | 7 days |
|--------|--------|
| West Economy Park & Walk (seasonal) | $70 (7 × $10) |
| East Economy Lot or 24th Street Station | $112 (7 × $16) |
| West Economy Garage or East Economy Garages A & B | $133 (7 × $19) |
| Terminal 3 or Terminal 4 Garage | $231 (7 × $33) |
| Premium Parking | $259 (7 × $37) |
| Valet | $301 (7 × $43) |

**The takeaway:** a week in the East Economy Lot or at 24th Street Station costs $119 less than the Terminal 3 or 4 garage, and the Sky Train is free.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Private off-airport lots exist around Sky Harbor. The airport doesn't list approved ones, so compare final totals including fees against $112 for a week at East Economy.
- Valley Metro light rail: ride the A Line to the 44th St/Washington station, then the free PHX Sky Train to the terminals. Check Valley Metro for station parking and fares.

## Public transport

- PHX Sky Train: free, 24 hours, connects the 44th St/Washington Valley Metro Rail station, East Economy parking, Terminals 3 and 4, 24th Street economy parking and the Rental Car Center.
- Valley Metro says buses on routes 1, 13, 44 and 70 connect to the airport. Valley Metro Rail ticket machines are on Level 2 of the 44th Street Sky Train station. Check [Valley Metro](https://valleymetro.org/how-to-ride/airports) for current fares.
- Between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m., Sky Harbor adds buses from the south side of the 44th Street station to Terminal 3 (outer curb by door 8) and Terminal 4 (Level 2 outside door 22).

## Lounges at PHX by terminal

From [Sky Harbor's lounge directory](https://www.skyharbor.com/at-the-airport/amenities/airport-lounges/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/phoenix-sky-harbor-international), checked 23 Nov 2026. Hours below are as the airport lists them and can change, so check with the lounge.

### Terminal 3
- **Delta Sky Club**, near Gate F8, 4:45 a.m. to 12:30 a.m.
- **Escape Lounges**, Level 4 after security where the E and F gates meet, 4:30 a.m. to 10 p.m.
- **United Club**, near Gate E3, 5 a.m. to 11:30 p.m.

### Terminal 4
- **American Airlines Admirals Club**, above Gates A7 and A9 (5 a.m. to 10 p.m.), between Gates A19 and A21 (6 a.m. to 8 p.m.), and above Gates B5 and B7 (6 a.m. to 8 p.m.)
- **The Centurion Lounge**, across from Gate B21, 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.
- **Escape Lounges**, across from Gate B22, 5 a.m. to 10 p.m.
- **Sapphire Lounge by The Club**, near Gate D11, 4:30 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. daily
- **USO** (military members and families), before security on the east end of Level 2, 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. daily

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** eligible tickets, membership or status, under each airline's rules.
- **Escape Lounges:** Escape Lounges lists both PHX lounges at a $60 walk-up price, or from $45 per person booked ahead ([Escape Lounges: Phoenix](https://escapelounges.com/us/airport-lounges/phoenix/)).
- **Priority Pass:** on 23 Nov 2026 it listed one PHX lounge, Escape Lounges in Terminal 3. Sky Harbor also says the Terminal 3 Escape Lounge accepts Priority Pass (Terminal 3 only).
- **Bank-branded lounges (Centurion, Sapphire):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
