## Official CPH parking rates

From Copenhagen Airport's [prices and products](https://www.cph.dk/en/parking-transport/prices-products) page, last checked 20 Nov 2026. Online prices are "from" prices that change with your dates. The page doesn't mention VAT.

| Category | Car parks | Online booking (from) | Without a booking | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | P19, P17, P15 | **499**, 549, 599 DKK per week | Online only (minimum 4 days) | Terminal bus (1.3–2.1 km) |
| **Standard** | P11, P12, P1 | 899 DKK per week (P1: 1,199) | Online only | About 500 m to 1 km walk |
| **Standard+** | P3, P5, P9, P10 | 1,409 DKK per week | 70 DKK per hour, **325 DKK** per day, **1,625 DKK** per week | About 300–500 m walk |
| **Direct** | P4, P6, P7b, P8 | 1,759 DKK per week | 70 DKK per hour, **375 DKK** per day, **2,250 DKK** per week | About 100–170 m walk |

Without a booking, the first 15 minutes are free and the day rate applies per commenced day. Two weeks without a booking costs 3,305 DKK in Standard+ and 4,210 DKK in Direct.

**Good to know (per Copenhagen Airport):**
- In Budget, if you overstay your booking, the drive-in day rate of **250 DKK** applies.
- You can always drive in and park without a booking, even when the website shows sold out, but the airport recommends booking in peak weeks.
- The drive-up rates above don't apply to the short-term areas in P4 and P8, which have their own rates (0–15 minutes free).
- Direct has height limits of 2.05–2.15 m; P11 and the Budget car parks have no height limit.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Copenhagen Airport publishes weekly prices, so no arithmetic needed.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Budget P19, online (from) | **499 DKK** |
| Standard P11/P12, online (from) | 899 DKK |
| Standard+, no booking | 1,625 DKK |
| Direct, no booking | 2,250 DKK |

The takeaway: booking Budget online can save over 1,100 DKK on a week compared with driving into Standard+.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Copenhagen Airport's **Budget** car parks are its own long-stay option, with a terminal bus.
- Private car parks operate near the airport. Compare final totals, including any fees, before booking.

## Public transport

From the Copenhagen Metro, checked 20 Nov 2026:

- The **metro station is at Terminal 3**, a short walk from arrivals. Take the **M2 (yellow line) towards Vanløse** for Christianshavn, Kongens Nytorv and Nørreport.
- **Fare:** the Metro says a ticket between the city centre and the airport costs **30 kr**, valid for 1 hour 30 minutes; youths 12–15 pay half and an adult can bring two children under 12 free. Buy it in the Rejsebillet app or from the machine before you board (there are no barriers; the fine is DKK 750).
- The metro runs **24/7**; at night (about midnight to 5 a.m.) the M2 leaves every 15–20 minutes.
- **Trains** to Copenhagen Central Station leave from the airport station, also at Terminal 3.

## Lounges at CPH by gate area

From Copenhagen Airport's lounge pages and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/denmark/copenhagen-kastrup), checked 20 Nov 2026. All are after security. The airport's lounge overview page wasn't loading today, so check each lounge's own page before you go. We don't list opening hours, which the airport says vary through the year.

### Gates A and B (Schengen)
- **Aspire Lounge**, first floor near gates A. Walk-in access is DKK 339 per person.
- **Carlsberg Aviator Lounge**, first floor between gates A and B. Paid access DKK 229 for up to 3 hours, if there's space.
- **Danske Bank Aviator Business Lounge**, next to the Carlsberg Aviator Lounge. Paid access DKK 289 for up to 3 hours, if there's space.

### Gates C
- **SAS Lounge**, after security at gate C, opposite the entrance to passport control. Paid access 349 DKK, if there's space.
- **Eventyr Lounge**, after passport control towards gates C26–C28, for flights outside the Schengen area only. Access DKK 300 per person. (Priority Pass still lists it as Pearl Lounge.)

### How you get in
- **SAS Lounge:** SAS Business and SAS Premium (domestic) passengers, EuroBonus Gold and Diamond, and SkyTeam Elite Plus; SAS Economy and other SkyTeam passengers can buy access if there's space.
- **Aspire, Carlsberg Aviator and Eventyr lounges:** any passenger can buy access at the entrance; some airlines' passengers get in free.
- **Priority Pass:** on 20 Nov 2026 it listed Aspire Lounge and Carlsberg Aviator Lounge (Terminal 2) and Pearl Lounge (Terminal 3). The Carlsberg and Eventyr pages say cardholder places are limited, so you may be turned away at busy times.
