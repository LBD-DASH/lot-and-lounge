## Official FLL parking rates

From the Broward County Aviation Department [FLL Parking](https://www.broward.org/airport/Passengers/Parking) page, last checked 23 Nov 2026. The airport page does not state whether these rates include tax.

| Parking option | Rate | Location and details |
|---|---|---|
| Long-Term | $2 per 20-minute interval, maximum $20 per day | Palm Garage levels 2-4, Hibiscus Garage levels 3-7, Cypress Garage levels 6-9. |
| Short-Term | $2 per 20-minute interval, maximum $36 per day | Hibiscus Garage level 2 only, Palm Garage level 1. |
| Curbside Valet | $30 per day (no proration) | Departures (upper) level roadway, west end of Terminal 1 and west end of Terminal 4. |
| Overflow Lot | Flat $10 per day | 2755 SW 42nd Street. Opens only at peak periods when garages are full. Closes once garages have space. |

The parking complex features over 11,000 spaces. During peak periods, valet and self-parking may temporarily close when full. You can check real-time availability using the Space Finder green lights in the Palm and Hibiscus garages or via the FLL Airport app. 

Vehicle clearance limits apply: Hibiscus level 1 is 10 feet; Hibiscus levels 2-7, Palm levels 1-4, and Cypress levels 6-9 are 7 feet 3 inches. Vehicles over 10 feet cannot be accommodated.

Payment for the Overflow lot is due at exit, with credit cards preferred. A free shuttle runs from the Overflow lot to designated stops on the upper-level roadway between Terminals 1 and 2 and between Terminals 3 and 4.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic based on 7 times the daily maximum rates:

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Long-Term | $140 (7 × $20) |
| Short-Term | $252 (7 × $36) |
| Curbside Valet | $210 (7 × $30) |
| Overflow Lot | $70 (7 × $10, only if open) |

**The takeaway:** Long-Term saves $112 over Short-Term for a week. The $10 Overflow lot opens only when the garages are full, so you can't plan on it.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- The airport page doesn't name any authorized off-airport lots. Private lots exist; compare final totals including all fees before booking.
- The Overflow Lot (above) is the airport's own remote lot, used only at peak times.

## Public transport

From the Broward County Aviation Department [FLL Transportation](https://www.broward.org/airport/Passengers/Transportation) page:

- **Broward County Transit:** Route 1 stops at the Rental Car Center, Stop G, with daily schedules. Fares are not stated on the airport page; check the operator.
- **Tri-Rail:** Free shuttle buses run from the terminals to the Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport Station at Dania Beach, located a few miles away. Shuttle stops are on the lower level (Arrivals) at GTA-1 (west end of Terminal 1), GTA-2 (between Terminals 2 and 3), and GTA-3 (between Terminals 3 and 4).
- **Brightline:** An airport connector shuttle runs Monday through Sunday from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. from GTA-1, GTA-2, and GTA-3 at a flat per-person one-way rate (amount not stated).
- **Taxis:** Estimated fares from the airport page (estimates, may vary): Port Everglades $18-$21, Port of Miami $86, Miami International Airport $72.
