## Official SAN parking rates
From [SAN Official Parking Information](https://www.san.org/before-you-go-travel-tips/parking-information/), last checked 25 Nov 2026. Rates are clearly posted at each parking lot. Both payment areas accept cash and major credit cards (VISA, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, and Diner's Club). Lost tickets are charged at the maximum daily rate.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terminal 1 Parking Plaza | $3 per every 15 minutes | $40 | Directly across from Terminal 1 |
| Terminal 2 Parking Plaza | $3 per every 15 minutes | $40 | Directly across from Terminal 2 |
| Valet Parking | $60 per day | $60 | Curbside service at Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 |

SAN's parking page says reservations are available up to 30 days ahead; check the airport's [booking page](https://reservations.san.org/SanDiegoBooking/?fresh_start=y) for current windows. In the Terminal 2 Parking Plaza, the height restriction is 8 feet 2 inches. In the Terminal 1 Parking Plaza, the height restriction is 9 feet 6 inches on level 1 and 8 feet 4 inches on levels 2 through 5. Clean vehicle charging points are available with a per-session fee of $1.00 and usage at $0.55 per kWh.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from official rates (7 × daily maximum).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 Parking Plaza | 7 × $40 = $280 |
| Valet Parking | 7 × $60 = $420 |

The takeaway: Choosing the standard Terminal Parking Plazas instead of valet parking saves you $140 over a 7-day trip.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives
- Compare final totals across authorized local options, or contact SP+ Parking at 619-291-2087 to check lot status before driving to the airport.

## Public transport
- **Airport Flyer #992:** Operates in front of each terminal. Request a Regional Day Pass for $5 for free transfers, exit at Broadway and Kettner, and take the Blue Line Trolley toward San Ysidro from American Plaza Transit Center.

## Lounges at SAN by terminal
From [SAN Lounges Guide](https://www.san.org/news-articles/lounges-at-san-your-pre-flight-escape/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/san-diego-international), checked 25 Nov 2026.

### Terminal 1
SAN's lounge guide lists no lounges in Terminal 1. It says all four of SAN's lounges are in Terminal 2.

### Terminal 2
- **Aspire Lounge:** Located between Gates 23 and 33. Hours: 05:00 - 22:00.
- **Chase Sapphire Lounge by The Club:** Located between Gates 46 and 47.
- **Delta Sky Club:** Located on the Mezzanine.
- **United Club:** Terminal 2 (SAN's guide gives no gate).
- **PGA Tour Grill:** Hours: 07:00 - 21:00 (Priority Pass restaurant voucher).
- **Be Relax Spa:** Hours: 06:00 - 21:00 (Priority Pass spa experience).

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** Access is available through airline programs such as Alaska Lounge+, Delta Sky Club memberships, United Club memberships, eligible tickets, and qualifying credentials. Check individual airlines for current access policies.
- **Priority Pass:** Access is available at the Aspire Lounge (pre-booking available), along with participating options like PGA Tour Grill and Be Relax Spa.
- **Bank-branded lounges:** Access to the Chase Sapphire Lounge by The Club is available to eligible Chase cardholders. We do not recommend cards.
