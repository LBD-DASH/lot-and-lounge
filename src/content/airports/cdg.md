## Official CDG parking rates

From Paris Aéroport's [CDG parking tariff page](https://www.extime.com/fr/paris/parking/aeroport-cdg/tarifs) (rates without a booking, in euros), last checked 20 Nov 2026. The page doesn't say whether VAT is included, except for EV charging (€0.42 per kWh including VAT).

| Car parks | First day | Longer stays | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Economy: PR ECO, PX ECO** (PW ECO is online booking only) | **€50** | +€25 a day; **€175** for 6 days; then +€20 a day; €255 for 10 days; then +€15 a day; €555 for 30 days | CDGVal and free shuttles (PR ECO: CDGVal every 15 minutes) |
| **Close to the terminals: P1, PAB, PEF, P3, PG** | €60 | €95 for 2 days; +€35 a day; **€270** for 7 days; then +€30 a day | P1: lift to Terminal 1. PAB: 2A/2B. PEF: lift to 2E/2F. PG: 5-minute walk to 2G. P3: a few minutes' walk to Terminal 3 |
| **Close, renovated: PCD** (for 2C and 2D) | €62 | €97 for 2 days; +€35 a day; **€272** for 7 days; then +€30 a day | Next to 2C/2D |

Short stays in the close car parks start at €5.00 for 10–15 minutes (€5.50 in PCD), free under 10 minutes. The economy car parks start at €4.00 for 10–15 minutes.

**Good to know (per Paris Aéroport):**
- Booking online is offered at better prices than the drive-up grid, with free cancellation up to 6 hours before your planned entry.
- EV charging (P1, PAB, PG, PCD E-Park and PW) costs €1 to access plus €0.42 per kWh, on top of the parking charge.
- Motorbikes park in P1, PAB and PEF: €28 a day, €111 for 7 days. Motorbike spaces can't be booked.
- **Drop-off:** free under 10 minutes, then €1 per minute: €15 for 15 minutes, €20 for 30 minutes, €50 for an hour, €80 for the 90-minute maximum.
- Air France says CDG's terminals and gates change names from 16 March 2027, so check your departure terminal.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the tariff above (no booking).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy PR ECO / PX ECO (€175 for 6 days + €20) | **€195** |
| Close car parks P1, PAB, PEF, P3, PG | €270 |
| PCD (renovated) | €272 |

The takeaway: economy parking saves €75 on a week compared with the car parks next to the terminals, and an online booking may cut it further.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Paris Aéroport's own economy car parks (above) are the official long-stay option.
- Private car parks and valet services operate around Roissy. Compare final totals, including any shuttle and fees, before booking.

## Public transport

From Île-de-France Mobilités, checked 20 Nov 2026:

- **RER B** links Charles de Gaulle with central Paris.
- The **Paris Region <> Airports ticket** costs **€14** per trip at full price and **€7** reduced, valid across all zones, on rates applicable from 1 January 2026. You can load it on the app.

## Lounges at CDG by terminal

From Paris Aéroport's [Extime Lounge](https://www.extime.com/fr/paris/services/extime-lounge) page, [Air France](https://wwws.airfrance.fr/en/information/prepare/salons) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/france/paris-charles-de-gaulle), checked 20 Nov 2026. CDG has many more airline and alliance lounges than we can verify here; check your airline's lounge page. We don't list opening hours.

### Terminal 1
- **Extime Lounge** (Paris Aéroport's official lounge; adult access at CDG1 from €95)

### Terminals 2B and 2D
- **Extime Lounge** (official lounge)

### Terminal 2E
- **Air France lounges** in **Hall K**, **Hall L** and **Hall M**
- **YotelAir Paris Charles de Gaulle** (Priority Pass lists it)

### Terminal 2F
- **Air France lounge**

### Terminal 2G
- **Air France lounge**

Air France also lists a **La Première** lounge at Charles de Gaulle; it doesn't give the terminal on its overview page.

### How you get in
- **Air France lounges:** included with eligible tickets or Flying Blue/SkyTeam status. Air France also sells a paid **Lounge option** at some airports, subject to availability, from 3 hours before departure.
- **Extime Lounge:** book access online or pay at the lounge desk. Extime says its online rate is up to 15% below the walk-up price for Extime Rewards members.
- **Priority Pass:** on 20 Nov 2026 it listed only YotelAir Paris Charles de Gaulle (Terminal 2E).
