## Official MSY parking rates
From [flyMSY Parking](https://flymsy.com/parking), last checked 25 Nov 2026. MSY doesn't say whether rates include tax.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per day | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economy Garage | $4 first half hour, then $3 per half hour | $12 | MSY's parking page doesn't say; check the facility map |
| Surface Lot | First half hour free, then $3 per half hour | $20 | Showed as FULL when we checked on 25 Nov 2026 |
| Long Term | $4 first half hour, then $3 per half hour | $22 | MSY's parking page doesn't say; check the facility map |
| Short Term | First half hour free, then $3 per half hour | $26 | MSY's parking page doesn't say; check the facility map |
| Valet | $36 per day | $36 | Available 6 am - 12 am |

Parking reservations are available for all parking facilities. Reserved parking is non-refundable if canceled, changes can't be made less than 24 hours before the reserved time, and you must enter the facility within two hours of your reservation time. Your confirmation email has a QR code to scan at entry and exit; don't pull a ticket, or you will be charged again. MSY parking accepts credit cards only.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

| Option | 7 days |

The takeaway: Choosing the Economy Garage instead of valet saves you $168 over a 7-day trip.
