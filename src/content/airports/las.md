## Official LAS parking rates

From [Harry Reid's official parking page](https://www.harryreidairport.com/parking/), last checked 19 Nov 2026. The page doesn't say whether tax is included. Its FAQ still mentions new rates taking effect on 1 July 2026, so the tables below are what the page showed on the check date.

| Lot | First hours | Daily maximum | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economy | $6 for 0–2 h, then $2 per hour | **$14** | Short walk to the terminals (uncovered) |
| Remote | $6 for 0–2 h, then $2 per hour | **$14** | Free shuttles to and from the terminals |
| Long Term (Terminal 1 and Terminal 3) | $12 for 0–2 h, then $4 per hour | **$20** | Walk; pre-booked parking is here |
| Short Term garages | $4 for 0–1 h, $8 for 1–2 h, then $5 per hour | **$38** | Garage at each terminal |
| Valet | – | **$34 per day** | Valet service operated by ABM Aviation, 702-261-6999 |

**Reservations (per the airport):** Terminal 1 $30, Terminal 3 $23. Discounted Terminal 3 Long Term parking is $16 for Saturday to Wednesday and $20 for Thursday and Friday; it requires a reservation, availability is limited, and drive-up customers pay posted rates. The page doesn't state whether reservation prices are per day.

**Good to know (per the airport):** maximum stay is 30 days; call 702-261-5122 for longer. Garage clearance is 8'2" at Terminal 1 and 9'4" at Terminal 3. Payment: cash, debit and credit cards, and contactless wallets. Free jump starts, tire inflation and lost-car searches are available on 702-261-5122.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic: 7 × the published daily maximum. Drive-up rates.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy or Remote | **$98** (7 × $14) |
| Long Term | $140 (7 × $20) |
| Valet | $238 (7 × $34) |
| Short Term garages | $266 (7 × $38) |

The takeaway: Economy or Remote saves $168 against the Short Term garages for a week, and $140 against valet.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Private off-airport lots around Las Vegas sell daily parking with shuttles. Compare their final total, after taxes and fees, against Economy at $14 a day. The airport's own page names no authorized operators.
- The airport lists a cell phone lot near each terminal's passenger pickup.

## Public transport

From RTC's airport and fares pages, checked 19 Nov 2026:

- **Route 108** and the **RED LINE** (which replaced Route 109 on 23 Aug 2026, per RTC) stop at **Terminal 1, Ground Level Zero**. RTC says the RED LINE reaches the South Strip Transit Terminal in about 12–15 minutes.
- **Terminal 3** has no stop in RTC's listing. Use the airport's free inter-terminal shuttle from Terminal 1.
- **Tickets:** a vending machine at the Level Zero bus stop takes cards or exact cash, or you can pay on board with exact change.

**Fares (RTC residential routes):** $2 single ride, $3 for a 2-hour pass and $5 for a 24-hour pass. The Deuce and other Strip routes have a separate $4 single fare and $8 for 24 hours; RTC doesn't list the Deuce as an airport route. Confirm which fare applies to your route in the rideRTC app.

## Lounges at LAS by terminal

From the airport's official [airport services page](https://www.harryreidairport.com/airport-services) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/harry-reid-international), checked 19 Nov 2026. The airport lists lounges by gate group, not gate number. We list no hours: check with each operator.

### Terminal 1, C Gates
- **Chase Sapphire Lounge by The Club**

### D Gates (reached from both terminals by the airport's tram)
- **Capital One Lounge**
- **The Centurion Lounge**
- **Sidecar by The Centurion Lounge**
- **The Club at LAS**. The airport says you can buy a day pass at the reception desk.
- **United Club**

### Terminal 3, E Gates
- **The Club at LAS**

### How you get in
- **United Club:** membership, eligible tickets or elite status, under United's rules.
- **The Club at LAS:** open to all passengers with a day pass bought at reception, per the airport. Priority Pass lists both The Club LAS locations (Terminal 1 and Terminal 3).
- **Priority Pass:** on 19 Nov 2026 it listed The Club LAS (Terminal 1 and Terminal 3), plus The Chiroport and XpresSpa spa services. It did not list the Chase Sapphire Lounge.
- **Bank-branded lounges (Centurion, Sidecar, Capital One, Chase Sapphire):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
