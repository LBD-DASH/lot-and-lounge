## Official SEA parking rates

From the [Port of Seattle's parking page](https://www.portseattle.org/sea/parking/parking-information) and [ReserveSEA](https://reservesea.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/54653626606483-How-much-does-it-cost-to-park-in-the-garage), last checked 20 Nov 2026. The Port says all rates include taxes and fees.

| Lot | Rate | Maximum | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| General Parking (Levels 1–3 and 6–8, all rows; Level 5 rows A–M) | $8 per hour | **$37** per day, **$222** per week | Walk across the skybridges |
| Reserved Parking (Level 4, all rows; Level 5 rows N–W) | $47 per 24 hours; overstay $10 per hour up to the daily maximum | **$47** per day, no weekly rate | Walk across the skybridges |

**Reservations:** advance booking is required for the Reserved area. ReserveSEA says there's a minimum 2-day (25-hour) stay; for shorter trips it points you to General Parking. A reservation guarantees a space in the reserved area, not a specific level or stall.

**Good to know (per the Port of Seattle):**
- The garage height limit is 6'10". Taller vehicles use the Over-height lot (78 stalls, first come, first served) off S 182nd St and International Blvd, within walking distance of the terminal. It charges General Parking rates.
- 94 stalls have **free Level 2 EV charging** for parking customers: 64 on Level 7 (General Parking, rows E and F) and 30 on Level 4 (Reserved, rows E and F). They can't be reserved.
- For stays over 30 days, reserve online or call the Public Parking Office on (206) 787-4069, or the car may be treated as abandoned and towed.
- Motorcycles pay the same rates as cars.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the official rates.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| General Parking | **$222** (published weekly rate; 7 × $37 would be $259) |
| Reserved Parking | $329 (7 × $47) |

The takeaway: drive-up General Parking is $107 cheaper than Reserved Parking for a week.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- The Port of Seattle names **Doug Fox Parking** as a remote lot on Port property just north of the airport. Its own site says prices are quoted for your dates and vehicle size, so compare the final total against $222 a week in the garage.
- Many other private lots operate around SeaTac. Compare final totals, after taxes and fees, before booking.

## Public transport

From Sound Transit's airport and fares pages, checked 20 Nov 2026:

- **Link light rail (1 Line)** stops at **SeaTac/Airport Station**. From baggage claim, find carousel 16, go up one floor to the skybridge level, cross Skybridge 6 and turn left through the garage to the station. A free electric shuttle runs between the station and the skybridge.
- Trains leave every 8–10 minutes for most of the day, and the trip to downtown Seattle takes 38 minutes.
- **Fare:** **$3** one way for adults (19–64), $1 for seniors, riders with disabilities and ORCA LIFT, free for 18 and under. Pay with an ORCA card, contactless card, or a ticket from the station machine before boarding.
- For early or late flights, Sound Transit's Night Bus runs between the airport and downtown about every 30 minutes, starting about 15 minutes after the last train.

## Lounges at SEA by concourse

From the Port of Seattle's [Lounges at SEA page](https://www.portseattle.org/services-amenities/airport-lounges), [Alaska Airlines](https://www.alaskaair.com/content/airport-lounge/location-and-hours) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/seattle-tacoma-international), checked 20 Nov 2026. We list hours only where the operator publishes them; check with each lounge.

### Central Terminal
- **American Express Centurion Lounge**, mezzanine level above Evergreens, Pallino, Pei Wei and Lucky Louie Fish Shack
- **USO Center**, mezzanine above Checkpoint 4, for military travelers

### Concourse A
- **The Club SEA**, near Gate A12, 5 a.m. to midnight daily (run by the airport)
- **United Club**, near Gate A9
- **Delta Sky Club**, near Gate A1
- **Delta lounge near Gate A11** (the Port lists it as "Delta Sky Lounge")
- **Delta One Lounge**, near Gate A11

### Concourse C
- **Alaska Lounge**, mezzanine next to Gate C16, 5 a.m. to 7 p.m. daily per Alaska

### Concourse D
- **Alaska Lounge**, near Gate D1 (Alaska: just beyond Security Checkpoint 4), 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily per Alaska

### North Satellite (N gates)
- **Alaska Lounge**, mezzanine above Gates N13–18, 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily per Alaska

### South Satellite (S gates)
- **The Club SEA**, mezzanine above Gate S10, 6 a.m. to midnight daily (run by the airport)
- **British Airways Terraces Lounge**, near Gate S10

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (Alaska, Delta, United, British Airways):** eligible tickets, membership or status, under each airline's rules. Alaska says lounge passes are available at all three of its SEA lounges. Alaska lists holiday hours separately (the C Concourse lounge is closed on Thanksgiving Day), so check before you go.
- **The Club SEA:** day passes are sold through The Club SEA. ReserveSEA also lets you add Club SEA access to a Reserved Parking booking.
- **Priority Pass:** on 20 Nov 2026 it listed The Club SEA in Concourse A and The Club SEA in the South Satellite.
- **Bank-branded lounges (Centurion):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
