## Official MCO parking rates

From [MCO's official parking pages](https://flymco.com/parking-information/), last checked 19 Nov 2026. MCO's pages don't say whether tax is included. Every garage and lot below shows a per-24-hour price.

| Lot | Per 24 hours | Max stay | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| North Park Place Economy | **$14** ($5 for 11 min–3 h) | 90 days | Free 24-hour shuttle to Terminal A (Level 1, space A17) |
| South Park Place Economy | **$14** ($5 for 11 min–3 h) | 90 days | Free 24-hour shuttle to Terminal B (B66) and Terminal C (C286) |
| West Park Economy | **$14** ($5 for 11 min–3 h) | 90 days | Free 24-hour shuttle to Terminal A (A16) and Terminal C (C-285) |
| Surface Lot Discovery | $20 | 45 days | Walk to Terminal C through the train station; E-PASS or SunPass Plus only |
| Parking Garage A, Garage B, Terminal Top | $24 (first 20 min free) | 45 days | Walk to Terminals A and B; Terminal C is a 4-minute Terminal Link ride |
| Parking Garage C | $24 (first 20 min free) | 45 days | Walk to Terminal C; Terminals A and B by Terminal Link |
| Parking Garage C, reserved | $32 | 45 days | As above |
| Valet | $35 | 45 days | Departure curbs at Terminals A, B and C; open 6:00 a.m.–11:00 p.m. |

**Reservations:** only Parking Garage C. MCO says the economy lots need no reservation.

**Good to know (per MCO):** garages can't take vehicles over 7'0"; taller vehicles and those up to 25 feet long fit in South Park Place. Commercial vehicles aren't allowed. Economy lots are fenced and uncovered; the economy shuttles are wheelchair accessible. E-PASS and SunPass work in marked lanes in the garages. A vehicle with a Florida Disabled Veteran (DV) plate parks free in the garages and economy lots, but not in surface lots, valet or reserved spaces. MCO lists Surface Lot Atlantis separately; we leave it out because its availability changes.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic: 7 × the published per-24-hour rate.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy lots | **$98** (7 × $14) |
| Surface Lot Discovery | $140 (7 × $20) |
| Garages A, B, C and Terminal Top | $168 (7 × $24) |
| Garage C, reserved | $224 (7 × $32) |
| Valet | $245 (7 × $35) |

The takeaway: the economy lots save $70 against the garages for a week, and $147 against valet.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- Private off-airport lots near MCO sell daily parking with shuttles. Compare their final total, after taxes and fees, against the economy lots at $14 a day. MCO's parking pages name no authorized operators.
- MCO also lists cell phone waiting areas for drivers meeting arriving passengers.

## Public transport

From MCO's local bus page, checked 19 Nov 2026:

- **LYNX buses** pick up on the A side of the terminal, Ground Transportation Level 1, at spaces A38–A41.
- **Destinations** MCO lists include downtown Orlando, SeaWorld, Destination Parkway, Florida Mall, the Orange County Convention Center, Premium Outlets and the Sand Lake Road SunRail Station.
- **Fare:** **$2.00** for each destination, or **$4.50 all day**. LYNX customer service is on (407) 841-5969.

## Lounges at MCO by terminal

From MCO's official [lounge list](https://flymco.com/frequently-asked-questions/while-youre-waiting/) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/orlando-international), checked 19 Nov 2026. MCO lists lounges by gate range, not by gate number. We list no hours: check with each operator.

### Terminal A, Gates 1–29
- **The Club at MCO**. Priority Pass lists it as The Club MCO (Gates 1–29).

### Gates 30–59
- **American Airlines Admirals Club**
- **United Club**

### Gates 70–99
- **Delta Sky Club**
- **The Club at MCO**. Priority Pass lists it as The Club MCO (Gates 70–99).

### Terminal C, Gates C230–C245
- **Plaza Premium Lounge**, Gates C230–C245. Priority Pass lists it in Terminal C.

### Terminal A, Level 1
- **USO Welcome Center**, a military-only lounge

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (Admirals Club, Delta Sky Club, United Club):** membership, eligible tickets or elite status, under each airline's rules.
- **Priority Pass:** on 19 Nov 2026 it listed The Club MCO (Gates 1–29 and Gates 70–99) and the Plaza Premium Lounge (Terminal C), plus XpresSpa in Terminal B.
- **Terminal link:** Terminal C is a 4-minute Terminal Link ride from Terminals A and B, so check where your lounge is before you go through security.
