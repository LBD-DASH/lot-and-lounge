## Official DCA parking rates

From the airport's [Parking Information](https://www.flyreagan.com/parking-transportation/parking-information), [Economy Parking](https://www.flyreagan.com/parking-transportation/parking-information/economy-parking), [Parking 1](https://www.flyreagan.com/parking-transportation/parking-information/parking-1) and [Parking 2](https://www.flyreagan.com/parking-transportation/parking-information/parking-2) pages, last checked 25 Nov 2026. The airport is run by the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority. The pages don't say whether rates include tax.

**Economy Lot capacity is down about 40 percent** for construction of a new parking structure inside the lot. The airport strongly encourages reserving in advance and suggests Metrorail if you have no reservation. If a facility fills, reservations are still honored.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economy Lot (all airlines) | $7 per hour | $25 | Shuttle to all terminals every 10 minutes |
| Parking 1 (Terminal 1, Gates A1 to A9) | $7 per hour | $35 | Underground walkway to Terminal 1 |
| Parking 2 (Terminal 2, Gates B10 to E59) | $7 per hour | $35 | Covered walkways to Terminal 2 |

- **Parking 1** serves Air Canada, Frontier and Southwest. Enter on the west side of the airport. Only levels 4 and 5 are public. Height clearance is 7 ft.
- **Parking 2** serves American, Alaska, Delta, JetBlue and United. EV charging is first come, first served on the ground level. Height clearance is 8 ft 2 in.
- **Free for up to 60 minutes** in the terminal garages ([Park Free Up to 60 Minutes](https://www.flyreagan.com/parking-transportation/parking-information/park-free-60-minutes)).
- A free shuttle runs 24/7 between the terminals and all on-airport parking.
- **Reservations:** the airport says reserving gets the lowest rates and must be done at least 12 hours before arrival ([Parking Reservations](https://www.flyreagan.com/parking-transportation/parking-reservations)). No reserved price is quoted on the pages. Reserved customers scan a QR code to enter and exit.
- There is no minimum or maximum stay, but vehicles left more than 45 days must be registered with the parking office.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

This is our arithmetic from the drive-up daily maximums.

| Parking | Formula | 7-day total |
|---|---|---|
| Economy Lot | 7 × $25 | $175 |
| Parking 1 or Parking 2 | 7 × $35 | $245 |

**The takeaway:** Economy saves $70 over the terminal garages for a week, if you can get a space.

## Public transport

Sources: [DCA: Metrorail](https://www.flyreagan.com/metro), [WMATA fares](https://www.wmata.com/pay.html), [WMATA: Reagan National station](https://www.wmata.com/ridertools/station/ronald-reagan-washington-national-airport).

- **Metrorail Blue and Yellow lines** stop at the Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport station. It is elevated and linked to the Terminal 2 concourse level by two enclosed pedestrian bridges.
- **From Terminal 1:** take any "Airport Shuttle" bus to the Terminal 2 or Parking 1 & 2 stops, then use the bridge to the station.
- **Fares (WMATA):** $2.25 to $6.75 by distance on weekdays from 5 a.m. to 9:30 p.m. Weekday evenings and weekends are $2.25 to $2.50. Metrobus is $2.25.
- **Paying:** tap a contactless credit, debit or mobile wallet card at the faregates, or use a SmarTrip card from the machines at the station entrances. 1, 3 and 7-day passes need a SmarTrip card.
