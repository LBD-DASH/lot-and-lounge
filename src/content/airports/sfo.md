## Official SFO parking rates

From SFO's official parking pages ([Long-Term](https://www.flysfo.com/passengers/parking/long-term), [Domestic](https://www.flysfo.com/passengers/parking/domestic-hourly), [International](https://www.flysfo.com/passengers/parking/international-hourly), [ParkFAST](https://www.flysfo.com/passengers/parking/parkfast)), last checked 21 Nov 2026. SFO says every rate includes all taxes and fees, and that rates are subject to change.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-Term Parking (Garages 1 and 2, plus surface lot) | $4 per 30 minutes | **$27** | AirTrain Blue Line from Level 5 of the Long-Term Garage, 24 hours a day, as often as every 4 minutes |
| International Garage A or G | $4 per 30 minutes | **$37** | Walkways to the International Terminal; AirTrain to Terminals 1, 2 and 3 (about 10 minutes) |
| Domestic Garage | $4 per 30 minutes | **$41** | Short access tunnels to all terminals |
| ParkFAST (inside the Domestic Garage, booked only) | $4 per 30 minutes | **$70** (8-hour minimum stay) | Same as the Domestic Garage |

**Booking:** SFO says its lowest rates are only available through its [online booking system](https://parking.flysfo.com/), and bookings can be changed or cancelled up to 2 hours before the booked entry time. It doesn't publish those booked rates on its pages, so compare your quote against the drive-up maximums above.

**Valet:** SFO Valet runs from the entrance of the Grand Hyatt at SFO, which is on the AirTrain. It's prepaid online, with an EV charging option. SFO doesn't publish valet prices on its page; you see them when you book.

**Good to know (per SFO):**
- Height limits: Domestic Garage and ParkFAST 6'6"; Long-Term Garage 1 6'10"; Long-Term Garage 2 and International Garages A and G 8'2". Vehicles 8'2" and over can use the Long-Term surface lot.
- EV charging is free in marked "Green Vehicle Parking" stalls in all public garages, first come, first served (Level 1 and Level 2; bring your own cable for Level 1).
- **Remote Bag Check** at the Long-Term Garage (before the AirTrain station on Level 5) is complimentary, open 4:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. daily, for domestic flights on Alaska, American, Delta, Frontier, Hawaiian, JetBlue and United. Airline bag fees still apply.
- Long-Term Parking is at 794 South Airport Boulevard, reached from Highway 101 (San Bruno Avenue exit) or Highway 380.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from the official 24-hour maximums (7 × daily maximum). SFO publishes no weekly rate.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Long-Term Parking | **$189** (7 × $27) |
| International Garage A or G | $259 (7 × $37) |
| Domestic Garage | $287 (7 × $41) |
| ParkFAST | $490 (7 × $70) |

The takeaway: Long-Term Parking is $98 cheaper than the Domestic Garage for a week, and $70 cheaper than the International garages.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- SFO's own parking pages don't name authorized off-airport lots. Many private lots operate near the airport; compare final totals, after taxes and fees, against $189 a week in Long-Term Parking.
- Check SFO's online booking system too, since SFO says its lowest rates are only offered there.

## Public transport

From BART, checked 21 Nov 2026:

- **BART** stops at the **San Francisco International Airport (SFO) Station**, on Level 3 of the International Terminal. BART says it's a short walk to United in Terminal 3, and Terminals 1, 2 and 3 are one to three minutes away on AirTrain.
- **Fare:** BART's fare calculator gives **$11.80** one way (adult Clipper) from SFO to Powell St or Embarcadero in downtown San Francisco; $4.40 with a Senior/Disabled Clipper card and $5.90 with Youth Clipper or Clipper START.
- Check BART's [service alerts](https://www.bart.gov/schedules/advisories) before you travel.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** eligible tickets, membership or status, under each airline's rules. SFO says the United Polaris Lounge is for United Polaris business class passengers and outbound long-haul international first or business class passengers on a Star Alliance airline.
- **Priority Pass:** on 21 Nov 2026 it listed The Club SFO and the Alaska Lounge (additional fee applies) in Terminal 1, plus the Air India Maharajah Lounge, China Airlines Lounge, Golden Gate Lounge and Virgin Atlantic Clubhouse in International Terminal A. SFO's directory says The Club SFO takes Priority Pass and Lounge Key by online booking only, and that the China Airlines and Virgin Atlantic lounges admit Priority Pass members only during set hours. Priority Pass also lists restaurant offers at SFO.
- **Bank-branded lounges (Centurion):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
