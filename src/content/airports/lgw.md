## Official LGW parking rates

From London Gatwick's [roll-up car park prices](https://www.gatwickairport.com/parking/roll-up-car-park-prices.html), last checked 20 Nov 2026. These are prices for turning up without a booking. Gatwick says every car park is cheaper booked in advance. The page doesn't mention VAT.

| Car park | Roll-up rate | Each extra day | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Short Stay** (North and South Terminal) | £8.00 to 30 min, £14.00 to 1 h, £18.00 to 2 h, £21.50 to 3 h, £26.00 to 4 h, £31.50 to 5 h, £40.50 to 6 h, £60.00 to 12 h, **£70.00** to 24 h | £70.00 | Close to the terminal |
| **Premium Short Stay** (dedicated area in both Short Stay car parks) | £12.00 to 30 min up to **£87.50** for 12–24 h | £87.50 | As close as possible, same-level access |
| **Long Stay** | Free for 0–2 h; **£38.00** for 2–24 h | £32.00 | Gatwick doesn't give the transfer on this page |
| **Mid Stay** (Terminal Mid Stay North) | Pre-book only | – | – |
| **Valet** | Pre-book only (at least 24 h ahead) | – | – |

**Drop-off:** a minimum £10 charge applies in the drop-off zones outside the terminals. Gatwick recommends Short Stay for picking up, or Long Stay, where drop-off, pick-up and parking are free for up to two hours.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic from Gatwick's roll-up prices. Pre-booked prices vary by date, so get a quote on Gatwick's site and compare.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Long Stay, roll-up (£38 + 6 × £32) | **£230** |
| Short Stay, roll-up (7 × £70) | £490 |
| Premium Short Stay, roll-up (7 × £87.50) | £612.50 |

The takeaway: a week in Long Stay costs £260 less than Short Stay at roll-up prices, and pre-booking is usually cheaper again.

## Public transport

From Gatwick's train page, checked 20 Nov 2026:

- The **train station** is at the **South Terminal**, a five-minute walk from departures and arrivals. From the North Terminal, Gatwick's free shuttle takes two minutes.
- **Gatwick Express** runs non-stop to **London Victoria**, twice an hour, in about 30 minutes. Gatwick says you save 10% by booking online in advance with Gatwick Express.
- **Southern**, **Thameslink** and **Great Western Railway** also serve the airport. Gatwick says a train leaves on average every four minutes, with direct trains to over 120 stations and easy interchange to the Elizabeth line.
- Fares depend on operator, time and ticket type, so check the train operator before you travel.

## Lounges at LGW by terminal

From London Gatwick's [lounge page](https://www.gatwickairport.com/premium-services/lounge-airport.html) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-kingdom/gatwick), checked 20 Nov 2026. We don't list opening hours; check each operator before you go.

### North Terminal
- **No1 Lounge** (Gatwick: from £38 per adult when booked)
- **My Lounge** (from £34 per adult)
- **Plaza Premium Lounge** (from £40 per adult)
- **Plaza Express** (from £28 per adult)
- **Clubrooms** (from £44 per adult; waiter service and à la carte menu)

### South Terminal
- **No1 Lounge** (from £38 per adult)
- **My Lounge** (from £34 per adult; outdoor terrace in the South Terminal)
- **Club Aspire** (from £36 per adult)
- **Clubrooms** (from £44 per adult)

Airlines may run their own lounges for eligible passengers; check with your airline.

### How you get in
- **Independent lounges:** book a visit through Gatwick or the operator. The "from" prices above are Gatwick's published starting prices.
- **Priority Pass:** on 20 Nov 2026 it listed No1 Lounge, My Lounge, Clubrooms (additional fee) and Plaza Premium Lounge in the North Terminal, and No1 Lounge, My Lounge, Club Aspire Lounge and Clubrooms (additional fee) in the South Terminal.
- **Airline lounges:** eligible premium-cabin tickets or frequent-flyer status, under each airline's rules.
