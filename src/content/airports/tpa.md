## Official TPA parking rates
From [Tampa International Airport Parking](https://www.tampaairport.com/parking), last checked 25 Nov 2026. TPA says all three maximums include tax.

| Lot | Drive-up rate | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short Term Garage (Levels 4-9 above the Main Terminal) | First hour free, 1-2 hrs $8, each additional hr $8 | $32 (tax included) | Elevators to the Main Terminal |
| Long Term Garage (adjacent to the Main Terminal) | First hr free, 1-2 hrs $8, each additional hr $8 | $26 (tax included) | Moving walkway on Level 4 to the Main Terminal |
| Economy Parking (south of the Main Terminal) | First hr free, each additional hr $5 | $22 (tax included) | SkyConnect train; station via the elevators on Level 1 of the garage |

The maximum length of parking at any airport facility is 45 days. Tampa International Airport is proud to offer free electric vehicle (EV) charging stations at most of our airport parking facilities.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip
Our arithmetic from official rates (7 x daily maximum).

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy Parking | $154 (7 x $22) |
| Long Term Garage | $182 (7 x $26) |
| Short Term Garage | $224 (7 x $32) |

The takeaway: Choosing Economy Parking instead of the Short Term Garage saves you $70 over a 7-day trip.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives
- TPA says booking online saves on all parking (conditions apply). It doesn't name off-airport lots, so if you use a private lot, compare final totals including fees.

## Public transport
- TPA's pages we checked don't give transit fares, so check with the airport for public transport options.

## Lounges at TPA by terminal
From [TPA Layovers Guide](https://www.tampaairport.com/news/tips-dealing-long-layovers-tpa) and [American Express TPA Lounges](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card/TPA/), checked 25 Nov 2026.

TPA's four airsides (A, C, E and F) link to the Main Terminal by automated shuttle, and each has its own security checkpoint. TPA says its lounges have no showers.

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges:** American Airlines Admirals Club and Delta Sky Club are available to eligible passengers and day-pass holders, offering complimentary snacks, drinks, Wi-Fi, and comfortable seating.
- **Priority Pass:** TPA's pages don't list Priority Pass access, so check Priority Pass before you go.
- **Bank-branded lounges:** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
