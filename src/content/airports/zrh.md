## Official ZRH parking rates

From Zurich Airport's [parking for passengers](https://www.flughafen-zuerich.ch/en/passengers/practical/parking-and-transport/parking-for-passengers) and [hourly fees](https://www.flughafen-zuerich.ch/en/passengers/practical/parking-and-transport/parking-for-shopping-and-visitors) pages, last checked 20 Nov 2026. On-site fees are in **CHF including VAT**.

| Length of stay | P1 / P2 / P3 / P4 | P6 |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 1 hour | CHF 2 (15 min), 4 (30 min), 5 (45 min), 6 (1 h) | see the airport's page |
| 2 / 4 / 8 hours | CHF 10 / 19 / 36, then CHF 4 per extra hour | see the airport's page |
| 1 day maximum | CHF 56 | CHF 56 |
| 2 days | CHF 99 | CHF 87 |
| 4 days | CHF 160 | CHF 138 |
| 5 days | CHF 189 | CHF 157 |
| 7 days | **CHF 247** | **CHF 195** |
| Each additional day | CHF 29 | CHF 19 |
| 14 days | CHF 450 | CHF 328 |

Up to the fourth day, part-days are charged in smaller steps up to the daily maximum; from the fifth day, the daily maximum is charged as a flat rate.

**Where they are (per Zurich Airport):** P1 connects directly to Terminal 1, P2 to Terminal 2 and P3 to Check-in 3. P6 is a four-minute walk from the terminals. P1, P2, P3 and P6 can be used without a booking, subject to availability. **P3 XXL**, **P60** (outside, 15-minute walk) and **P65** (outside, free shuttle bus, 5–7 minutes) are online booking only.

**Good to know:**
- Online booking starts **from CHF 94 a week**, and the airport says online rates are often cheaper than on-site rates.
- If you leave more than three hours after your booked time, the extra time is charged every 12 hours: CHF 18 in P1/2/3, CHF 16 in P6, CHF 12 in P60/65.
- Most car parks have a 2 m height limit. Taller vehicles can book P60 or P65 online, use P38 for up to 24 hours, or use P4 up to 2.2 m.
- Motorbikes: CHF 2 for the first hour, CHF 1 per extra hour, CHF 16 a day, CHF 8 per extra day.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Zurich Airport publishes 7-day prices, so no arithmetic needed.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Online booking (airport's "from" price) | from CHF 94 |
| P6, drive-up | **CHF 195** |
| P1 / P2 / P3 / P4, drive-up | CHF 247 |

The takeaway: P6 saves CHF 52 on a week compared with the terminal car parks, and an online booking can cost less than half as much.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

- The airport's own outdoor car parks **P60** and **P65** (online only) are its budget options.
- Private car parks and valet services operate around the airport. Compare final totals, including any fees, before booking.

## Public transport

From Zürich Tourism and ZVV, checked 20 Nov 2026:

- **Trains** run from Zurich Airport to Zurich Main Station (Zürich HB) **every 5–10 minutes** between about 5 am and midnight, including S-Bahn lines **S2** and **S16** and regional trains, in about **10 minutes**.
- **Tram line 10** takes about 35 minutes to the Main Station.
- **Fare:** Zürich Tourism lists a single trip at **CHF 7** (CHF 3.50 for children). ZVV sets the fares; check its price finder before you travel, because fares are by zone.

## Lounges at ZRH

From Zurich Airport's [lounge page](https://www.flughafen-zuerich.ch/en/passengers/practical/services/services-for-travellers/lounges), [SWISS](https://www.swiss.com/ch/en/fly/airport/lounges/lounges-zurich) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/switzerland/zurich), checked 20 Nov 2026. Zurich has one main terminal building; the airport lists lounges by gate area. We list hours only where the operator publishes them.

### Gates E
- **SWISS First Lounge E**, 06:00–22:30 daily per SWISS (SWISS First, Lufthansa First Class and HON Circle)
- **SWISS Senator Lounge E**, 06:00–22:00 daily per SWISS (Miles & More Senators and Star Alliance Gold)
- **SWISS Business Lounge E**, 06:00–22:00 daily per SWISS (Business Class)
- **Emirates Lounge**
- **Prime Class Lounge** (Priority Pass lists it as Primeclass Lounge)

### Airside Center
- **marhaba Lounge** (Priority Pass lists it)
- **VIP Service**

### Other areas
- **Aspire Lounge**: the airport lists two locations; Priority Pass lists both.
- **SWISS Lounges**: SWISS says it runs nine lounges at Zurich, for departures, transfers and arrivals. See SWISS for the others' locations and access rules.

### How you get in
- **SWISS lounges:** eligible tickets or status, as listed above. SWISS also sells lounge access to SWISS Economy and Premium Economy passengers.
- **Independent lounges** (Aspire, marhaba, Prime Class): a paid visit booked with the operator, or a membership they accept.
- **Priority Pass:** on 20 Nov 2026 it listed the Marhaba Lounge, two Aspire Lounges and the Primeclass Lounge, all in the Main Terminal.
