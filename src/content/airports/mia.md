## Official MIA parking rates

From [MIA's official parking page](https://www.miami-airport.com/airport-parking.asp), last checked 19 Nov 2026. The page doesn't say whether tax is included, and it adds a $0.35 convenience fee for the Park & Ride payment.

| Lot | Rate | Daily maximum | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economy Park & Ride (1350 NW 45 Ave) | $2 per 20 min | **$12** + $0.35 fee per transaction | Shuttle bus, 24 hours a day |
| Garages (Dolphin, Flamingo, Ibis) | $2 per 20 min | **$25**, applies after 4 hours | Walk to the concourses |
| Valet (Dolphin and Flamingo, Departures level) | $25 for 0–3 h on the first day | **$39** | Closest parking at MIA |

**Which garage:** Dolphin serves Concourses D–E, for American, British Airways, JetBlue and Qatar. Flamingo serves Concourses E–J and Ibis serves J–H, for all other airlines. Ibis has EV charging.

**Reservations:** book a space in the Dolphin or Flamingo garages up to a year ahead; MIA emails a QR code for entry and exit. You can change or cancel up to 24 hours before, but the **$2.99 reservation fee is non-refundable**. The page doesn't list the reserved-space price, so check the booking page.

**Good to know (per MIA):** garage limit is 45 days, valet is 20 days. Vehicles over 7 feet can use the High Vehicle area next to the Dolphin Garage or the Economy Park & Ride. Park & Ride has about 350 spaces, with availability that varies, and takes payment through PayByPhone.

## Real total cost for a 7-day trip

Our arithmetic: 7 × the published daily maximum.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Economy Park & Ride | **$84** (7 × $12), plus the $0.35 fee |
| Garages | $175 (7 × $25) |
| Valet | $273 (7 × $39) |

The takeaway: Economy Park & Ride saves $91 against the garages for a week, and $189 against valet.

## Off-airport and park-and-ride alternatives

## Public transport

From Miami-Dade Transit's Metrorail page, checked 19 Nov 2026:

- **Metrorail** serves **Miami International Airport (MIA) Station**. On weekdays, take an Orange Line train northbound; trains run every 15 minutes.
- **Weekends:** the Orange Line only runs an Airport Shuttle train between Earlington Heights and the airport, so you transfer at Earlington Heights.
- **Hours:** Metrorail runs 5 a.m. to midnight, seven days a week.
- **Fare:** **$2.25**, or $1.10 with a discount. Bus-to-rail transfers are free. Fare gates take contactless payment, an EASY Card or Ticket, or the GO Miami-Dade Transit app, not cash.

## Lounges at MIA by concourse

From MIA's official [VIP clubs and lounges page](https://miami-airport.com/clubs-and-lounges.asp) and [Priority Pass](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/lounges/united-states-of-america/miami-international), checked 19 Nov 2026. We list no hours: check with each operator.

- **American Airlines Admirals Club**, above Gate D15 (third floor)
- **American Airlines Admirals Club**, across from Gate D30
- **American Airlines Flagship Lounge**, across from Gate D30

### Concourse E (Central Terminal)
- **Turkish Airlines Lounge**, Level 2, after security
- **British Airways Lounge**, Central Terminal E Satellite, Level 4
- **Military Hospitality Lounge**, 2nd level, before security. For US and allied military travelers.
- **Admirals Club**, 5th floor: MIA lists it as temporarily closed.

### Concourse F
- **Club America**, Level 3: MIA lists it as temporarily closed.

### Concourse H (South Terminal)
- **Delta Sky Club**, level 2, after security
- **Turkish Airlines Lounge**, Level 3, after security

### Concourse J (South Terminal)
- **LATAM VIP Lounge**, near Gate J6
- **Avianca and TAP Portugal Lounge**, across from Gate J6, after security

### How you get in
- **Airline lounges (Admirals Club, Flagship, Delta Sky Club, British Airways, LATAM, Avianca/TAP):** eligible tickets, membership or status, under each operator's rules. MIA says day passes are available at several of them, subject to capacity.
- **Priority Pass:** on 19 Nov 2026 it listed an AV/TAP VIP Lounge and a Turkish Airlines Lounge in the South Terminal (Concourses H and J), and a Turkish Airlines Lounge in the North Terminal (Concourses D and E), plus Corona Beach House and XpresSpa. MIA places its Concourse E Turkish lounge in the Central Terminal, so check the location before you go. MIA's own page also says Priority Pass members are accepted at Turkish Airlines Lounge (Concourse H) and Avianca/TAP.
- **Bank-branded lounges (Centurion):** access set by the issuer. We don't recommend cards.
