Getting into a Newark airport lounge without status is harder than at many big US hubs, mainly because Priority Pass lists no lounges at EWR. There are still two routes that don't need elite status or a premium ticket, and both depend on your airline and your terminal. Lounge details below are from the airport's official directory and Priority Pass, checked 22 Nov 2026, plus United's own one-time pass page.

## Which lounges are at Newark, by terminal?

| Terminal | Lounges (per the airport's directory) |
|---|---|
| **A** | American Airlines Admirals Club (near A8), Delta Sky Club (near A8), United Club (near A27; the directory also mentions a new one near A26) |
| **B** | Lounge & Co (near B51), Lufthansa Senator / Business Lounge (near B68), SAS Lounge (near B60) |
| **C** | United Club (near C74), United Club (near C123, 30,000 sq ft with shower suites), United Polaris Lounge (near C120) |

The airport's directory lists **no bank-branded lounges** at EWR.

## How to get into a Newark airport lounge without status

### Option 1: a United Club one-time pass

United sells a **one-time pass for $59 per person** on the United app, and sometimes at the club itself when there is extra space. Per United:

- You need a **same-day boarding pass** for a flight run by United, Star Alliance or another partner airline.
- You can use it only in the **3 hours before your departing flight**, or any time before a connecting flight.
- Entry depends on space, and club members go in before pass holders.
- Each pass is for one person and one visit. Passes bought on the app are valid for 12 months and can't be refunded.
- It does **not** get you into the United Polaris Lounge.

At Newark, that opens the United Clubs in Terminal A and Terminal C. If you are flying United from EWR, this is the most reliable paid route.

### Option 2: buy entry at the Lufthansa lounge

The airport says the **Lufthansa lounge in Terminal B sells access subject to availability**. It doesn't publish a price, so ask at the desk. This is the one to try if you are flying from Terminal B.

## Is Priority Pass worth it at Newark?

Not for lounges. On 22 Nov 2026, Priority Pass listed **no lounges at EWR**, only two other experiences: **Minute Suites** (sleep pods) in Terminal C and **Be Relax Spa** (Gate 7) in Terminal A. If Newark is your home airport, check what Priority Pass lists at the airports you fly to before you count on it. ## Lounges that do need a ticket or status

- **United Polaris Lounge (near C120):** the airport says it is for long-haul international business class passengers.
- **Lufthansa Senator Lounge:** the airport says Star Alliance Gold members are invited in.
- **Lounge & Co:** La Compagnie's business class lounge, which also serves other airlines from Gates B50 to B57. The airport says it is generally open three hours before scheduled departure.
- **Admirals Club, Delta Sky Club, SAS Lounge:** eligible tickets, membership or status, under each airline's rules. The airport says Delta Sky Club hours vary, so check with Delta.

## Tips before you go

- **Check your terminal first.** Terminal A isn't directly on the AirTrain: take the 5-minute shuttle bus or walk about 15 minutes along a covered pathway from the Terminal A station.
- **Hours change often.** The airport advises checking with each lounge operator before you go.
- **Buy the United pass before you queue,** on the app, since availability at the desk isn't guaranteed.

## The short answer

Without status, the realistic way into a Newark airport lounge is a $59 United Club one-time pass, if you are flying United, Star Alliance or a partner and the club has space. In Terminal B, ask at the Lufthansa lounge, which the airport says sells access subject to availability. Don't buy Priority Pass for EWR: it lists no lounges there.
