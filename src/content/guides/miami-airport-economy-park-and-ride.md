Miami airport economy park and ride vs garage parking is mostly a question of how long you are away. Both charge the same $2 per 20 minutes, but their daily caps are very different, so a week in the Park & Ride costs less than half the garage price. These are MIA's official rates, checked 19 Nov 2026. MIA's page doesn't say whether tax is included.

## Economy Park & Ride vs garage: the numbers

| Option | Rate | Daily maximum | 7 days (our arithmetic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Economy Park & Ride** (1350 NW 45 Ave) | $2 per 20 min | **$12** + $0.35 fee per transaction | **$84**, plus the fee |
| **Garages** (Dolphin, Flamingo, Ibis) | $2 per 20 min | $25, applies after 4 hours | $175 |
| **Valet** (Dolphin and Flamingo, Departures level) | $25 for 0 to 3 hours on the first day | $39 | $273 |

Over a week, Economy Park & Ride saves **$91** against the garages and **$189** against valet.

## How the garage 4-hour rule works

At $2 per 20 minutes, both the garages and the Park & Ride cost $6 an hour until they reach their caps. MIA says the garage daily maximum of $25 **applies after 4 hours**. In practice:

- For a quick pick-up or a short visit, the garage is just as cheap per hour as the Park & Ride, and you can walk to the concourses.
- For anything longer than about 2 hours, the Park & Ride's $12 cap starts to save you money.
- For a full day or more, the Park & Ride is less than half the garage price.

## What is Economy Park & Ride like?

- A **shuttle bus runs 24 hours a day** to the terminal.
- It has about **350 spaces**, and MIA says availability varies, so allow time for a backup plan at busy times.
- You pay through **PayByPhone**, which is where the $0.35 convenience fee per transaction comes in.
- Vehicles over 7 feet can park here, or in the High Vehicle area next to the Dolphin Garage.

## Which MIA garage is right for your airline?

If you do choose a garage, MIA matches them to concourses:

- **Dolphin:** Concourses D to E, for American, British Airways, JetBlue and Qatar.
- **Flamingo:** Concourses E to J, for all other airlines.
- **Ibis:** Concourses J to H, for all other airlines. Ibis has EV charging.

The garage limit is 45 days, and valet is 20 days.

## Should you reserve a garage space at MIA?

You can book a space in the Dolphin or Flamingo garages up to a year ahead, and MIA emails a QR code for entry and exit. You can change or cancel up to 24 hours before, but the **$2.99 reservation fee is non-refundable**. MIA's page doesn't list the reserved-space price, so compare the quote on the booking page with the $25 drive-up maximum before you pay.

## Is Metrorail cheaper than parking at MIA?

For one traveller, by a long way. Metrorail serves Miami International Airport (MIA) Station for **$2.25** a trip, or $1.10 with a discount. On weekdays, take an Orange Line train northbound, every 15 minutes. At weekends the Orange Line runs only an Airport Shuttle train between Earlington Heights and the airport, so you change there. Metrorail runs 5 a.m. to midnight, and the gates take contactless payment, an EASY Card or the GO Miami-Dade Transit app, not cash.

## The short answer

For a trip of a day or more, MIA's Economy Park & Ride wins: $12 a day plus a $0.35 fee, or $84 a week, against $175 in the garages. For a pick-up of an hour or two, the garage costs the same per hour and is closer. Check [the MIA page](../../airports/mia/) for the latest official rates before you drive.
