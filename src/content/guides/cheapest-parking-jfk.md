The cheapest parking at JFK is the airport's own Long-Term Lot, and it gets cheaper again if you book online. The garages next to the terminals cost close to double for the same week. Here is every official lot ranked by what a 7-day trip really costs, using the Port Authority's rates checked 19 Nov 2026.

## What is the cheapest official parking at JFK?

The **Long-Term Lot** wins at both price points. It is **$45 per 24 hours** if you just drive in, and **from $35 a day** if you pre-book at least 3 hours ahead on the official reservations site. You reach the terminals on the AirTrain, which is free between the terminals and the on-airport lots. One thing to check before you go: it has an **11'6" height limit**.

The terminal lots cost more for the convenience of walking to check-in:

- **Terminal 8 Red Lot:** $70 per 24 hours drive-up, from $56 a day pre-booked. JFK recommends it for Terminal 1.
- **Terminal 5 Yellow Lot:** $75 drive-up, from $59 pre-booked.
- **Terminal 4 Blue Lot:** $82 drive-up, from $66 pre-booked. JFK says availability is reduced during construction, and the height limit is 6'4".
- **Federal Circle Station Lot:** reservation only, from $45 a day.

## How much does a week of parking at JFK cost?

These are our totals from the official rates: 7 times the 24-hour maximum, or 7 times the lowest pre-book price. Pre-book prices are "as low as" figures, so your quote may be higher on busy dates.

| Lot | 7 days drive-up | 7 days pre-booked (lowest) |
|---|---|---|
| Long-Term Lot | $315 | **$245** |
| Federal Circle Station Lot | not available | $315 |
| Terminal 8 Red Lot | $490 | $392 |
| Terminal 5 Yellow Lot | $525 | $413 |
| Terminal 4 Blue Lot | $574 | $462 |

The gap is bigger than most people expect. A pre-booked week in the Long-Term Lot is **$329 less** than driving up to the Terminal 4 Blue Lot, and $70 less than driving up to the same Long-Term Lot.

## Is pre-booking JFK parking worth it?

At JFK, yes, in every lot that publishes both prices. Booking at least 3 hours ahead takes $10 a day off the Long-Term Lot and $14 to $16 a day off the terminal lots. Over a week that adds up to $70 to $112, before you even switch lots.

Two things to keep in mind:

- The pre-book rate is a starting price. Check the live quote for your dates and compare it with the drive-up maximum above.
- The Federal Circle Station Lot only takes reservations, and at "from $45 a day" it matches the Long-Term Lot's drive-up rate, so it rarely beats a pre-booked Long-Term space.

## When does the AirTrain beat driving to JFK?

The AirTrain costs **$8.75 one way** between the terminals and **Jamaica** or **Howard Beach**, where you connect to the subway, or the LIRR at Jamaica. Children under five ride free.

For one traveller, the AirTrain part of a round trip is $17.50, plus your subway or LIRR fare at each end. That is a long way below the $245 minimum for a pre-booked week, so for a solo trip of more than a day or two, the train usually wins if you live near a subway or LIRR station.

For a group, paying with OMNY contactless unlocks a group fare: $8.75 for the first rider plus $4.50 for each extra rider, up to 5 riders and $26.75 in total. Even a group of five pays the AirTrain part for much less than a week of parking, though each person still needs a subway or LIRR fare, and luggage plus a late-night return can tip the balance back to the car.

## Rules that can cost you

- **Over 30 days:** you must register the car with ABM Parking, or JFK may treat it as abandoned and tow it.
- **Accessible parking:** with valid ADA plates or a placard, the 24-hour maximum is $35, but not with pre-booked rates or E-ZPass payment.
- **Off-airport lots:** JFK says to use only on-airport parking or an authorized provider, and it names two. Compare their final total, with taxes and shuttle frequency, against $245 for the Long-Term Lot.

## The short answer

The cheapest parking at JFK is the Long-Term Lot, pre-booked at least 3 hours ahead: from $35 a day, or $245 for a week at the lowest published rate. Avoid driving up to the terminal garages for long trips, where a week runs $490 to $574. If you are travelling alone, price the AirTrain plus subway or LIRR first, and check [the JFK page](../../airports/jfk/) for the latest rates before you leave.
