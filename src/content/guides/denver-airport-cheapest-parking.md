The cheapest parking at Denver airport is the Pikes Peak shuttle lot, and the gap to the garages is big: $70 against $245 for a week. DEN is also one of the simpler airports to budget for, because its FAQ says its parking never adds taxes or fees to the published rates. These are DEN's official prices, checked 19 Nov 2026 using its fee estimator.

## Denver airport parking, cheapest first

DEN's estimator gave the same price for 6 hours and 24 hours, so each lot works as a flat daily rate.

| Lot | Per day | 7 days (our arithmetic) | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pikes Peak shuttle lot** | **$10** | **$70** | Free shuttle |
| West Economy / East Economy | $20 | $140 | Free shuttle |
| West Garage / East Garage | $35 | $245 | Walk to the terminal |
| Premium Reserve (Arrivals Level 4) | $45 | $315 | Walk; reservation required |

Pikes Peak saves **$175** against the garages for a week, and **$245** against Premium Reserve.

## Can you reserve parking at Denver airport?

Only Premium Reserve, and you must book at least 24 hours ahead. Every other lot is first come, first served, and DEN says you don't need a coupon or reservation. That means the cheapest lots are also the ones you can't guarantee, so on busy travel days it is worth knowing your second choice: Economy at $20 still costs $105 a week less than the garages.

## How the DEN shuttles work

Per DEN, the shuttles are free, run 24 hours a day, every 7 to 10 minutes, and pick up at the shelters in the Economy lots. On your return, the West and East Economy shuttles pick up at the DEN Transit Center outside the Westin. DEN notes that shuttle stops can change during construction, so check the signs on the day.

## Rules worth knowing before you park at DEN

- **Height:** maximum garage clearance is 8 feet, and DEN can't take oversized vehicles.
- **Long stays:** parking beyond 30 days needs advance approval.
- **Grace period:** 7 minutes in the Garage and Economy lots.
- **EV charging** is free to use on Level 1 of the garages and at 61st and Peña, but standard parking rates apply.
- **Security:** DEN warns of vehicle break-ins and catalytic converter theft, and says you park at your own risk.
- **Short-term lots:** DEN says they aren't intended for overnight stays.

## Is the train cheaper than parking at Denver airport?

For one person, often. The **A Line** runs from the DEN Transit Center to **Denver Union Station**, and RTD buses also serve the Transit Center. Any trip to or from DEN needs an Airport fare product: the standard **Airport Day Pass is $10**, per RTD. Discount fares for seniors 65 and over, people with disabilities, Medicare recipients and LiVE members include the airport at no extra charge, and youth 19 and under and active-duty US military ride free.

A solo traveller buying two standard Airport Day Passes pays $20, against $70 for a week at Pikes Peak. A couple pays $40, still well under the cheapest week of parking. For a group with luggage or a very early flight, the car can still make sense.

## Lounges at DEN: what parking money won't get you

If you were hoping to put the parking savings towards a lounge, note that on 19 Nov 2026 **Priority Pass listed no lounges at DEN**. It listed a restaurant voucher at Mercantile Dining and Provision and Golf DEN, both on Concourse A. The airline lounges (Admirals Club, Delta Sky Club and United Club) go by each airline's rules on tickets, membership and status. DEN also lists a free Rest and Recharge seating area on the Concourse A mezzanine.

## The short answer

The cheapest parking at Denver airport is the Pikes Peak shuttle lot at $10 a day, or $70 a week, with no taxes or fees added. It can't be reserved, so have Economy ($20) as your backup.
