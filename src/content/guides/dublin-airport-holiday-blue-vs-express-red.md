Choosing between Dublin Airport Holiday Blue vs Express Red is a trade between €35 a week and about 10 minutes on the shuttle. Both are the airport's own long-term car parks, both run shuttles to Terminals 1 and 2, and both beat the short-term car parks by a wide margin. These are Dublin Airport's flat rates without pre-booking, checked 20 Nov 2026. The pages don't mention VAT.

## Holiday Blue vs Express Red at a glance

| | Holiday Blue | Express Red |
|---|---|---|
| Drive-up rate | **€20.00 a day** | €25.00 a day |
| 7 days (our arithmetic) | **€140** | €175 |
| Shuttle frequency | Every 15 minutes | Every 10 minutes |
| Shuttle time to T1 and T2 | About 15 minutes | About 5 minutes |

Over a week, Express Red costs **€35 more** for a shuttle that runs more often and takes about 10 minutes less each way.

## Which should you choose?

**Pick Holiday Blue if** price matters most and you have a little time to spare. It is the cheapest drive-up parking at Dublin Airport.

**Pick Express Red if** you are travelling at a busy time, flying with children, or tend to cut it fine. A shuttle every 10 minutes means less waiting on the way in and on the way home.

For a short trip the difference is small: €5 a day. For a two-week holiday it grows to €70, by the same arithmetic.

## How do the short-term car parks compare?

The short-term car parks at T1 and T2 are a short walk to both terminals, but they are priced for short visits: **€4.00 for the first hour, €6.00 for each extra hour or part, up to €50.00 a day**. A week there is €350, so Holiday Blue saves **€210**.

There is also **Terminal 2 Surface**, 270 spaces about a 5-minute walk from departures. Dublin Airport recommends booking it and lists no drive-up rate.

## Is pre-booking cheaper at Dublin Airport?

Dublin Airport says online rates vary by dates, times and how far ahead you book, with no booking fee. It doesn't publish a fixed online price, so get a quote for your dates and compare it with the €140 and €175 drive-up weeks above.

Useful rules for pre-booked customers, per Dublin Airport:

- You can arrive up to 4 hours before your booked entry time.
- Your exit time is extended by 24 hours at no cost.
- If online parking shows sold out, a limited number of drive-up spaces may still be available, with prices at the entrance, but that isn't guaranteed at very busy times.

## Where the shuttles stop at the terminals

- **Terminal 1:** Zones 8 and 9 of the Bus Park, behind Blocks A and B of the T1 multi-storey.
- **Terminal 2:** Zone 21, opposite the ground-floor entrance to departures.

There are also 30 EV charging points across the car parks.

## Could the bus be cheaper than parking?

For one person, usually yes. Dublin Bus stops in the Transport Zones outside both terminals. Route 41 runs between Swords and Lower Abbey Street in the city centre around the clock, and Route 16 runs to Ballinteer about every 10 to 15 minutes. Dublin Bus lists a **€2.00** single with a Leap Card, and its airport page gives a **€2.60** cash fare, paid in coins.

All rates and Dublin Airport's lounges are on [our full Dublin Airport page](../../airports/dub/).

## The short answer

Holiday Blue is the cheapest drive-up car park at Dublin Airport at €20 a day, or €140 a week. Express Red costs €5 a day more for a faster, more frequent shuttle. Both save over €170 a week against the short-term car parks, and a pre-booked quote may be cheaper again, so check [the Dublin page](../../airports/dub/) before you go.
