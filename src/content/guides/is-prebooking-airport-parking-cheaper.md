Is it cheaper to prebook airport parking? The common advice says yes, and at many airports the savings are real. But at some big US airports, reserving a space costs more than turning up, because what you are buying is a guaranteed space rather than a discount. Here is what the official prices showed at 10 airports on our pages, checked between 19 and 22 Nov 2026.

## Where prebooking saves the most

These airports publish online prices that start well below their drive-up rates. Online prices are "from" figures, so your quote may be higher on busy dates.

| Airport | Prebooked, 7 days | Drive-up, 7 days | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Copenhagen](../../airports/cph/) | from 499 DKK (Budget P19, online only) | 1,625 DKK (Standard+) | over 1,100 DKK |
| [Johannesburg](../../airports/jnb/) | R504 (booked 5+ days ahead) | R2,340 (pre-booked area, no booking) | R1,836 |
| [Frankfurt](../../airports/fra/) | from €64 (Holiday Parking) | €258 (Terminal Parking cap) | €194 |
| [Zurich](../../airports/zrh/) | from CHF 94 | CHF 195 (P6) | CHF 101 |
| [Munich](../../airports/muc/) | from €59.99 | €107 (Economy, standard rate) | €47.01 |
| [New York JFK](../../airports/jfk/) | $245 (Long-Term Lot, lowest rate) | $315 (Long-Term Lot) | $70 |

Two of these come with conditions. Copenhagen's Budget car parks are **online only with a 4-day minimum**. At Johannesburg the big gap is for turning up at the pre-booked area without a booking. MSP2 long stay comes to the same R504 as pre-booking.

## Where prebooking costs more

**Seattle (SEA).** General Parking is $37 a day, with a published weekly rate of $222. Reserved Parking is $47 a day with no weekly rate, or $329 for a week. Reserving guarantees a space, but costs **$107 more** for the week. See [our Seattle page](../../airports/sea/).

**Orlando (MCO).** Only Parking Garage C takes reservations, at $32 per 24 hours. Driving up to the same garage costs $24, so a reserved week is $56 more, and the $14 economy lots need no booking at all. Our [Orlando parking guide](../cheapest-parking-orlando-airport/) covers which lot to use.

**Miami (MIA).** You can reserve a space in the Dolphin or Flamingo garages, but the $2.99 reservation fee is non-refundable, and MIA's page doesn't list the reserved price. The Economy Park & Ride at $12 a day needs no booking.

## Where you can't prebook the cheap lots

**Denver (DEN)** only takes reservations for Premium Reserve, the most expensive option at $45 a day, booked at least 24 hours ahead. Every other lot, including the $10 Pikes Peak shuttle lot, is first come, first served. Prebooking at DEN buys certainty, not savings. Read more in our [Denver parking guide](../denver-airport-cheapest-parking/).

## Why prebooked prices vary

Several airports say their online prices change with dates and demand. Frankfurt's price list says online deals depend on availability, and Zurich says online rates are often, not always, cheaper than on-site rates. So treat the "from" figure as a best case, and get a quote for your actual dates.

## A quick checklist before you book

1. **Find the drive-up rate** for the lot you would use, and work out a 7-day total.
2. **Get the live online quote** for the same dates and lot.
3. **Check what you're buying:** a discount, or just a guaranteed space?
4. **Read the conditions:** minimum stays, booking deadlines, non-refundable fees and cancellation rules.
5. **Compare lots, not just prices.** A cheaper drive-up economy lot can beat a prebooked garage.

## The short answer

Is it cheaper to prebook airport parking? At Copenhagen, Johannesburg, Frankfurt, Zurich, Munich and JFK, yes, often by a lot. At Seattle and Orlando, reserving costs more than driving up, and at Denver the cheapest lots can't be booked at all. Compare the live quote with the drive-up rate every time, using our airport pages, such as [Frankfurt](../../airports/fra/), for the official numbers.
