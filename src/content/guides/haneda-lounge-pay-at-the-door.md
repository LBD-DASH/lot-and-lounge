Most Haneda airport lounges outside the airline clubs let you pay at the door, which makes Tokyo Haneda one of the easiest big airports for a lounge on a budget. The domestic lounges cost about the same as a café stop, and the international ones are open around the clock. Prices and hours here are from Haneda Airport's official lounge pages, checked 20 Nov 2026. Haneda says the prices include tax.

## Haneda airport lounge pay-at-the-door prices

| Lounge | Adult | Ages 4 to 12 |
|---|---|---|
| POWER LOUNGE (domestic), FOREST LOUNGE, Lounge Us | **¥1,320** | ¥660 |
| SKY LOUNGE | ¥1,500 | ¥750 |
| TIAT LOUNGE, POWER LOUNGE PREMIUM | ¥4,400 | ¥2,200 |

**Children 0 to 3** get in free at all of them.

## Domestic flights: Terminals 1 and 2

The ¥1,320 lounges are all on the domestic side.

**Terminal 1**
- **POWER LOUNGE SOUTH**, 2F domestic gate area (south), 6:00 to 21:00
- **POWER LOUNGE NORTH**, 2F domestic gate area (north), 6:00 to 20:30
- **FOREST LOUNGE**, 2F North Satellite, in front of Gate 26, 6:00 to 19:00
- **POWER LOUNGE CENTRAL**, 1F domestic arrival lobby, 6:00 to 20:00

**Terminal 2**
- **Lounge Us**, 4F domestic gate area (north), 6:00 to 21:30. Opened 1 Oct 2026, for domestic departures only.
- **POWER LOUNGE NORTH**, 3F domestic gate area (north), 6:00 to 21:30, domestic departures only.
- **POWER LOUNGE CENTRAL**, 3F domestic departure lobby, 6:00 to 20:00, with paid shower rooms.

Haneda says the former Airport Lounge (South) in Terminal 2 closed on 30 Sep 2026, so ignore older guides that still list it.

## International flights: Terminals 2 and 3

The international lounges cost more, keep long hours, and all have a **3-hour maximum stay**.

- **POWER LOUNGE PREMIUM** (Terminal 2, 3F international gate area), 6:30 to 25:30, **¥4,400**.
- **TIAT LOUNGE** (Terminal 3, 4F international gate area), 24 hours, **¥4,400**.
- **SKY LOUNGE** (Terminal 3, 4F international gate area), 24 hours, **¥1,500**. Soft drinks are free; snacks and alcohol are paid.
- **SKY LOUNGE SOUTH** (Terminal 3, 3F international gate area), 24 hours.

For a short wait before an international flight from Terminal 3, SKY LOUNGE is the budget pick at ¥1,500, a third of the TIAT LOUNGE price.

## Does Priority Pass work at Haneda?

Partly. Haneda's lounge page lists Priority Pass at **SKY LOUNGE SOUTH**, and at **TIAT LOUNGE only from 1:00 to 5:00 AM**. Its FAQ also mentions Priority Pass at POWER LOUNGE PREMIUM. Haneda says Priority Pass users with children pay the adult rate for them. Confirm on Priority Pass before you rely on it.

## Why visitors usually pay at the door

Haneda calls the POWER, FOREST, SKY and TIAT lounges its card lounges. It notes that, with some exceptions, **cards issued overseas can't be used for free entry** there, so most international visitors should plan to pay the door price. We don't recommend cards.

## Airline lounges at Haneda

- **Terminal 1:** Japan Airlines (JAL)
- **Terminal 2:** All Nippon Airways (ANA), domestic and international
- **Terminal 3:** JAL, ANA, Cathay Pacific and Delta Air Lines

These go by each airline's rules on tickets and status. Terminal 3 also has The Centurion Lounge (AMEX), 4F international gate area, 8:00 to 22:00, with access set by the issuer.

## Tips for a budget lounge visit at Haneda

- **Domestic:** at ¥1,320, a domestic lounge costs less than a third of the international TIAT price, and the Terminal 2 POWER LOUNGE CENTRAL has paid showers.
- **International:** SKY LOUNGE (¥1,500) is the cheapest door price in Terminal 3; every international lounge has a 3-hour limit.
- **Very early or late flights:** the Terminal 3 lounges are open 24 hours.

Parking caps, train fares and every lounge by terminal are on [our full Haneda page](../../airports/hnd/).

## The short answer

You can pay at the door at most non-airline Haneda lounges: ¥1,320 on the domestic side, ¥1,500 at SKY LOUNGE and ¥4,400 at TIAT LOUNGE or POWER LOUNGE PREMIUM, all tax included. Visitors with overseas cards should expect to pay. See [the Haneda page](../../airports/hnd/) for hours and locations before you fly.
