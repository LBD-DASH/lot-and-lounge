You don't need a business class ticket to use a Changi airport lounge. Changi lists three pay-per-use lounges across its four terminals, all open around the clock, so even an economy passenger on a long layover can buy a shower, a seat and some quiet. These are the adult walk-in rates on Changi's own pages, checked 20 Nov 2026.

## Which Changi lounges can anyone pay to use?

| Lounge | Terminal and level | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| **Plaza Premium Lounge** | T1, Transit Level 3 | 24 hours |
| **Ambassador Transit Lounge** | T2 and T3, Transit Level 3 | 24 hours |
| **Blossom, SATS & Plaza Premium Lounge** | T4, Transit Level 2M | 24 hours |

All three are in the **transit area, after immigration**, so you need to have cleared immigration before you can walk in. Arriving passengers who have already left the transit area can't use them.

## How much does a Changi lounge cost without business class?

| Lounge | 3 hours | 5 hours | 10 hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plaza Premium (T1) | **S$55** | S$90 | S$178 |
| Ambassador Transit (T2, T3) | **S$55** | S$97 | S$195 |
| Blossom (T4) | **S$55** | S$97 | S$195 |

- **Children under 2** get in free.
- Plaza Premium and Blossom say **local taxes and service charges may be added**, so the final bill can be higher than the listed rate.
- For a short stop, all three cost the same. For a longer stay, Plaza Premium in T1 is the cheapest of the three on Changi's listed rates: S$7 less for 5 hours and S$17 less for 10 hours.

## Which pay-per-use lounge should you choose?

Start with **your departure terminal**. The lounges are in the transit area of their own terminals, so the lounge in the terminal you fly from is usually the practical pick:

- Flying from **T1**: Plaza Premium.
- Flying from **T2 or T3**: Ambassador Transit, which has a lounge in each.
- Flying from **T4**: Blossom.

## What about the other lounges at Changi?

Most of Changi's other lounges are run by or for airlines:

- **Airline lounges:** Singapore Airlines SilverKris and KrisFlyer Gold (T2 and T3), Qantas, British Airways, Emirates and Qatar Airways (T1), and Cathay Pacific (T4). Entry is by eligible ticket or frequent-flyer status, under each airline's rules.
- **SATS Premier and Marhaba lounges:** Changi says entry depends on criteria set by the airlines that use them.

Several airline lounges keep limited hours. For example, Changi lists the British Airways Lounge in T1 as open 15:00 to 23:00 daily, while the pay-per-use lounges never close. That makes the pay-per-use lounges the dependable option for very early or late flights.

## Tips for using a Changi lounge on a budget

- **Pick the shortest package that fits.** At S$55 for 3 hours, a short stop is the best value. Only buy 5 or 10 hours if your layover is long enough to use them.
- **Allow for extras.** Taxes and service charges may be added at two of the three lounges.
- **Check the terminal on your boarding pass,** since Changi's lounges are spread across T1 to T4.

You can use a Changi airport lounge without business class: Plaza Premium (T1), Ambassador Transit (T2 and T3) and Blossom (T4) sell walk-in access 24 hours a day from S$55 for 3 hours.
