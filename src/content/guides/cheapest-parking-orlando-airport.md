The cheapest parking at Orlando airport is one of MCO's three economy lots, and you don't need to book. Unusually for a big US airport, reserving a space at MCO costs more than turning up, so the usual "book ahead to save" advice works the wrong way here. These are MCO's official rates, checked 19 Nov 2026. MCO's pages don't say whether tax is included.

## What is the cheapest parking at Orlando airport?

All three economy lots cost **$14 per 24 hours**, or $5 for a stay of 11 minutes to 3 hours, with a 90-day maximum stay and a free 24-hour shuttle. They are fenced and uncovered, and the shuttles are wheelchair accessible.

| Option | Per 24 hours | 7 days (our arithmetic) |
|---|---|---|
| Economy lots (North Park Place, South Park Place, West Park) | **$14** | **$98** |
| Surface Lot Discovery | $20 | $140 |
| Garages A, B, C and Terminal Top | $24 (first 20 min free) | $168 |
| Garage C, reserved | $32 | $224 |
| Valet | $35 | $245 |

For a week, the economy lots save **$70** against the garages and **$147** against valet.

## Which MCO economy lot should I use for my terminal?

The price is the same, so pick the lot whose shuttle goes to your terminal. Per MCO:

- **North Park Place:** shuttle to **Terminal A** (Level 1, space A17).
- **South Park Place:** shuttle to **Terminal B** (B66) and **Terminal C** (C286).
- **West Park:** shuttle to **Terminal A** (A16) and **Terminal C** (C-285).

Check which terminal your airline uses before you set off, then drive to the matching lot.

## Should I reserve parking at MCO?

Only if a guaranteed garage space matters more to you than the price. **Parking Garage C** is the only option MCO lets you reserve, at **$32 per 24 hours**. Driving up to the same garage costs $24, so the reservation adds $8 a day, or $56 over a week. MCO says the economy lots need no reservation.

## Garages, Surface Lot Discovery and valet

- **Garages A, B and Terminal Top** are a walk to Terminals A and B. Terminal C is a 4-minute Terminal Link ride.
- **Garage C** is a walk to Terminal C, with Terminals A and B by Terminal Link.
- **Surface Lot Discovery** ($20) is a walk to Terminal C through the train station, and takes E-PASS or SunPass Plus only.
- **Valet** ($35) is at the departure curbs at Terminals A, B and C, open 6:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.

Garages, Discovery and valet have a 45-day maximum stay, against 90 days in the economy lots.

## Rules that can catch you out at MCO

- **Height:** garages can't take vehicles over 7'0". Taller vehicles, and those up to 25 feet long, fit in South Park Place.
- **Commercial vehicles** aren't allowed.
- **Disabled Veteran plates:** a vehicle with a Florida DV plate parks free in the garages and economy lots, but not in surface lots, valet or reserved spaces.
- **Off-airport lots:** private lots sell daily parking with shuttles. MCO's parking pages name no authorized operators, so compare the final total, after taxes and fees, with $14 a day.

## Can I take the bus to Orlando airport instead?

LYNX buses pick up on the A side of the terminal, Ground Transportation Level 1, at spaces A38 to A41. The fare is **$2.00** per destination or **$4.50 all day**, and MCO lists destinations including downtown Orlando, the Orange County Convention Center and the Sand Lake Road SunRail Station. For one person with light luggage, that is far cheaper than any lot.

## The short answer

The cheapest parking at Orlando airport is any of MCO's economy lots: $14 per 24 hours, $98 for a week, with a free shuttle and no booking needed. Pick the lot that serves your terminal, skip the $32 Garage C reservation unless you need a guaranteed garage space, and check [the MCO page](../../airports/mco/) for the latest rates.
