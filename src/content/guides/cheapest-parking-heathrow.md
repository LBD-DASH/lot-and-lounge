Looking for the cheapest parking at Heathrow? The answer depends on one decision: whether you will take a shuttle bus. Heathrow's Park & Ride costs less than half of Terminal Parking for a week if you just turn up, and Heathrow says booking ahead is cheaper again. These are Heathrow's on-the-day prices, checked 20 Nov 2026.

## Heathrow's two official car parks

Heathrow renamed its car parks on 29 September 2025. **Short Stay became Terminal Parking** and **Long Stay became Park & Ride**. The road signs still say Short Stay, so follow those if you are heading for the terminal car parks.

| Car park | First day (drive-up) | Each extra 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Park & Ride** | **£50.00** | **£40.00** | Free shuttle every 15 minutes, 04:00 to 23:30 |
| **Terminal Parking** (Terminals 2, 3 and 5) | £100.00 | £100.00 | Covered walkways to the terminal |

Heathrow's shuttle times from Park & Ride to the terminal doors are 10 to 20 minutes for Terminals 2 and 3, and 10 to 15 minutes for Terminal 4. The T2 and 3 car park is on Eastern Perimeter Road (TW6 2SB) and the T4 car park is on Scylla Road (TW6 3XL).

## How much is a week of parking at Heathrow?

Our arithmetic from Heathrow's drive-up prices:

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Park & Ride, drive-up (£50 + 6 × £40) | **£290** |
| Terminal Parking, drive-up (7 × £100) | £700 |
| T5 Fast Track/Overheight (7 × £115) | £805 |

Turning up at Terminal Parking for a week costs **£410 more** than Park & Ride. The T5 Fast Track/Overheight entrance is for vehicles over the 2.20 m height limit, and costs £115 a day.

## Is it cheaper to pre-book Heathrow parking?

Heathrow says pre-booking gets the best price for stays over 24 hours. It doesn't publish fixed pre-book prices, because they change with dates and demand, so the only way to know is to get a quote on Heathrow's site and compare it with the £290 and £700 drive-up weeks above.

Pre-booking rules, per Heathrow:

- Terminal Parking can be pre-booked for stays over nine hours, up to two hours before you arrive.
- The longest pre-bookable stay is 99 days. Drive-up has no limit.
- Pre-booked Park & Ride and Terminal Parking can be amended or cancelled free up to two hours before arrival.

## Is the train cheaper than parking at Heathrow?

Often, if you are travelling alone and live near a rail line. Heathrow's rail options, checked 20 Nov 2026:

- **London Underground:** Heathrow calls the Piccadilly line the most cost-effective rail route into London. It stops at all Heathrow terminals.
- **Elizabeth line:** journeys that start, end or pass through Zone 1 start from **£13.90** with contactless or Oyster, per Heathrow. Six trains an hour serve Terminals 2 and 3.
- **Heathrow Express** to London Paddington: **£26.00** for a Standard single, or **from £10.00** for an Advance Discounted Single booked 30 or more days ahead (non-refundable).

Even at the Heathrow Express Standard single price, a solo return is a fraction of a £290 Park & Ride week. For a family, each person pays a fare, so the car park can come out ahead.

## Picking someone up at Heathrow

Picking up on the terminal forecourts is prohibited, and Heathrow says unattended cars will be removed by the police. Park & Ride is free for under 30 minutes and £10.00 from 30 minutes to 1 hour 59 minutes. Terminal Parking costs £8.50 up to 29 minutes, £12.50 up to 44 minutes and £16.00 up to 59 minutes.

## The short answer

The cheapest official parking at Heathrow is Park & Ride: £290 for a drive-up week against £700 in Terminal Parking, with a free shuttle to the terminal. Get a pre-booked quote first, since Heathrow says that is the best price for stays over 24 hours. Travelling alone?
