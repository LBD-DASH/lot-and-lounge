Is Priority Pass worth it? For US travellers the honest answer is that it depends on two things: which airport you fly from, and which terminal. We checked what Priority Pass listed at 13 US airports on our pages between 19 and 22 Nov 2026, and the spread runs from no lounges at all to eight. This guide doesn't quote membership prices, which you should check on Priority Pass for the plan you are considering.

## Airports where Priority Pass is hard to justify

**Denver and Newark** had no Priority Pass lounges on our check dates. If either is your home airport, a membership only pays off through the airports you fly to.

**Los Angeles** listed one lounge, and it carries an extra fee. Our LAX page puts it plainly: don't count on a free Priority Pass lounge at LAX.

## Airports where it can work

At **JFK** and **SFO** the list is long, but it is concentrated in certain terminals: Terminals 1 and 4 at JFK, and Terminal 1 and International Terminal A at SFO. Priority Pass shows limited hours for some JFK lounges, such as the Terminal 4 Virgin Atlantic Clubhouse. SFO's directory says The Club SFO takes Priority Pass by online booking only, and that the China Airlines and Virgin Atlantic lounges admit Priority Pass members only during set hours.

At **Seattle, Las Vegas, Dallas Fort Worth, Orlando and Charlotte**, the lounges Priority Pass lists are run by The Club or Plaza Premium rather than by an airline, and our pages place them in main concourses or terminals. Check that yours is on the side of security you will be on.

## The terminal catch

Several airports list Priority Pass lounges only in one part of the airport:

- **O'Hare:** both lounges are in Terminal 5.
- **Atlanta:** both are in Concourse F.
- **Miami:** the lounges are in the South and North Terminals, and MIA places its Concourse E Turkish lounge in the Central Terminal, so check the location before you go.

If your gate is elsewhere, a lounge on the list may not be practical, or reachable, on the day.

## Questions to ask before you buy

1. **Does my home airport have a lounge on the list, in my usual terminal?**
2. **Are any of them "additional fee" lounges?** At LAX and SFO some are.
3. **Do the hours match my flights?** Some lounges limit Priority Pass entry to set hours.
4. **Will it get busy?** Some lounges admit members only when there is space, so have a backup at peak times.
5. **Could I just pay per visit?** Several of these lounges sell day passes. See our [budget lounge access guide](../lounge-access-on-a-budget/).

We don't recommend credit cards or financial products, including cards that bundle lounge memberships.

## The short answer

Is Priority Pass worth it at US airports? Only if the airports and terminals you actually use have lounges on its list. It listed none at Denver or Newark, one with an extra fee at LAX, and several at JFK, SFO and Miami. Check the Priority Pass page for your airport before you buy, and use our airport pages, like [JFK](../../airports/jfk/), to see every lounge by terminal.
