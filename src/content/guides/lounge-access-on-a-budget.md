Learning how to get lounge access on a budget mostly means ignoring memberships and looking for lounges that sell single visits. Plenty do. Every price below comes from the airport's or operator's own page, on the check dates in our airport pages (19 to 22 Nov 2026). We don't recommend credit cards.

## 1. Pay at the door: the cheapest lounge prices we found

| Airport | Lounge | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Tokyo Haneda](../../airports/hnd/) | POWER LOUNGE, FOREST LOUNGE, Lounge Us (domestic) | **¥1,320** per adult | Tax included; ages 4 to 12 ¥660 |
| Tokyo Haneda | SKY LOUNGE (Terminal 3, international) | ¥1,500 per adult | 24 hours, 3-hour maximum |
| [Copenhagen](../../airports/cph/) | Carlsberg Aviator Lounge | DKK 229 | Up to 3 hours, if there's space |

| Copenhagen | Aspire Lounge | DKK 339 per person | Walk-in |

| [Singapore Changi](../../airports/sin/) | Plaza Premium, Ambassador Transit, Blossom | From **S$55** for 3 hours | 24 hours; after immigration |

## 2. Book online when the lounge lets you

Dublin Airport shows how much booking ahead can save. It runs four lounges and sells visits online up to 2 hours before entry, or at reception:

- **The Phoenix Lounge (T1):** from €35 online, €45 at reception.
- **The Liffey Lounge:** from €37 online, €40 at reception.
- **The Martello Lounge:** from €39 online, €55 at reception.
- **The 51st & Green Lounge** (T2, after U.S. Preclearance): from €45 online, €55 at reception.

At the Martello Lounge, booking online saves €16 per person on the listed prices.

## 3. Buy a day pass at US airports

Several US airports have lounges that sell day passes to any passenger:

- **Las Vegas:** the airport says The Club at LAS is open to all passengers with a day pass bought at reception.
- **Seattle:** day passes are sold through The Club SEA, near Gate A12 and above Gate S10.
- **Atlanta:** ATL says The Club ATL sells day passes.
- **Chicago O'Hare:** the CDA says travellers without membership can buy day passes for most clubs, though airline policies change.
- **Miami:** MIA says day passes are available at several of its airline lounges, subject to capacity.
- **Newark:** United sells a $59 one-time pass for its United Clubs, valid with a same-day United, Star Alliance or partner boarding pass. The airport also says the Lufthansa lounge sells access subject to availability. See our [Newark lounge guide](../newark-lounge-without-status/).

Where an airport doesn't publish a day pass price, ask at the desk before you commit.

## 4. Match the lounge to your terminal

A cheap lounge is no use if it is in the wrong part of the airport:

- Changi's pay-per-use lounges are all in the **transit area**, so you need to have cleared immigration.
- Copenhagen's Eventyr Lounge is for **flights outside the Schengen area only**.
- Haneda's domestic lounges are in Terminals 1 and 2, and some Terminal 2 lounges are for domestic departures only.

## 5. Watch the time limits

Budget lounges often cap your stay. Haneda's international lounges have a 3-hour maximum, Copenhagen's Carlsberg Aviator price covers up to 3 hours, and a Dublin lounge visit is up to 2 hours. If you have a long layover, compare a longer package, such as Changi's 5-hour and 10-hour rates, against two separate visits.

## 6. Remember busy times

Many of these prices come with "if there's space" or "subject to availability". Copenhagen's Carlsberg and Eventyr pages say cardholder places are limited, and Dublin says access at the Liffey Lounge is subject to availability. At peak times, book online where you can, or have a backup plan.

## Is a membership ever cheaper?

Only if you visit lounges often, at airports where your membership is accepted. Our [Priority Pass by US airport guide](../priority-pass-us-airports/) shows how uneven that can be. For a once-a-year trip, paying per visit is usually the cheaper way in.

## The short answer

The cheapest way to get lounge access on a budget is to pay per visit at lounges that sell single entries: from ¥1,320 at Haneda, DKK 229 at Copenhagen, €35 online at Dublin or S$55 at Changi, plus day passes at several US airports. Book online where it is cheaper, check your terminal and time limits, and see our airport pages, like [Dublin](../../airports/dub/), for every lounge.
