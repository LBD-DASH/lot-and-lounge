Is it cheaper to park or take the train to the airport? For a solo traveller it almost always is cheaper to take the train, but the answer changes with every extra person in the car. Parking costs the same for one person or five, while each rail ticket is per head. Here are the real numbers for a 7-day trip at seven airports, using the official parking rates and rail fares on our airport pages, checked between 19 and 21 Nov 2026.

## Park or train: a 7-day trip compared

All parking totals are our arithmetic from the official tariffs. Rail fares are adult fares for one person, return.

## When does the train stop being cheaper?

Divide the week of parking by the return fare and you get the group size where the two costs meet. Our arithmetic:

- **Haneda:** ¥16,400 against ¥654 a head. Even a large family pays far less by train.
- **Taipei Taoyuan:** NT$3,430 against NT$320 a head. The train stays cheaper up to 10 people.
- **Hong Kong:** HK$1,494 against HK$215 a head. The train is cheaper for up to 6 adults, and children aged 3 to 11 pay half fare with Octopus or a QR code.
- **Narita:** ¥9,850 against ¥4,930 a head. One person pays about half by train; two people pay ¥9,860, about the same as parking. For three or more, driving to P-3 or P-5 is cheaper, and from day 6 each extra day costs only ¥1,000.
- **JFK and Heathrow:** the fares above cover only part of the trip at JFK, and are a starting price at Heathrow, so add your own connection. With a pre-booked week at $245 or a Park & Ride week at £290, the train is clearly cheaper for one or two people.

## Things the fare tables don't show

**Luggage and children.** A train fare is cheap, but getting a family and four suitcases across a city by rail is a different trip from a solo business run.

**Time of day.** Rail timetables matter for very early or late flights. At Munich, for example, the airport says the S8 runs 24/7, but only every 40 minutes between about 1 a.m. and 4 a.m.

**Peak parking.** At Haneda the airport asks travellers to take the train when it can, and the year-end peak (18 Dec 2026 to 3 Jan 2027) raises a week of parking to ¥19,800.

**Free extras on the parking side.** At Munich, the Economy Parking ticket covers the S-Bahn or bus to the terminal for everyone travelling with you, which makes driving to the airport and parking there easier to justify.

## How to work it out for your airport

1. **Find the cheapest official parking for your dates.** Use the 7-day totals on our airport pages, or a pre-book quote if the airport offers one.
2. **Price a return rail ticket per person,** including your connection from home to the airport line.
3. **Multiply by the number of adults,** and add child fares where they apply.
4. **Add the time and hassle,** especially with luggage or a pre-dawn flight.

## The short answer

Is it cheaper to park or take the train to the airport? For one person, the train wins at every airport here, from ¥654 at Haneda to HK$215 in Hong Kong. For families the maths can flip: at Narita two people already pay about the same either way, while at Haneda and Taipei the train stays cheaper even for a big group.
