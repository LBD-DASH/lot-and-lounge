Gatwick long stay vs short stay parking comes down to how long you are leaving the car. Short Stay is close to the terminal and priced by the hour, while Long Stay is built for trips and costs less than half as much over a week. These are Gatwick's roll-up prices, for turning up without a booking, checked 20 Nov 2026. The price page doesn't mention VAT.

## Gatwick Long Stay vs Short Stay: the prices

| Car park | First 24 hours | Each extra day | 7 days (our arithmetic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Long Stay** | £38.00 (free for 0 to 2 hours) | £32.00 | **£230** |
| **Short Stay** (North and South Terminal) | £70.00 | £70.00 | £490 |
| **Premium Short Stay** | £87.50 | £87.50 | £612.50 |

At roll-up prices, a week in Long Stay costs **£260 less** than Short Stay, and £382.50 less than Premium Short Stay.

## When is Short Stay worth it?

Short Stay is priced in steps through the day: £8.00 up to 30 minutes, £14.00 to 1 hour, £18.00 to 2 hours, £21.50 to 3 hours, £26.00 to 4 hours, £31.50 to 5 hours, £40.50 to 6 hours, £60.00 to 12 hours and £70.00 to 24 hours.

That makes it a good fit for:

- Meeting a flight, when you want to walk straight to arrivals.
- A same-day trip where being close to the terminal matters more than price.

**Premium Short Stay** is a dedicated area inside both Short Stay car parks, as close as possible with same-level access. It runs from £12.00 for 30 minutes up to £87.50 for 12 to 24 hours.

## When is Long Stay better?

For any trip of more than a day, Long Stay is the clear choice. It is £38.00 for anything from 2 to 24 hours, then £32.00 for each extra day. Gatwick's price page doesn't say how you get from Long Stay to the terminal, so allow extra time and check Gatwick's directions before you go.

Long Stay also has a useful free window: drop-off, pick-up and parking there are **free for up to two hours**. Gatwick recommends it, or Short Stay, for picking people up.

## Avoid the drop-off zone charge

The drop-off zones outside the terminals carry a **minimum £10 charge**. If someone is dropping you off, Long Stay's free first two hours can be the cheaper place to do it.

## Mid Stay and Valet are pre-book only

- **Mid Stay** (Terminal Mid Stay North) can only be pre-booked.
- **Valet** can only be pre-booked, at least 24 hours ahead.

Neither has a roll-up price, so you can only compare them with a live quote. Gatwick says every car park is cheaper booked in advance, which includes Long Stay and Short Stay too.

## Is the train cheaper than parking at Gatwick?

Gatwick's train station is at the South Terminal, a five-minute walk from departures and arrivals, and the free shuttle from the North Terminal takes two minutes. Gatwick Express runs non-stop to London Victoria twice an hour in about 30 minutes, and Gatwick says booking online in advance saves 10%. Southern, Thameslink and Great Western Railway also serve the airport.

Fares depend on the operator, time and ticket type, so check them for your journey and compare with £230 for a week in Long Stay. For one traveller near a direct line, the train often wins.

For Gatwick long stay vs short stay parking, use Long Stay for any trip: £230 for a roll-up week against £490 in Short Stay. Use Short Stay for quick visits, and remember Long Stay is free for up to two hours for pick-ups.
