The cheapest parking at LAX is not at the terminals, and for a solo traveller it is not even at the airport. The official choices run from a $3 a day Metro lot to a $60 a day garage, so the lot you pick can change the cost of a week by almost $400. These are the official rates from flylax.com, checked 19 Nov 2026. LAX says they include the 10% Los Angeles City Parking Occupancy Tax.

## Cheapest official parking at LAX, ranked

| Option | Max per 24 hours | Getting to the terminal |
|---|---|---|
| LAX Budget (5455 W. 111th St.) | **$20** | Free shuttle, 24 hours, every 20 to 25 minutes; uncovered |
| LAX Economy (6100 W. 94th St.) | $35 | Free shuttle, 24 hours |
| Central Terminal Area garages, General (P1 to P7) | $60 | Across from the terminals |
| CTA Premium (P1, P3, P7 Arrivals level) | $70 | Across from the terminals |
| CTA Valet | $75 (24-hour minimum) | Across from the terminals |

**LAX Budget** is $5 an hour up to its $20 cap, so it is also the cheapest airport lot for a stay of a few hours. **LAX Economy** costs more but is a covered garage, with its shuttle leaving from Level 1 to the pink LAX Shuttle zones on the Arrivals level.

## How much does a week of parking at LAX cost?

Our totals from the official rates: 7 times the daily maximum, plus transit fares where you need them. Transit fares are per person.

| Option | 7 days |
|---|---|
| Metro park-and-ride ($3/day) plus Metro fare both ways ($1.75 × 2) | **$24.50** |
| Van Nuys FlyAway parking ($6/day) plus FlyAway both ways ($12.75 × 2) | $67.50 |
| LAX Budget | $140 |
| LAX Economy | $245 |
| CTA garage, General | $420 |
| CTA Premium | $490 |

A solo traveller saves about **$395** over a week by using a Metro park-and-ride instead of a terminal garage. Even sticking to an airport lot, Budget saves $280 against the garages.

## Metro park-and-ride vs FlyAway vs LAX Budget

**Metro park-and-ride ($3 a day).** LA Metro lists $3 daily parking at Aviation/LAX and Hawthorne/Lennox on the C Line, and Crenshaw/Expo and Fairview Heights on the K Line. Norwalk is $3 a day or $60 a month. Ride to the **LAX/Metro Transit Center**, where a free shuttle from Bus Bays 1 and 2 runs to every terminal. The regular Metro fare is $1.75, with free transfers within two hours.

**Van Nuys FlyAway ($6 a day).** Parking is $1 an hour up to $6 a day, for FlyAway riders only and first come, first served, though reserved parking is also available. The bus runs nonstop to each LAX terminal for $12.75 one way. Up to two children under 5 ride free with each paying adult. The height limit is 8 feet.

**LAX Budget ($20 a day).** The simplest option if you are driving with family or lots of luggage, because there is no train or bus leg and no per-person fare.

## Which LAX parking is best for families?

Transit fares multiply with each adult, and parking does not. Using the fares above, each extra adult adds $3.50 to the Metro option and $25.50 to the FlyAway option for the round trip. The Metro park-and-ride stays far below LAX Budget even for a group of adults, while the FlyAway option passes Budget's $140 week once four adults are travelling ($67.50 + 3 × $25.50 = $144). Run the numbers for your group before you choose.

## Rules worth knowing before you park

- **Reserved parking** is sold separately. LAX says reserving can save up to 60% off drive-up rates, so compare a reserved quote with the drive-up caps above.
- **Over 30 days:** LAX says vehicles parked over 30 days may be impounded. You can request an extension from ABM.
- **Height limits** vary by garage, and Economy Level 1 is the tallest at 14'0". EV charging costs extra on top of parking.
- **Private lots** near LAX sell daily parking with shuttles. Compare their final price, including the 10% city parking tax, with the official $20 Budget lot.

## The short answer

The cheapest official way to park for LAX is a $3 a day Metro park-and-ride, about $24.50 for a solo week with fares. If you would rather park at the airport, LAX Budget at $20 a day ($140 a week) is the cheapest lot, and the terminal garages at $60 a day are the most expensive way to leave a car for a trip.
