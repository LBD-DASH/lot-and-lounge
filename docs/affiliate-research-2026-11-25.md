### Notes that do not fit in one cell

**Civitatis sources.** Commission tiers, the 30-day cookie, the €50 roll-forward, the €2 minimum transfer fee, euro payouts, gift vouchers, and accept-or-refuse-by-email are in the [Affiliate Program General Conditions](https://www.civitatis.com/en/affiliates/affiliate-program-general-conditions/) (updated as of January 2024), sections 1, 4.1, 4.2 and 16. The no-network rule, PayPal, the travel-website requirement, and the ban on bidding on the Civitatis brand are in the [affiliates FAQ](https://www.civitatis.com/en/affiliates/landing-frequently-asked-questions/). The landing page repeats the headline rates: [affiliates](https://www.civitatis.com/en/affiliates/). The Help Centre timing is [this article](https://civitatis.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/213280365-Does-Civitatis-have-an-affiliation-program-Can-I-join). South Africa appears as option value `ZA` in the country field of the registration form on the affiliates page.

**World Nomads payout conflict.** All three of these are current World Nomads pages:

- [Affiliates](https://www.worldnomads.com/partnerships/affiliates/): fees from quotes are paid every fortnight, in local currency by direct deposit or Payoneer.
- [FAQs](https://www.worldnomads.com/partnerships/affiliates/faqs): CJ pays on or about the 20th for the previous month's transactions, if the balance exceeds USD $100. Payoneer is how CJ pays local currency.
- [Onboarding guide](https://www.worldnomads.com/partnerships/affiliates/onboarding-guide): commissions lock by the 10th and payouts run between the 20th and the 28th. The bank account must be in the same country as the CJ profile. This page is also the source for the 60-day cookie and the 5-business-day review.

The tier names and the ban on a percentage of premium are in the [FAQs](https://www.worldnomads.com/partnerships/affiliates/faqs). The branded CJ URL above is the signup link in the onboarding-guide HTML.

**World Nomads, South Africa and insurance copy.** Eligibility categories are on the [affiliates page](https://www.worldnomads.com/partnerships/affiliates/). Online news, tour operators, tour guides, airlines, transport providers, wholesalers, travel agents, travel journalists and accommodation providers are listed as accepted. Travel-insurance buying guides, comparison sites, review sites, travel bloggers, content creators, podcasts and communities need an approval review. Coupon sites, non-travel sites, social-only influencers, YouTube or TikTok vloggers, and extreme-sport or self-guided expedition sites are not eligible. Lot & Lounge is a content and review site, so it falls in the review group.

The [content guidelines](https://www.worldnomads.com/partnerships/affiliates/content-guidelines) say an affiliate is not licensed or authorised to provide financial services. Allowed statements are that World Nomads provides travel insurance, that the publisher is an affiliate, and that a fee is paid for quotes, plus factual copy World Nomads supplies. The publisher may not recommend insurance, compare policies, present the cover as its own, or write its own insurance copy. The [FAQ](https://www.worldnomads.com/partnerships/affiliates/faqs) says a quote widget is not allowed, deep links are not allowed, and the only disclaimer is the one in CJ (the FAQ gives Link ID 15423653).

The [rest-of-world plan summary](https://www.worldnomads.com/row/travel-insurance) is written for residents other than the USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Europe, Brazil, China and the Philippines, and it says coverage may not be available for every country. South Africa is not in that excluded list. A quote was not submitted, so whether a South African resident can buy is **UNKNOWN**. No World Nomads page reviewed here states an extra South African licence rule for publishers. That legal question is **UNKNOWN**.

The old partner portal still describes a 10% sale commission, a 60-day cookie and a $250 threshold: [old FAQ](https://partner.worldnomads.com/partnerpublic/frequently-asked-questions). The current [FAQ](https://www.worldnomads.com/partnerships/affiliates/faqs) says that programme closed on 31 January 2023. Those old figures are not the live CJ programme.
